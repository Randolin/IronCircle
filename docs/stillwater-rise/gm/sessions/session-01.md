## Shape of session 1

1. **The handoff.** Casale gives the case away with relief ("The last guy who caught a case like this teaches traffic school in Yonkers now"). Cole's brief: "Homicide didn't want it, which is the first interesting thing about it. Second interesting thing's in the coroner's van. Go."

3. **The first door.** One Tier B contact the players choose. Klein's shop (the roll, and Toby) is the natural one; Grosz or Cutty are alternatives. End on a hook, not a reveal.
