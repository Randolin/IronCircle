# Decisions Log

Dated record of what's locked and why. Newest at the bottom. Reconstructed from three chat sessions (Aug 20–22, 2026) at migration; entries before 2026-09-03 are from memory and chat summaries — verify against the campaign doc once it's pasted in.

## 2026-08-20 — Foundations

- **Setting:** a fictionalized City, 1962, period noir. City of Mist as the base system. Mythological figures operate beneath a mundane surface.
- **System hybrid deferred.** A Daggerheart (Hope/Fear economy) + Fabula Ultima (progression) + City of Mist (Mythos/Logos) hybrid was discussed and deliberately set aside pending more thought. Stat blocks stay stubbed.
- **The lesson from Heliakros:** well-paced, but revealed rather than deduced. Everything here is designed so the players work it out.
- **Three-layer culprit structure:** Bunting (the hand) → Maas (the authorizer) → Weaver (the author). Each layer resolves separately: identity + method + mandate; authorized policy, not a glitch; intent, motive, and the voice on the wire.
- **Cast draws from multiple myth traditions**, not one. No predominantly Greek cast. No music-heavy theming beyond the Piper. Diversity and representation are active priorities — the Flats is a Black and Puerto Rican district.
- **Casting decisions:** Anansi recast twice, landing on Vivian "Viv" Weaver, young Chinese-American silk-fortune heiress and television tastemaker. Mouse King → Queen Ottilie Mauser, born in a Hoover camp. Gerald → Geraldine "Gerry" Fitch, motivated by being *seen*. Ray Ochoa → Raymond Sato, Nisei ex-detective. Vera Kestrel is Central American.

## 2026-08-21 — Design corrections

- **The Piper is present at the crime scene**, not absent.
- **Maas knowingly authorizes disappearances.** He is not innocent. Weaver still launders by nature (never his hands, never his name); hiding Vera's significance from Maas mattered because "I need this specific woman gone" creates a witness with leverage.
- **Kill schedule is an accelerating demolition-eve logic**, not a fixed calendar date. The 26th was too slow and too arbitrary.
- **Rimm arranged the tip that put the agency on the case**, through an untraceable Exchange line. Arranging witnesses isn't testifying. Payoff line: "Who do you think told your agency there was something worth seeing?"
- **Okafor had two separate fallings-out with Vera** (refused the families' stories six weeks out; recognized the tune days before) which witnesses conflate into one grudge.
- **Image prompts split into headshot and full-body**, style-free for ComfyUI. Headshots pinned with bust-crop and per-character collar lines; faces given explicit brows, noses, marks, and asymmetries after generations washed together.
- **Suno cues for every character**, Style-field only, instrumental, with a locked texture/era spine byte-identical across tracks. Suno cannot carry a melody between generations; motif quotations must be Covers.

## 2026-08-22 — Restructure

- **Diagnosis accepted:** most of Tier A were information-holders with overlapping payloads. Test applied: *if the players never meet this person, what happens?* Rimm, Okafor, Shale failed it.
- **Tiers resorted by relationship to the killings.** Tier A = suspect board (chain + suspects + next victims). Tier B = routes into Tier A. Tier C = texture. Vera gets her own section (she's the case).
- **Decoy layer dissolved into Tier A.** "Tier 1/2/3 decoy" labels → "draws fire early / mid / late." Herzog → Tier A. Feeny, Dubcek → Tier B.
- **Clocks added:** Rimm (editing tapes), Okafor (warning families out from Day 4), Shale (opens the letter Day 10), Toby (stencils Day 6, first issue Day 11).
- **Tier B distinguished by access cost**, not payload — Ottilie wants a name, Okafor a promise, Rimm the right question, Grosz payment, Halloran corroboration, Casale deniability, Ida her father stopped not destroyed.
- **Danger board created**, sorted by actor. Three kill logics: Bunting kills entries; Maas kills nobody (institutional threat only); Weaver kills records, water only when record and person are inseparable. Weaver decides, Bunting performs.
- **Herminia Vega added** — the scheduled next name. Knock Day 8, pier Day 9. Saving her moves the date; it doesn't erase it.
- **Bunting does not want Okafor dead** — being known is the closest thing to recognition he's had — and collects him anyway.
- **Motive for Vera made explicit** for every suspect. Milner's added (Vera's visit nine days out). Pruitt's stipend reports covered Vera for eight months — hold this back; it's what destroys him.
- **Renamed:** *The Rat Line* → **Stillwater Rise**. Triple meaning (Maas / Weaver / Bunting). No apostrophe.
