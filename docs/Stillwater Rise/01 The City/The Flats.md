# Carver Flats

> Low against the water. Everything the Authority wants is here.

**Carver Flats**, the Flats to everyone who lives there: a district of walk-ups and stoops on the river side of the City — a barbershop that remembers everything, a pawnshop that appraises everyone, a print shop with a mimeograph in the back. Black and Puerto Rican, working, indebted, and scheduled for clearance under the [[Renewal Authority]]'s Stillwater Rise plan.

## Locations
- **Klein Job Printing** — where *[[The Plain Truth]]* was run off every week.
- **Okafor's barbershop** — the district's memory.
- **Grosz Loans & Appraisal** — the pawnshop.
- **The Half Note** — the jazz basement two blocks from the pier.
- [[Tin City]] — the camp under the on-ramp, at the district's edge.
