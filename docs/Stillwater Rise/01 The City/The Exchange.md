# The Exchange

> Every call in the district goes through one room, and one man listens.

The telephone exchange serving the riverfront. Switchboard downstairs ([[Mabel Cho]]); the archive upstairs ([[Hal Rimm]]), racks of tape reels labeled in a tiny even hand, and a window with the unfinished [[The Span|Span]] dead-ending in the sky. The tip that put [[The Agency]] on the case was placed from an untraceable Exchange line.
