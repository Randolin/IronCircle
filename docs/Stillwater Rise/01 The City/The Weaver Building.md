# The Weaver Building

> Paper, radio, Channel 6, one roof.

Downtown. Paper on the low floors, the radio hour above, Channel 6 on top, and a rooftop garden whose threads are visible from the street.

## Rooms

- **Studio B.** Where the nickname happened: the televised unveiling of the Renewal model, thirty years ago, and an eight-year-old on a live microphone saying the true thing.
- **The clippings morgue.** Has Flats-shaped gaps. Its request log shows Vera's photo request, six weeks out. That log is how Weaver confirmed what Vera was building.
- **The city desk.** [[Walt Herzog]]. Where Shale's Day −14 slip went upstairs.
- **The afternoon program.** [[Vivian Weaver]]'s stage. Interviews feel like winning until the transcript is read back.

## Hooks
