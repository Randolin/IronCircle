# The Model Room

> That's not a model. That's a promise.

The [[Renewal Authority]]'s showpiece: a gilt scale model of the future City, the Flats already a golden plaza. [[Sterling Maas]] gives tours. The model runs ahead of reality; blocks are gold on the table before the wrecking ball reaches them. Things left overnight in the room come back with a faint sheen on them. Nobody has tested this on purpose.

## Rooms

- **The signing desk.** Where the docket dies. Tuesdays. [[Gerry Fitch|Fitch]] brings the stacks; eleven minutes; [[Lou Padgett|Padgett]] sits outside the door.
- **The contracts office.** Fitch's floor. The routing stamps live here.

## Hooks

- The Day 14 condemnation hearing is held under the model.
- A player who has seen the tongue word will understand the model differently.
