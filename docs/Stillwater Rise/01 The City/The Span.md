# The Stillwater Span

> It dead-ends in the sky.

The **Stillwater Span**, the unfinished expressway bridge above the riverfront, centerpiece of the [[Renewal Authority]]'s **Stillwater Rise** — the Span, the cleared [[The Flats|Flats]], and the plaza at its foot, sold as one thing. There's a model on the Commissioner's desk. He gives tours.
