# The Half Note

> Clean, pretty, nothing you'd remember.

A jazz bar at the river end of [[The Flats]], two blocks from [[Pier 9]]. Long bar, a stand the size of a kitchen table, a house trio that plays standards and lets people sit in. [[Amalia Reyes|Doc Reyes]] owns it and works the bar. On the block after next on the docket.

## Who's here

- [[Amalia Reyes|Doc Reyes]] — the bar, the kit, the bat.
- [[Jack Rivers]] — the end stool.
- [[Mickey Shale]] — sunglasses on, expense account still mysteriously working. The Day −14 slip happened here, to [[Walt Herzog|Herzog]], loud.
- [[Manny Bunting]] — sits in with the band on Thursdays. Never drinks. Won't take pay. Print night.
- [[Vera Kestrel]] drank one rye here after cranking and walked home by the pier. [[Ray Sato|Sato]] photographed her through the front window on Day −10; a two-tone suit is at the edge of the stand.

## Hooks

- The first Thursday the agency is in the room, Bunting counts them from the stand.
- [[Vivian Weaver|Weaver]]'s Day 5 invitation to Jack arrives here, by hand, in an envelope with no name on it.
