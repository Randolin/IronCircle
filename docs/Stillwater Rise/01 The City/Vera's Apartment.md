# Vera's Apartment

> Four rooms, and three of them are the paper.

A third-floor walk-up on a Carver Flats side street, six blocks from [[Klein Job Printing]] and about the same from [[Pier 9]]. She lived alone. She paid rent on time for eleven years and the landlord will say so, twice, before he says anything else.

## The four rooms

1. **The kitchen.** Oilcloth table, four chairs and only one of them ever used, a stove, a window onto the airshaft. One chair pulled out and turned toward the front-room window. A bottle with the cap off and one glass with a finger of rye in it.
2. **The front room — the Archive.** No sofa. Card files floor to ceiling, labeled by street; one card per family with name, address, date of the notice, date they left, and where they went. A corkboard with a district map and pins in three colors. A desk, a chair, a lamp. Her framed first issue by the door.
3. **The bedroom.** A bed pushed on its side against the wall to make room for four more cabinets. A wardrobe with three dresses in it. Her cardigan is not here; it's over the kitchen chair.
4. **The back room — the darkroom.** A converted box room with a blacked-out window: an enlarger, trays, a line of clips overhead, chemistry on a shelf. A cleared space in the dust the size of a shoebox where the camera lives, and the line is empty.

## What matters

- **One card drawer is empty**, the one labeled with the pier's street, clean on the runners. Emptied Day −3.
- **The camera and every negative are gone** with it, wherever the small new key on her ring fits.
- **The window in the front room looks at the clearance fence**, which is why the chair faces it.

## Who comes here

- **Day 1:** [[Sal Lupo|Lupo]]'s crew, tossing it. The Session 1 fight.
- **Later:** [[Ray Sato|Sato]] breaks in for his own reasons, and can be caught at it.
- The landlord, who wants to re-let by Friday and will start bagging her things.
