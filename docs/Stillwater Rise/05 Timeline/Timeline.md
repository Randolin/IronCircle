# Timeline

Days are relative to **Day 1**, the morning the body came up. **Day 1 is the 1st of the month**; Vera went in the water the night of the 31st/1st. This note is authoritative where a character's clock disagrees with it. The night itself is in [[The Case#What actually happened]].
