# Timeline

Days are relative to **Day 1**, the morning the body came up. **Day 1 is the 1st of the month**; Vera went in the water the night of the 31st/1st (Day 0). This note is authoritative where a character's clock disagrees with it. Reconciled 2026-09-05 with [[The Rat Line (draft-2)]].
