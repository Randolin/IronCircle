# The Plain Truth

> Printed weekly. Right eventually.

Vera Kestrel's mimeographed weekly, run off in the back of a print shop in [[The Flats]] and sold for a nickel by a paperboy. Its last eight months went into an issue about people who were relocated and never arrived anywhere. The issue never ran.
