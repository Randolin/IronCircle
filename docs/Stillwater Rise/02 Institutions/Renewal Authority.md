# Renewal Authority

> That's not a model. That's a promise.

The City's redevelopment authority, under the Commissioner. Its plan is **Stillwater Rise**: clear the riverfront, finish [[The Span]], build the future on the [[The Flats|Flats]]. Tenants are *relocated*. There's an entire program.
