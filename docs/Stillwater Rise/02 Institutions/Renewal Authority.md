# Renewal Authority

> That's not a model. That's a promise.

The City's redevelopment authority, under the Commissioner. Its plan is **Stillwater Rise**: clear the riverfront, finish [[The Span]], build the future on the [[The Flats|Flats]]. The tenant side of it is the **Stillwater Rise Resettlement** program: a letter, a check, a forwarding address. Tenants say *relocated*. The Authority says *resettled*. The billboard says *The Riverfront's Future, Today.*
