# Zu Cheng

> *"You are asking the wrong question, which is normal. I will answer the right one, which is generous."*

**Concept:** A Taoist out of [[Little China]] with a Pai Mei attitude — pompous, holier-than-thou, and usually right about it. Underneath, **Sun Wukong**, the Monkey King.

## Logos — Zu Cheng
Brawler. Human, with a Simian cast to him that nobody in Little China remarks on. Loreborne: temple-schooled.

- *Forensics* — he reads a body the way a priest reads an omen.
- *Pressure Points* — he knows where people come apart.
- Cards: **Dodge**, **Movement**.

## Mythos — Sun Wukong
Brawler again, with a **werewolf-style transformation template** under consideration. Knowledge is replaced by Strength when the Monkey is out: he stops reasoning and starts hitting. The fiery eyes see through disguise; the body doesn't fold.

- Cards: **Natural Armor**, **Body Bashing**.

## Look
Thirties, compact, moves like he's already decided where he's going. Shaved head under a flat cap when he's downhill, bare when he's not. A gray changshan over work trousers, or a suit that doesn't fit the shoulders. Long fingers, scarred knuckles, an unreasonably neat mustache.

## Background
A temple orphan raised in the Three Purities hall by **Master Fen Baiyun**, who taught him everything and made him promise never to say whose student he was. At twenty-six Fen swept him out the door with one sentence: *a monkey needs a mountain; go find one.* He went down the hill and found the coroner's office instead, where he washed bodies for four years and learned what they say. The coroner fired him for telling a detective he was wrong, in front of the widow, correctly.

He came to [[The Agency]] because Cole asked him what a body could tell her that a report couldn't, and let him answer for eleven minutes without interrupting. Little China is uphill from the river and not on any clearance map. The temple roof was paid for by old silk money in 1957. He has never asked why.

At nineteen he went into the river off the Little China ferry landing on a dare and did not come up for four minutes. Fen never asked what he saw. Cheng has never said: a bench, and somebody on it, and a mirror he was not allowed to look into.

## Drives
**Wants:** to be right where it costs something.
**Bond:** the temple. Whatever is owed on that roof, he'll find out before it's collected.
**Flaw:** the attitude. Everyone is beneath him until they aren't.
