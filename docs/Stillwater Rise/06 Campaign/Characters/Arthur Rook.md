# Arthur Rook

> *"Everybody's got a door they think is locked. Most of them just never tried the handle."*

**Concept:** A down-on-his-luck gumshoe who dodged the draft and works for [[The Agency]] because nobody else would have him. Underneath, **The Tower**: the card that reveals illusions and brings the wrong path down.

## Logos — Arthur Rook
Ranger, human-level skills only. A detective's eye and a draft-dodger's instinct for the exit. Carries a revolver he can fan when the moment calls for it (his Hope feature).

- *Detective* — he's worked cases; he reads a room and a lie.
- *Draft-Dodger* — he knows how to not be where the paperwork says he is.
- Cards: **Tracking**, **Dodging**.

## Mythos — The Tower
War wizard. The Tower reveals what's false and destroys or blocks the wrong paths. When it surfaces, illusions fail and doors do what he says.

- Cards: **Guiding Bolt**, **Fireball**, **Arcane Revelation**, **Locking and Opening Portals**.

## Look
Late thirties, tall and going thin the wrong way. A good overcoat from a better year, hat brim pulled low, shoes resoled twice. Hands that don't shake when he draws and do when he lights a cigarette.

## Background
*Padgett & Rook, Inquiries* was a two-man shop with one desk. It folded in 1959, the year his number came up for a war nobody had voted on and he found somebody who could type up a reason he wasn't going. The partner, **Lou Padgett**, stopped returning calls and now wears a windbreaker for the Renewal Authority. Rook worked skip-traces and divorce tails out of a furnished room for two years, and then a woman named Odessa Cole offered him a desk, no questions, one condition: he answers to her.

Three weeks ago a small woman in thick glasses came to the furnished room and offered him five dollars to find out who was following her. He took the five, did a day's work, decided it was a harmless tail, and never told her. He read her name in a precinct carbon on the morning of Day 1.

There was a night in 1957, a stairwell, a shot that should have taken him. He doesn't talk about it. He remembers a corridor with a light at the end and being told, by nobody, to go back.

## Drives
**Wants:** to be the one who was right, once, in front of somebody who counts.
**Bond:** he owes Lou four hundred dollars and an explanation; he'll pay the money first.
**Flaw:** what the Tower reveals it also breaks. He doesn't always check who's standing on the wrong path.

## Connections
- [[Zu Cheng]] — a job in Little China two years ago, a door that shouldn't have opened, a man on the other side of it who wasn't surprised. They know what each other are and have never discussed it.
- [[Jack Rivers]] — Rivers sold him information for years, and then sold him to Cole. Rook isn't sure which he's grateful for.
- **Lou Padgett** — the old partner. Site security at the Span.
