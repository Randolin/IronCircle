# Jack Rivers

*Jesse's character. Everything below is Jesse's; the GM adds nothing here without him.*

## Logos — Jack Rivers
Human Wordsmith Bard. Chain-smoking, dive-bar-frequenting, joker of a fella. Easy to get on with, but keeps everyone at a distance. Has worked everywhere on a little bit of everything. Definitely has a nose for trouble, but rarely ignores the warning. Currently he makes rent through freelance journalism (or illicit info gathering).

- **Experiences:** Streetwise, Keen Sense of Smell
- **Cards:** Inspirational Words, Enrapture (viewed as a captivating interaction / low-grade psi-ops, not a spell)

## Mythos — Coyote
Infernis Moon Witch. Coyote is a herald of the strange and supernatural. Patron of curiosity, wonder, whimsy, foolishness, the lure of curiosity and the consequences thereof. He is the dark inside the closet, the answer waiting around the bend in the path.

- **Experiences:** Supernatural Sense (like detect magic), Exploit Weakness
- **Cards:** Nature's Tongue, Voice of Dread, Siphon Essence (once per long rest), Towering Stalk (once per long rest), Healing Field (once per long rest)
