# Jack Rivers

> *"I'm not nosy. I'm curious. Nosy people get punched. Curious people get punched and write it down."*

**Concept:** A chain-smoking, dive-bar, joker of a fella. Easy to get on with, keeps everyone at a distance. Has worked everywhere on a little bit of everything and makes rent now through freelance journalism, or illicit info-gathering, depending who's asking. Underneath, **Coyote**.

## Logos — Jack Rivers
Wordsmith Bard. A nose for trouble, and he rarely ignores the warning.

- *Streetwise* — he knows the City from the bottom.
- *Keen Sense of Smell* — literal, and useful.
- Cards: **Inspirational Words**, **Enrapture** — played as a captivating conversation, low-grade psychological pressure, never a spell.

## Mythos — Coyote
Infernis Moon Witch. Coyote is a herald of the strange and supernatural: patron of curiosity, wonder, whimsy, foolishness, the lure of curiosity and the consequences thereof. He is the dark inside the closet, the answer waiting around the bend in the path.

- *Supernatural Sense* — detects the Mythos layer.
- *Exploit Weakness* — finds the crack.
- Cards: **Nature's Tongue**, **Voice of Dread**, **Siphon Essence** (1/long rest), **Towering Stalk** (1/long rest), **Healing Field** (1/long rest).

## Look
Forties, or a hard thirties. Lean, unshaven by policy, a grin that arrives before he does. Sport coat with a notebook in one pocket and a pint in the other, no hat, a cigarette he's always just lit. Smells like the last three places he was.

## Background
Merchant marine, short-order cook, process server, copy boy, bookie's runner, and for one bad winter a night watchman at a cannery. Strings for whoever pays: the morning paper when it's news, other people when it isn't. He knew Vera Kestrel the way everybody in the Flats did, and better than most: he sold her two tips for nothing because she was the only editor who'd ever asked him a follow-up question.

He drinks at the Half Note, at the end of the bar, where **Doc Reyes** cuts him off at three. Two weeks before Vera went in the water he was three stools down from a conversation he should have listened to. He was laughing at something.

He got Rook the agency job by telling Cole about him. He got himself the job by telling Cole about everyone else.

The cannery winter, he fell through the ice on the loading canal and was clinically dead for a minute and a half. He makes a joke about it. The joke is that somebody read his name off a list, frowned, and sent him back for being early.

## Drives
**Wants:** to see what's around the bend, and to have been the one who told you.
**Bond:** Doc Reyes. The one he doesn't keep at a distance, and they both pretend otherwise.
**Flaw:** curiosity, and its consequences. He'll open the door.
