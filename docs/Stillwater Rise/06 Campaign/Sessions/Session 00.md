## Two Sheets

The house rules are public, in [[Two Sheets]]. Short form: Logos sheet by default; surface to the Mythos sheet by crossing a box (3 per session), for free on Severe damage, last Stress, or GM offer, or for the scene with 6 Hope; Incarnate instead of dying, then take a Scar. Every surface costs the GM side 1 Fear or a clock tick. Log them below.
