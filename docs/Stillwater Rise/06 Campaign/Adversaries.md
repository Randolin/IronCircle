# Adversaries

Combat stat blocks for Stillwater Rise, built to the [[Daggerheart SRD]] (stat block anatomy p. 93–95; Tier 1 comparables: Swarm of Rats, Bladed Guard, Head Guard, Jagged Knife Kneebreaker, Merchant). Convention per [[System Notes#Adversaries]]: ordinary people are one Logos block; a potent Mythos side is one tier higher, or a second block the adversary swaps into.

**Swapping blocks.** An adversary with two blocks starts on the Logos block. It swaps by its *Surface* reaction. When it swaps, keep marked HP and Stress as counts; thresholds and everything else come from the new block. It does not swap back.

## The rats

### The Swarm
*Tier 1 Horde (4/HP)*
A carpet of river rats moving as one animal. They go where the sax man has been and they do not scatter.
**Motives & Tactics:** Carry off, cover him, climb, drown
**Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** 6/11 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** −2 | **Bite and Climb:** Melee | 1d8+3 phy
**Experience:** Nowhere They Can't Go +2

- **Horde (1d4+1) – Passive:** When the Swarm has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals 1d4+1 physical damage instead.
- **One Mind – Passive:** The Swarm can't be Surprised. When you spotlight one Swarm, you may spotlight every other Swarm within Far range as part of the same move.
- **Carry Off – Action:** Mark a Stress to target a creature within Very Close range. The target must succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll (13) or be moved to Close range in a direction of your choice and become Restrained until they succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll (13) on their turn.
- **Where He Is – Reaction:** When a creature attacks the Piper within Far range of the Swarm, spend a Fear to summon another Swarm at Close range of the attacker.

### The Tide
*Tier 2 Horde (6/HP)*
The Swarm when the Piper is cornered or playing. Use this block instead of the Swarm, not alongside it.
**Motives & Tactics:** Fill the room, pull them under, protect him
**Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 8 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +1 | **A Thousand Mouths:** Very Close | 2d8+3 phy
**Experience:** Nowhere They Can't Go +3

- **Horde (2d4+2) – Passive:** When the Tide has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals 2d4+2 physical damage instead.
- **One Mind – Passive:** As the Swarm.
- **Underfoot – Passive:** The area within Close range of the Tide is difficult terrain. A creature that ends its move there must mark a Stress.
- **Pull Under – Action:** Spend a Fear to target every creature within Very Close range. Each must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (14) or take 2d8+3 physical damage and become Restrained.
- **He Didn't Ask – Reaction:** When the Piper marks HP within Far range, the Tide clears 2 HP and must be spotlighted next.

## The Piper's friends

The only people who still call him Manny. Dockworkers, a few of them ex-fighters, all of them owed something by the same people he's owed by. They don't know what he does. They know he buys the round when he can. Two of them have started to change.

### Wharf Rat
*Tier 1 Bruiser (Logos)*
A big dock hand in a pea coat with a length of pipe, loyal to the sax man for reasons he'd have to think about.
**Motives & Tactics:** Block the door, get between, hit hard, stay close to Manny
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 7/14 | **HP:** 7 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** −2 | **Lead Pipe:** Melee | 1d10+4 phy
**Experience:** Docks +2, Strongarm +2

- **Haymaker – Action:** Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, deal 1d10+4 direct physical damage.
- **Get Between – Reaction:** When an ally within Very Close range would take damage, mark a Stress to take the damage instead.
- **Surface – Reaction:** When this adversary marks Severe damage, spend a Fear to swap to the Wharf Rat (Surfaced) block. Describe the change.

### Wharf Rat (Surfaced)
*Tier 2 Bruiser (Mythos)*
The pea coat splits at the shoulders. Teeth too long for the mouth. He is still trying to say Manny's name.
**Motives & Tactics:** Gnaw, hold on, drag toward the water, protect him
**Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 8 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** 0 | **Teeth and Claws:** Melee | 2d10+3 phy
**Experience:** Nowhere They Can't Go +2

- **Vermin's Vigor – Passive:** While any Swarm or Tide is within Close range, this adversary clears 1 HP at the start of each GM turn.
- **Gnaw Through – Action:** Make an attack against a target within Melee range. On a success, deal 2d10+3 physical damage and the target must mark an Armor Slot without gaining its benefit.
- **Hold On – Reaction:** When this adversary deals damage to a creature, mark a Stress to make that creature Restrained until they succeed on a Strength Reaction Roll (14).

### Big Augie Dorsey, the Rat King
*Tier 1 Leader (Logos)*
Ex-longshoreman, ex-club fighter, a face like a dropped pie. Runs the crew that drinks with Manny and has decided nobody gets to him without going through Augie. Named; if he earns a third scene, give him a People note.
**Motives & Tactics:** Command, cover Manny, hit first, take the blame
**Difficulty:** 13 | **Thresholds:** 7/14 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +2 | **Sawed-Off:** Close | 1d8+3 phy
**Experience:** Docks +2, Loyal +3

- **Rally the Boys – Action:** Spend a Fear to summon two Wharf Rats, who appear at Far range.
- **Hard Knocks – Reaction:** When an ally within Close range marks HP, mark a Stress to let that ally clear the HP; Augie marks it instead.
- **Surface – Reaction:** When Augie marks Severe damage, or when the Piper within Far range marks his last HP, spend a Fear to swap to the Rat King (Surfaced) block.

### The Rat King (Surfaced)
*Tier 2 Leader (Mythos)*
Augie, and the rats, and no clear line between them. Tails knotted through his coat. He speaks for all of them.
**Motives & Tactics:** Be everywhere, spend the swarm, keep the Piper standing
**Difficulty:** 15 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 8 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** +3 | **Crown of Tails:** Very Close | 2d8+4 phy
**Experience:** Nowhere They Can't Go +3, Loyal +3

- **Many Bodies – Passive:** While any Swarm or Tide is within Close range, the Rat King can't be Restrained and can move through creatures and closed doors.
- **All of Us – Action:** Spend a Fear. Every Swarm, Tide, and Wharf Rat within Far range makes its standard attack against a target within its range.
- **Tied Together – Reaction:** When the Rat King would mark HP, mark a Stress to have a Swarm or Tide within Very Close range mark it instead.

## Faction muscle

Ordinary people. One block each. Efficacy and style differ on purpose; the players should be able to tell who sent them by how they fight.

### Lupo Demolition Crew
*Tier 1 Standard*
Four men with crowbars and one with a cigar. They leave marks, and they want you to know it was them.
**Motives & Tactics:** Break it open, find the paperwork, make a point, get paid
**Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** 6/11 | **HP:** 5 | **Stress:** 2
**ATK:** 0 | **Crowbar:** Melee | 1d8+2 phy
**Experience:** Wrecking +2

- **Loudmouth – Passive:** This adversary can't become Hidden. Rolls to track or identify this crew afterward are made with advantage.
- **Pile On – Reaction:** When another Lupo Demolition Crew within Very Close range deals damage to a creature, mark a Stress to make a standard attack against the same creature. On a success, combine the damage.

### Lupo's Foreman (the cigar man)
*Tier 1 Standard*
Bigger, older, and the only one who was told why they're here. He's been asking about a sax player.
**Motives & Tactics:** Ask questions first, then the crowbar; report to Sal
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 7/13 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +1 | **Crowbar:** Melee | 1d8+3 phy
**Experience:** Sal's Business +3

- **Job Offer – Action:** Target a creature within Close range. The target must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (12) or mark a Stress as the Foreman makes Sal's offer sound reasonable. If they succeed, they learn what Sal is hunting for.
- **Pile On – Reaction:** As the crew.

### Authority Site Security
*Tier 1 Standard*
Windbreakers, flashlights, a badge on a lanyard. They think this is a trespass call.
**Motives & Tactics:** Check papers, clear the site, call it in, avoid a fight
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 5/9 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 2
**ATK:** +1 | **Nightstick:** Melee | 1d6+2 phy
**Experience:** Procedure +3

- **Papers – Passive:** Presence rolls to talk past this adversary are made at Difficulty 15 unless the PC shows a pass or badge.
- **Call It In – Action:** Mark a Stress to start a countdown (4) named *Precinct*, or advance an existing one by 1. When it completes, two Precinct Uniforms arrive at Far range.

### Precinct Uniform
*Tier 1 Standard*
Blue coat, whistle, no interest in being here.
**Motives & Tactics:** Arrest, pin down, protect the badge, make it through the shift
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 5/9 | **HP:** 5 | **Stress:** 2
**ATK:** +1 | **Nightstick or Service Revolver:** Melee or Close | 1d6+2 phy
**Experience:** Local Knowledge +3

- **The Badge – Passive:** When a PC deals damage to this adversary, start a countdown (4) named *Heat* if none exists, or advance it by 1. The GM decides what completing it means (a warrant, a name in the paper, the scene pass revoked).
- **Pin Down – Action:** Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On a success, deal 1d6+2 physical damage and the target is Restrained until they succeed on a Finesse or Strength Reaction Roll (12).

### Weaver's Feature Writer
*Tier 1 Social*
Nice coat, nicer smile, a notebook that never closes. Wants to know who the agency is.
**Motives & Tactics:** Flatter, get it on the record, find the story, own it
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 4/8 | **HP:** 3 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** −4 | **Notebook:** Melee | 1d4+1 phy
**Experience:** Everybody Talks +3

- **Off the Record – Action:** Target a creature within Close range. The target must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (13) or say one true thing they didn't mean to. The GM decides what.
- **Flattering Profile – Reaction:** When a PC succeeds on a Presence roll against this adversary, mark a Stress; the writer agrees enthusiastically and repeats it, and a countdown (3) named *In Print* starts or advances.

### Weaver's Fixer
*Tier 2 Skulk*
Hired, competent, and never in the same room twice. The most dangerous person the players will meet before the Piper, and they'll never get a good look.
**Motives & Tactics:** Buy it, burn it, discredit the holder; leave nothing; never be seen
**Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 9/17 | **HP:** 5 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +2 | **Sap:** Melee | 2d6+3 phy
**Experience:** Never There +3, Bought and Paid +2

- **Cleanup – Passive:** Instinct or Knowledge rolls to identify or track this adversary after a scene are made at Difficulty 17.
- **Vanish – Reaction:** After this adversary makes an attack, mark a Stress to become Hidden.
- **Someone Else's Problem – Action:** Spend a Fear to make a PC's ally NPC within the scene unavailable for the rest of the session (arrested, transferred, bought, frightened). The GM chooses one and says how.

### Milner's Collector
*Tier 1 Social*
A quiet man with a ledger and a blackjack he has never needed. Everything he says is in writing somewhere.
**Motives & Tactics:** Collect, cite the contract, never raise his voice
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 5/9 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 2
**ATK:** 0 | **Blackjack:** Melee | 1d6+1 phy
**Experience:** The Paper Says +3

- **Lien – Action:** Target a creature within Close range and name something they own or owe. The target must succeed on a Knowledge Reaction Roll (13) or mark a Stress and hand it over, or agree to.

### Tin City Crowd
*Tier 1 Minion*
Ottilie's people: coats, sticks, numbers. They don't want to fight. They will.
**Motives & Tactics:** Protect the camp, climb, count you
**Difficulty:** 10 | **Thresholds:** None | **HP:** 1 | **Stress:** 1
**ATK:** −2 | **Sticks and Bottles:** Melee | 2 phy
**Experience:** The Embankment +2

- **Minion (4) – Passive:** This adversary is defeated when they take any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to this adversary, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.
- **Counted – Passive:** While three or more Tin City Crowd are within Close range of each other, they can't be Surprised, and Ottilie knows where the PCs are.

## Building the Session 1 fight

**The print shop, Day 1:** four Lupo Demolition Crew and the Foreman. Per SRD battle points (p. 94–95) that's a Standard-heavy fight for three level-1 PCs, on the easy side, which is right for a first fight. Klein is not a combatant unless the Golem wakes; he isn't statted here on purpose.

**Tin City, if they end there:** two Swarms passing through, not attacking unless attacked. The goal is not to be carried to the water. Tin City Crowd climb; they don't fight the rats.

**Not yet:** the Piper himself. He negotiates; the Tide and the Rat King are his fight.
