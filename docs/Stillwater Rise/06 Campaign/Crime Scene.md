# Crime Scene

Pier 9, Day 1, just before full light. The GM's breakdown by zone: what's there, what's free, what needs a roll, what a bad roll costs. Read-alouds are in [[Session 01]]. The clue web is in [[The Case]]. This note is the one open on the table.

**Daggerheart terms.** Action rolls use the trait named; Difficulty 10 is easy, 15 average, 20 hard. Success with Fear gives the clue *and* a complication from the "on Fear" line. An Experience like *Detective* or *Forensics* adds when it plainly applies. Nothing on the pier requires a roll to find; rolls buy detail, speed, and privacy.

## The four oddities

These are why the Coroner referred it. Free to notice; nobody at the scene can explain them.

| Oddity | Where | What the NPCs say |
|---|---|---|
| **A ring of drowned rats** around where she was found, dozens, all facing in. Still in the water; the harbor crew won't touch them | The eddy | Dubcek: "Rats don't drown. These did. All at once." |
| **The hum.** Stand at the pile a minute and you're humming four descending notes without noticing | The pile | A uniform did it. The ME's assistant did it. Toby does it constantly. Say it out loud when a player lingers |
| **Upstream.** Recovered a quarter mile up-current of where she went in | The eddy | Dubcek: "The river has rules." |
| **The eyes.** Vera's were brown. The body's are a hazy river-green | The van | Halloran: "I have her license." She writes *lividity?* |

## Who is watching the scene

Pruitt (reports tonight; Fitch reads it by 9 a.m. Day 2). Teddy (sells to whoever pays). Pell (tells his route book). Toby (tells everyone). The crowd, which is the Flats, which is Okafor's chair by noon. The rats.

## Timing

If they work every zone, it's ninety minutes of table. The van and the water are the two that should take longest. Halloran throws them out; Dubcek runs out of line to coil. When Toby offers to show them where Miss Vera worked, that's the exit.
