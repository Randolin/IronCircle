# Stillwater Rise

> Printed weekly. Right eventually.
> — masthead of *The Plain Truth*

*A period-noir murder mystery. The City, 1962. City of Mist.*

## The case

At 5:50 on a grey morning, harbor patrol pulled a woman out of the grain-elevator eddy below [[Pier 9]]. Her clothes were found folded on the pier above — squared, neat, shoes side by side. The precinct logged it as a jumper before the body was dry.

Her name was **Vera Kestrel**. Most of the district knew her by another name, the one the papers used. She published a mimeographed weekly out of a print shop in [[The Flats]], and she had spent the last eight months working on an issue about people who had been *relocated* and never arrived anywhere.

Homicide didn't want it. That's the first interesting thing about it.

## The City, 1962

The river runs through it and the Flats sit low against the water — a district of walk-ups, stoops, a barbershop that remembers everything, a pawnshop that appraises everyone, and a camp of tin and coats under the on-ramp that the census says is empty. Above it all, the unfinished [[The Span|Span]] dead-ends in the sky, the centerpiece of **Stillwater Rise**: the [[Renewal Authority]]'s plan to clear the riverfront and build the future on it. There's a model on the Commissioner's desk. He gives tours.
