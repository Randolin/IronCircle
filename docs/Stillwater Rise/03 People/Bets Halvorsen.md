# Bets Halvorsen

> *The wagon.*

**Pitch:** Drives the coroner's van. Talks to the dead in the back and says they answer. Charon's daughter, more or less.
**Wants:** Every passenger delivered where they're going. And for somebody to finally ask about the nine that felt empty.
**Gives:** a glove-box notebook of every run for twenty years. Nine marked empty, two never filed. The players' first ally.

## Detail

- **Look:** Broad, weathered white woman, fifties, iron-gray braid under a county cap, a man's work jacket over the uniform, licorice in the breast pocket. Dockhand's hands.
- **Manner:** Talks over her shoulder to the empty van. Names every corner. Radio off. Won't let anyone ride in the back with a body: 'It's their seat.'
- **The notebook:** date, address, weather, one word. Nine marked *empty*. Two have no precinct file. The only list of the pattern nobody upstairs has edited.
- **Cost:** none the first time. After that she wants to be told where the passengers went, and she'll know if it's a lie.
- **Danger:** buyable in theory, not in practice, so she moves toward the water faster than expected once the notebook is known.

**Voice:**
- [the pier, Day 1]: "She's quiet. They're usually loud the first hour. This one's just... already gone somewhere. You want to know how many times I've said that? Nine. I write it down."
- [the joke that isn't]: "I don't hear voices. I hear passengers. There's a difference and I'm not going to explain it to a man holding a clipboard."
- [alliance]: "You want the nine? Glove box. Dates and weather. Don't lose it, that's twenty years of people."
