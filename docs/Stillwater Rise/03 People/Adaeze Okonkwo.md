# Adaeze Okonkwo

> *The record.*

**Pitch:** Inquest stenographer. What she types is the record. Has been told to strike testimony four times and remembers all four word for word.
**Wants:** The record to be true.
**Gives:** the struck passages, to Blum only. The finale runs through her hands. Danger: the inquest gets rescheduled away from her.

## Detail

- **Look:** Tall, composed Nigerian woman, fifties, wide-brimmed navy church hat, matching suit with a brooch, glasses on a chain. Posture like a pillar.
- **Manner:** Hands never leave the machine while anyone speaks. Corrects nobody out loud. Looks at the ceiling when remembering something struck. Doesn't share the tea.
- **The short inquests:** 'Twelve minutes. Nine. Eleven. A jumper inquest takes forty if anyone asks a question. Nobody asked.'
- **The finale:** 'Say the name again. Slower. I want it spelled right on the first page and the last.'
- **Danger:** Weaver has the inquest rescheduled into a room where she isn't typing. Brogan can do it with a call.

**Voice:**
- [the job]: "I type what is said. If the Coroner strikes it, I strike it. What I remember is my own business."
- [asked about the short inquests]: "Twelve minutes. Nine minutes. Eleven. A jumper inquest takes forty if anyone asks a question. Nobody asked a question. I noticed. I'm paid to type, not to notice, so I typed."
- [the finale, if it comes to her]: "Say the name again. Slower. I want it spelled right on the first page and the last."
