# Teddy Ferrante

> *The negatives.*

**Concept:** Freelance photographer. Shoots crime scenes for the Coroner on weekdays and weddings on Saturdays, and is broke on both. Twenty-five, fast, cocky, honest by accident: he shoots everything because he can't tell yet what matters. His scene photos are the only record of the pier that nobody upstairs has edited, and he keeps every negative in a shoebox under his bed.

**Motivation (specific):** Get out of the county's per-frame rate and into a newspaper's staff job. The Weaver morning paper is the only one hiring. Immediate: he shot Pier 9 at 6:40 on Day 1 before the precinct's own man arrived, and he thinks there's something in the frames he can sell.

**Performable mannerisms:** Talks while shooting, never stops moving. Calls everyone "chief." Smells of fixer. Keeps a cigarette behind his ear he never lights on a scene. Counts frames out loud.

**Voice:**
- [the pier]: "Twenty-two frames, chief. Clothes, the pile from four sides, the edge, the water, the crowd. Always shoot the crowd. Nobody looks at the crowd."
- [the pitch]: "Weaver's people pay ten a print. County pays a dollar. You do the math, I did."
- [the turn]: "They wanted the crowd frames. Only the crowd frames. Why does a newspaper want the crowd and not the body, chief? Chief?"

**Visual description:** Wiry Italian-American kid, twenty-five, dark curly hair under a snap-brim, a Speed Graphic on a strap, a flash bag on the other shoulder, a jacket with fixer burns on the cuffs. Grin first, thinks later.

**Use:** A race. His twenty-two frames of the pier include the peppermint wrapper before the precinct photographer stepped on it, the crowd with [[Nathaniel Pell|Pell]] and [[Roy Pruitt|Pruitt]] in it, and, at the edge of frame nineteen, a lean man in a two-tone suit on the far side of the cordon tape, watching. Teddy doesn't know what he has. Weaver's paper offers him ten dollars a print for the crowd frames on Day 3. If the players haven't asked him for the negatives by then, they buy them back at Weaver's price or not at all.

**Cost:** cash, or a byline. He'll take a byline in *[[The Plain Truth]]* if Toby's restarted it, and be embarrassed how much he wanted it.
