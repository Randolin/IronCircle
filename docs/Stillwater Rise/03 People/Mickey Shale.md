# Mickey Shale

> *The Judas.*

**Pitch:** Washed-up columnist who gave Vera her mocking nickname, then secretly helped her. He let slip about her list in a bar two weeks before she died.
**Wants:** To never open the letter she left him.
**Gives:** the tip chain, in his own voice, if someone's with him when he opens it. Clock: Day 10, alone or not.

## Detail

- **Look:** White man, mid-forties, handsome gone soft. Sharp clothes a decade out of date, no hat, sunglasses indoors. The letter's corner always showing in the breast pocket.
- **Manner:** Quotes his own old columns bitterly. Buys rounds on a Weaver expense account that still works. Pats the pocket. Funny like a man at his own wake.
- **Day −14:** told Herzog about Vera's list at the Half Note, loud. Doc Reyes and Jack were there.
- **Day 10:** opens the letter. Alone, it finishes him. With someone holding him up, it's the confession that puts Herzog and the route upward on the record.

**Voice:**
- [deflecting]: "Roan coined it. I just — remodeled. Youngest column in the City and the first smart thing I did with it was a little girl's nickname. That's the business, kid. Buy me another."
- [the meetings]: "She let me help. You understand what that — she *let* me. Fifteen years and she just... put me to work like the debt was payable."
- [the letter, drunk]: "It's addressed in her hand. Whatever's in it, I earned it. That's exactly why it stays shut."
