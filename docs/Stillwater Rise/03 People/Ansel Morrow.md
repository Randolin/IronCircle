# Ansel Morrow

> *The only man who's been in the room.*

**Pitch:** The Coroner's chief clerk. The only person who has ever been in a room with him. Delivers the referrals by hand.
**Wants:** The office to run exactly as it always has.
**Gives:** 'Tall.' Later, maybe, 1931. Don't decide what he is.

## Detail

**Concept:** Chief clerk to the [[County Coroner]]. Sixties, bald, galoshes indoors in every weather. He carries the referrals to the agency by hand, stands while [[Odessa Cole|Cole]] reads them, and leaves. He is the only living person who has been in a room with [[The Judge|the Coroner]], and he has never described it.

**Motivation (specific):** Keep the office running exactly as it has always run. He has done this job for thirty-one years and the job has not changed, and he thinks that's the point of it. He is not curious about the Coroner. He was, once. He doesn't say what happened to that.

**Performable mannerisms:** Never sits. Holds his hat in both hands. Answers questions with the fewest possible words and then waits, in case there's another one. Wipes his galoshes on the mat on the way *out*.

**Voice:**
- [delivery]: "Referral. Coroner's stamp. Sign here that you got it."
- [asked about the Coroner]: "Tall."
- [asked again, later, if the players have earned it]: "He asked me once if I'd stay. I said I would. That was 1931. I don't think it was a question."

**Visual description:** Thin white man in his sixties, bald with a fringe of white, long-faced, a gray suit that was good once and is clean now, black rubber galoshes over his shoes. A leather document case worn to suede at the corners.

**Use:** He is how the referral gets from the Coroner to the agency, and how the Coroner is felt without being seen. If the players follow him after a delivery, he walks to the county building, goes in, and does not come out that day. Do not decide what he is. He might be the Judge's one mortal friend, or something that took the job in 1931 and never left.
