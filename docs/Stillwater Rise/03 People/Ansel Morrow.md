# Ansel Morrow

> *The only man who's been in the room.*

**Pitch:** The Coroner's chief clerk. The only person who has ever been in a room with him. Delivers the referrals by hand.
**Wants:** The office to run exactly as it always has.
**Gives:** 'Tall.' Later, maybe, 1931. Don't decide what he is.

## Detail

- **Look:** Thin white man, sixties, bald with a white fringe, a gray suit that was good once, black rubber galoshes indoors. A leather document case worn to suede.
- **Manner:** Never sits. Holds his hat in both hands. Fewest possible words, then waits. Wipes his galoshes on the way out.
- **If followed** after a delivery: walks into the county building and doesn't come out that day.
- **Later, if earned:** 'He asked me once if I'd stay. That was 1931. I don't think it was a question.'

**Voice:**
- [delivery]: "Referral. Coroner's stamp. Sign here that you got it."
- [asked about the Coroner]: "Tall."
- [asked again, later, if the players have earned it]: "He asked me once if I'd stay. I said I would. That was 1931. I don't think it was a question."
