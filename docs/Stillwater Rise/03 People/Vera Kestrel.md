# Vera Kestrel

> *The victim.*

**Concept:** The child who said the emperor was naked, grown old inside the joke. Counter-media crank, posthumous protagonist.

**Motivation (in life):** Finish the disappeared-persons issue of *The Plain Truth* and force one official body — any one — to read a name aloud on the record. Not justice in the abstract: an acknowledgment, in a building with a flag in it, that these people existed.

**Performable presence (she's dead — she appears in artifacts and testimony):** Margins full of "confirmed. obviously." A filing system no one else can operate that turns out to be flawless. The nickname everyone uses before catching themselves. People imitate her voice when quoting her, without noticing they're doing it.

**Voice (from the archive, letters, the roll):**
- [zine, masthead motto]: "Printed weekly. Right eventually."
- [marginalia on an official denial]: "Third denial. They number them now. So do I."
- [to August, on the roll]: "You're the only one in this district who listens with his eyes. Everyone else has it backwards."
- [the last line]: "There's no other side. It's just water."

**Visual description:** Central American woman, late thirties, small and wiry, light brown skin. Thick round glasses that magnify her eyes; dark hair pinned up for work regardless of the day. In play (not in the base prompts): ink-stained fingers, a pencil behind the ear, the cardigan pocket stretched by a notebook. Dressed always one notch too formal for wherever she is — hearing clothes.
