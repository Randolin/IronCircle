# Esther Blum

> *Counsel.*

**Pitch:** The agency's lawyer. Builds the case and the courtroom. Hammurabi: the law is written down and she can recite it back, including the clause you hoped she'd miss.
**Wants:** The machinery of law used honestly.
**Gives:** what you can legally do and how to stand next to what you can't. Knows who the Coroner is. Has never said.

## Detail

- **Look:** Compact white woman, late fifties, silver-streaked dark curls, reading glasses pushed up into them and forgotten. Dark suit, plain white collar, no ornament.
- **Manner:** Answers by asking the question you should have asked. Cites case names like gossip. Cuts every meeting off one beat before it goes soft.
- **Mythos:** Hammurabi. Any statute or contract, she can recite back, including the clause you hoped she'd miss. When someone lies about a document, she quotes the document.
- **Why it's personal:** her family's building went under a slum-clearance order in another city in another decade.
- **Upstairs:** read the Coroner's appointment paper in 1953 and understood. Built the retainer so the county could cut it and the Coroner couldn't be touched. Has never told anyone.

**Voice:**
- [advice]: "You can't subpoena a pawn ledger. You can, however, be standing beside it when someone opens it. Stand better."
- [the inquest]: "An inquest is a play with a script nobody admits exists. We can't rewrite it. We can recast it. Get me your coroner with her spine located."
- [the statutes]: "Condemnation law has exactly one honest sentence in it, and they put it in Latin so nobody would read it. I read it."
