One note per character. The tiers sort by **relationship to the killings**, not importance — see [[Reference#The tiers]]. The [[Danger Board]] says who's at risk; the [[Timeline]] says when.

| Who | Motive | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| [[Sal Lupo]] | She photographed one of his illegal evictions. That's prison, not embarrassment — and his men were seen casing her block. | Strong, criminal |

| [[Greta Milner]] | Nine days before her death, Vera asked one question Milner could not answer — about the payment chain into the subsidiary Milner had flagged in her own margin hand. Publishing it makes Milner a witness against something that erases people. | Strong |
| [[Preston Day]] | The transmitter tower is going on cleared Flats land, and he called on air, twice, for her to be dealt with. Recorded, and Rimm has it. | Moderate, and loud |
| [[Gerry Fitch]] | Her initials are on the routing stamps. The disappeared-persons issue surfaces them under her name. | Strong, understated |
| [[Roy Pruitt]] | Not a motive. His stipend reports covered Vera for the eight months before her death — which is how they knew where she would be that night. | Worse than motive |

The Pruitt line is the one to hold back. He does not know what his reports were for, has never asked, and finding out is what actually destroys him — not the accusation, not the envelope, but the arithmetic.

### What each one costs

The currency, not the conversation. This is the table's real map.

- **Ottilie** wants a name — the Piper's — and treats the players' word as a debt drawn against her camp.
- **Okafor** wants a promise, named out loud, that they'll do better with the song than he did.
- **Rimm** wants the right question. The wrong one burns the visit and possibly the tape.
- **Grosz** wants payment, at his price, until someone says out loud what is happening in his shop.
- **Halloran** wants corroboration sufficient to make honesty survivable.
- **Cutty** wants to be written down while he talks, and Ottilie's blessing before he starts.
- **Casale** wants deniability and future favors; his price falls as the players prove serious.
- **Ida** wants her father stopped, not destroyed, and withdraws everything if that line is crossed.
- **Prosper** wants absolution he cannot accept and will not ask for.
- **Klein** wants nothing. He wants the names spelled right.
- **Mabel** wants a reason to have repeated it to two people instead of one.
- **Dubcek** and **Feeny** want cover — a way to say it that doesn't end the job.
- **Padgett** wants Rook to say the draft thing out loud, then four hundred dollars, then the gate. His price gets specific, never lower.
- **Fen** wants Cheng on his knees in the hall. Then he tells them everything.
- **Doc Reyes** wants the Half Note left out of it. One straight answer for Jack; after that, everybody's price.
