# Nathaniel Pell

> *The Adjuster.*

**Pitch:** Insurance agent who denied eleven 'suicide' claims and knew from the folded clothes they weren't. Vera confronted him about it in public.
**Wants:** To not be found out by the families who still trust him.
**Gives:** eleven claim files with dates and addresses. The pattern without needing Halloran.

## Detail

- **Look:** Black man, fifties, full gray beard, a notch out of one ear, careful gray suit and fedora, a shiny leather route book. Polishes his glasses when cornered.
- **Manner:** Turns up wherever death does: the pier, the funeral, the stoops. Flinches at official questions.
- **Looks guilty because:** files on every victim, and a public fight with Vera three weeks before she died.
- **Actually guilty of:** knowing silence, paid for. The folding told him years ago.
- **After he deflates:** eleven files that prove dates, addresses, and the clearance correlation without Halloran. The families still trust him.
