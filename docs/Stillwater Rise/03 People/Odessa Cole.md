# Odessa Cole

> *Operations.*

**Pitch:** Runs the agency. Former Army nurse. Assignments, deadlines, cover. No Mythos, on purpose; she's the normal one who keeps the office looking normal.
**Wants:** To not bury a third case that deserved daylight.
**Gives:** one more day, one more favor. Suspects she isn't really in charge and has chosen not to check.

## Detail

- **Look:** Black woman, late forties, square-shouldered, hair pressed and pinned back, a silk scarf at the throat of a tailored jacket. Small mole at the corner of her mouth.
- **Manner:** Answers the phone mid-first-ring. Writes in a shorthand only she reads. States the time cost of everything. Has already thought of what you're about to say.
- **Buried two cases** the agency wouldn't have survived. Has decided not to bury a third. The players never hear this; they just keep getting one more day.
- **Upstairs:** nine years, never met the Coroner, met the clerk. Keeps a private count of referrals in the back of her shorthand book. Hasn't told Blum.

**Voice:**
- [briefing]: "Homicide didn't want it, which is the first interesting thing about it. Second interesting thing's in the coroner's van. Go."
- [cover]: "You have until Thursday before somebody upstairs asks me why. Make me a liar by Wednesday."
- [the tell]: "I've closed cases that should've stayed open. Don't ask which. Just understand why this one's different, and don't waste it."
