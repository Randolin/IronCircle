# Toby Small

> *Ratatoskr.*

**Pitch:** Fourteen, Vera's paperboy and apprentice. Restarting her paper because nobody told him he can't. Hums the Piper's tune without knowing what it is.
**Wants:** To find out who killed Miss Vera.
**Clock:** stencils Day 6, first issue Day 11. The second issue, Day 13, puts him on Weaver's list. The humming tells Bunting where he is.

## Detail

**Concept:** The message-runner on the world-tree: paperboy at the top of the city by dawn, mimeograph crank at the bottom by dusk — and bilingual, carrying words between the tower's English and the stoops' Spanish, which is the Ratatoskr job description exactly. Currently humming.

**Motivation (specific):** Immediate: find out who killed Miss Vera, by attaching himself to the only adults who seem to be trying. Beneath it: restart *The Plain Truth*, because he knows where the stencils are and nobody's told him he can't. Beneath that, unexaminable at fourteen: be somebody's apprentice again.

**Performable mannerisms:** Talks in headlines. Editorializes every message he delivers ("Mr. Okafor says come by, and he's in a MOOD"). Never still — perches, drums, winds between adults. Cadges cigarettes he doesn't smoke, trades them as currency. And lately: the tune, under his breath, when his mouth has nothing else to do.

**Voice:**
- [salesman]: "Morning edition! Everything in it's a lie except the ballgame! Nickel!"
- [grief, angry]: "Everybody calls her the joke name. She wasn't a joke. She paid me same day, every week, which is more than the OTHER paper does."
- [the hook, innocent]: "This? I dunno. The sax man taught me. The one that knocks on doors. It's pretty, ain't it?"

**Visual description:** Wiry Puerto Rican kid, fourteen — his mother's people are from Ponce; "Small" is his father's name. Brown skin, flat cap over untended dark curls, canvas paper-sack worn crosswise, knuckles ink-gray. One sock up, one down, permanently. Quick dark eyes that read every room for exits and customers. Switches languages mid-sentence when excited.
