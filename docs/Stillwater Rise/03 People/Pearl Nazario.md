# Pearl Nazario

> *Front desk.*

**Concept:** The agency's switchboard, typist, and door. Twenty-four, Delancey-block born, hired by [[Odessa Cole|Cole]] two years ago off a settlement-house typing certificate. [[Herminia Vega]]'s niece: her mother is the sister two doors down the hall.

**Motivation (specific):** Get out of the Flats and get her mother out with her, before the letters stop being letters. She is not sentimental about the district and thinks the buyout is the smart move, which is the argument she and her aunt have every Sunday. She will be wrong about that in a way that's hard to watch. Immediate: keep the job, which is the first one she's had where nobody calls her *the girl*.

**Performable mannerisms:** Switchboard cord in her teeth while she writes. A pencil behind each ear, one for messages and one for herself. Repeats phone messages back verbatim, in the caller's cadence, without noticing. Corrects the players' Spanish, kindly, once.

**Voice:**
- [the desk]: "Blum and Cole. No, she's not in. Yes, that's what I'd say if she were. Who's calling?"
- [Sunday]: "Titi, it's a *check*. It's money for a place with a bathroom that isn't in the hall. Read the third one. Just read it."
- [Day 8, after her mother's call]: "A man came to the fourth floor this morning. Very polite. Mama says he tipped his hat to her. Mama says he told Titi to pack the good dishes first." *(beat)* "Why does everybody know about the dishes?"

**Visual description:** Puerto Rican woman, twenty-four, light brown skin, dark hair in a smooth set with a ribbon band, cat-eye glasses she takes off to think. Office cardigan over a print blouse; a settlement-house typing pin on the collar she's proud of and pretends not to be.

**Use:** She is how the Delancey block reaches the office, and how the office learns that the ninth is a person. When the players find *Vega, H., the 9th* in the notebook, Pearl is at the desk. Cole didn't know until now. The [[Danger Board]] paper column can reach her: an agency employee with family on the docket is a "conflict of interest" the county board can use to suspend the Coroner's retainer.
