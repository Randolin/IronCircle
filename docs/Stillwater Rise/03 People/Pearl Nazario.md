# Pearl Nazario

> *Front desk.*

**Pitch:** Front desk. Phones, typing, the door, and the agency's connection to the Delancey block. Herminia Vega's niece.
**Wants:** To get her mother out of the Flats before the letters stop being letters.
**Gives:** the neighborhood. Danger: the family tie is the 'conflict' Ard uses to cut the retainer.

## Detail

- **Look:** Puerto Rican woman, twenty-four, cat-eye glasses she takes off to think, dark hair in a set with a ribbon band, office cardigan, a typing-certificate pin she's proud of.
- **Manner:** Switchboard cord in her teeth. A pencil behind each ear. Repeats messages back in the caller's cadence. Corrects your Spanish, kindly, once.
- **Sundays:** argues with her aunt Herminia about taking the check. She thinks the buyout is the smart move. She's wrong in a way that's hard to watch.
- **Day 8:** 'Mama says he told Titi to pack the good dishes first. Why does everybody know about the dishes?'

**Voice:**
- [the desk]: "Blum and Cole. No, she's not in. Yes, that's what I'd say if she were. Who's calling?"
- [Sunday]: "Titi, it's a *check*. It's money for a place with a bathroom that isn't in the hall. Read the third one. Just read it."
- [Day 8, after her mother's call]: "A man came to the fourth floor this morning. Very polite. Mama says he tipped his hat to her. Mama says he told Titi to pack the good dishes first." *(beat)* "Why does everybody know about the dishes?"
