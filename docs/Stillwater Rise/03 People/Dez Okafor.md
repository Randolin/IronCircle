# Dez Okafor

> *The Griot.*

**Concept:** The barber who is the district's living library. Keeps the dead alive out loud, and won't hand them to paper.

**Motivation (specific):** Keep the recitation intact through the clearances — every family, every name, every debt and kindness, held in the spoken line. Immediate and private: the guilt. Vera hummed him the tune and he threw her out instead of telling her what it was. He knew in eight bars. He's known the song since his grandmother's warnings.

**Performable mannerisms:** Cuts hair through entire conversations; the scissors never pause and never err. Answers questions with genealogy ("You're asking about the Harmon boy — his mother's people came up in '31…"). Prices haircuts by what the chair's occupant can afford, silently. Will not speak the dead's names in front of strangers until the strangers have been *placed*.

**Voice:**
- [gatekeeping]: "I know who you are. Question's who your people are. Sit. Talk. The cut's a dollar; the rest gets earned."
- [the recitation, once earned]: "Fourteen from the Delancey block. You want them in order of going, or in order of who they were?"
- [the guilt]: "She brought me eight bars and I put her on the sidewalk. You want to know what the song is? First tell me you'll do better with it than I did."

**Visual description:** Black man, forties, straight-backed, close-trimmed beard, short groomed hair, sharp dresser under the pressed smock. Shop walls dense with photographs — decades of the district's faces, none labeled; the labels are in him. Scissors like an extension of his hand.

**Stat block:** — (his power is the recitation; threats against him mobilize the entire district)

**Clock:** From Day 4 he begins quietly telling families to leave — no explanation offered, no reason given, just *go, this month, don't wait for the letter.* It works. The Delancey and Boyle blocks empty ahead of schedule, which delights the Authority, accelerates the clearances, and puts Okafor on the very short list of people visibly interfering with a docket. By Day 10 he is on the danger board. He does not know it and would not stop if he did.
