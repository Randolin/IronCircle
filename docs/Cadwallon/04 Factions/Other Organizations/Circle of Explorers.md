# Circle of Explorers

> "Groups coming back up from the underground display their latest discoveries there, or share incredible rumors from the deep."

The hub of underground exploration in Cadwallon — part guild, part guild-hall, part tavern, part marketplace for the dangerous trade of plundering the [[Cynwäll Ruins|ruins]] beneath the city. The Circle is where the **shrews** (underground explorers) gather, outfit, recruit, sell their finds, and trade rumors before descending into the entrails of the Free City.

## Glory-Hunt

The Circle's home is **[[Glory-Hunt]]** — a bustling base camp in a great cave under the **east district of [[Soma]]**, near the cliff (with a second access from [[Gamehead]], near [[The Skinner]]). An opening thirty meters up lets in a pale, surreal light. A troglodyte village is dug into the cave walls. This is the staging ground for every serious expedition into the deep.

- **Night rights** — Glory-Hunt is the one place to legally buy the **"night rights"**: a 50-ducat license to exploit the underground's riches *without* being a free leaguer. An ancient metal portcullis, **guarded by the [[Militia]]**, controls access to the foundations of the old Cynwäll city beyond.
- **The Wheelbarrows** — a succession of caves where dwarves sell underground maps and knowledge for a percentage of discoveries; peddlers hawk mining gear, light sources, compasses, and instruments. Reliable goods cost ~20% more here, with Availability one point higher.
- **Compass** — a notable blind contact who dispenses advice; his presence surprises no one

## The Circle Building

The unavoidable checkpoint of Glory-Hunt: a building studded with ornate gargoyles and disturbing sculptures emerging from the chalky dust. Returning groups display their finds and share rumors. A warm **tavern in the basement** is where shrews spend a last evening before descending — and where **patrons and scholars** come to mount expeditions for specific items.

## What They Do

- **Exploration brokerage** — matching shrews to patrons, expeditions to funding, finds to buyers
- **Outfitting** — gear, maps, light, expertise
- **Knowledge trade** — the dwarves of the Wheelbarrows sell hard-won underground intelligence
- **The night-rights system** — legitimizing (and taxing) non-leaguer exploration

## The Watchers

> [!secret] Cynwäll surveillance
> **Cynwäll spies permanently watch the Circle of Explorers building** to keep an eye on what the shrews bring up from the deep. When something significant surfaces — a [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]]-era artifact, a [[Cynwäll Ruins|Cynwäll]] vestige, anything touching the [[Labyrinth]] — the shrews are **roughly dispossessed, or offered great sums of gold** to surrender the find. The Cynwäll (via [[Dyrsin]]) do not want certain things leaving the underground. The Circle knows it's watched. The Circle takes the gold.

## Relations

- **[[Militia]]** — guards the portcullis to the deep ruins; regulates the night-rights system
- **[[Cynwäll]] / [[Dyrsin]]** — surveillance and quiet confiscation (above)
- **[[Guild of Architects]]** — clients; the Architects hire shrews for vestige extraction and catacrypt survey (including, secretly, around the [[Tractor]] bore)
- **[[The Rags]]** — trade partners; Rags know the shallow tunnels, the Circle the deep
- **[[Free Leagues]]** — overlapping membership; many shrews are leaguers and vice versa, though night-rights let non-leaguers play too
- **[[Sophet Drahas]]'s [[Court of Ashes]]** — uncomfortably adjacent; the Court sits in the same underground the Circle explores

He's paying well and asking no questions. The Cynwäll watchers have already noticed him. So has someone from the Court of Ashes.

> [!hook] The find that got someone killed
