# Other Organizations

The bodies that hold real power in Cadwallon without a formal seat at any council — secret societies, underground networks, semi-organized communities, and the shadow groups that shape the city from below and between.

## Underground Networks

- [[Circle of Explorers]] — the hub of underground exploration; HQ at [[Glory-Hunt]] under east [[Soma]]; sells "night rights"; watched by Cynwäll spies
- [[The Rags]] — the ragmen of the Demijohn; waste-collectors turned archivists and evidence-readers; led by Vytriol; the militia's go-to for physical evidence
- [[The Howlers]] — the outcast Wolfen kin; HQ at the Howling pack house on [[Windswept Hill]]; bounty hunters and Devastation exiles

## Magical & Mystic Societies

- [[The Tarot-Mages]] — the inner circle of tarot magic; the Open Hand vs Closed Door civil war over the [[Labyrinth]]
- [[Voyagers]] — magicians who walk the strange Realms beyond mortal senses
- The **Order of the Chimera** — Lion-aligned scholars in the [[Walled Library]]'s enlightened caves, studying the Unfinished Realm of Hollowdeep *(stub pending)*

## Shadow Powers

- [[Sophet Drahas]]'s **[[Court of Shadows]]** — the King of Ashes' hidden "fief" in the [[Court of Ashes]]; the Shadows clan; financed through the [[Guild of Usurers]]
- The **[[Ophidians]]** — the surviving Serpent holdouts; [[S'ylice]] in the [[Walled Library]] coiling depths; [[Ss'Alish]] beneath [[Buoyancy Bay]]
- The **[[Coiling Emissaries]]** — the six hooded figures who visit the Duke every Day of Ashes since 999

## Information & Influence

- The **[[Bards of Destiny]]** — itinerant truth-tellers and intelligence brokers; **Argos Brisandre** is the most active; **Podgy** runs the Fields cooperative in [[Trophy]]

## Historical & Vanished Bodies

These are not active organizations, but their structures, lineages, and cultural memory still shape Cadwallon — institutions referenced as if present.

- The **[[Dogs of War]]** — the founding mercenary company; dissolved 832 into the [[Houses of Peers]], the [[Free Leagues]], and the [[Calendar]] itself. The 22 [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]] are still in scattered hands. *(For the founding war's events, see [[The Dogs of War|The Dogs of War (history)]].)*

## How These Relate to the Three Pillars

The [[Houses of Peers]], [[Guilds]], and [[Free Leagues]] are the **formal** powers. The organizations here are the **informal** ones — but informal does not mean weak. The [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]] guard knowledge that could end the world; [[Sophet Drahas]] stabilizes the city from a throne no one acknowledges; the [[The Rags|Rags]] hold more honest records than the Scriptorium; the [[Circle of Explorers]] controls access to the [[Underground]] where the city's deepest secrets lie. Power in Cadwallon does not require a seat at the table.
