# Dogs of War

> "There are no more Dogs of War. There are only the lineages they founded, the leagues they fathered, and the cards they carried."
> — Inscription at the [[Great Hall]]

The founding mercenary company of Cadwallon, **dissolved at the city's founding in 832** when its surviving members became Peers, leaguers, or burials. It does not exist as an active organization today. But its **institutional footprint shapes nearly every body in the Free City**, and Cadwës still invoke "the Dogs of War" as both ancestry and identity. *(For the historical events of their war and founding, see [[The Dogs of War|The Dogs of War (history)]].)*

## Composition

- **200 warriors total** at the height of the campaign — outlaws, exiles, and sellswords drawn from every people on [[Aarklash]]
- Organized into **21 free companies**, each led by one of **Vanius's 21 lieutenants**
- **Plus Vanius the captain** — **22 in command**. The number is not incidental: it equals [[Desire]]'s 22 wounds, the [[The 22 Arcana|22 arcana]], and the 22 months of the [[Calendar|Free Calendar]]. The Dogs of War's organizational structure was **the same shape as the magic that saved them**, before they knew the magic existed.

## Command

- **Captain:** [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]] — the only commander. The Dogs of War had no second-in-command and no council of lieutenants; Vanius led directly through his 21.
- **Lieutenants (21):** each held one company. Several are named in surviving record:
  - **[[Khaurik]]** — the ogre lieutenant; died 832 defending the camp. His heirs hold [[Ogrokh]].
  - **[[Johann of Drakaër|Johann]]** — originally **of [[Alahan]]**; befriended the [[Cynwäll]] and won the dragon-riding privilege in 843. Changed his surname to **Drakaër** and founded the line.
  - **Racllk** — ennobled in 833; founded the [[Kraken|Kraken fiefdom]].
  - The other 18 are mostly lost to record; the [[Houses of Peers]] preserve fragments.

## Doctrine & Method

- **Excavation under fire.** The company's actual work was *clearing the bewitched [[Lanever]] jungle* to dig out the Cynwäll ruins. The companies were as much a labor force as a fighting force — they dug, hauled, and held simultaneously.
- **Fight by section.** Each of the 21 companies cleared and defended its own assigned stretch of ruin. The decentralization meant losing one company didn't break the line.
- **The tarot weapon.** At the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]], Vanius's deck was infused with [[Desire]]'s elixirs — the **22 [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]]** — and distributed one card per lieutenant (Vanius keeping two). This is the founding moment of Cadwë tarot magic and the lineage every modern [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mage]] traces back to.

## The Named Companies

The 21 company names are mostly lost. Fragments survive:

- **The Steel Hearts** — the dwarven-heavy company; [[Yelmor]] served in it before vanishing into the catacrypts in 857 and re-emerging from the [[Labyrinth]] in 1003 (he aged a few days; the city aged 146 years)
- A handful of others persist as **Cadwë idioms** — "when the Steel Hearts march" for late autumn, "to dig like the Twelfth" for thankless work — without anyone now remembering the company.

## What Became of Them

The Dogs of War did not retire — they **transformed into Cadwallon's foundational institutions**:

| What it became | What it was |
|---|---|
| [[Free Leagues]] | The Dogs of War's poorer descendants, kept exploring the ruins; reborn as defenders in [[The Akkyshan Offensive|983]] |
| [[Houses of Peers]] | Lineages of the 21 lieutenants who *did* settle |
| [[Calendar|Free Calendar]] | 22 months on the 21 + 1 structure |
| [[The 22 Arcana]] | One card per lieutenant + two for Vanius; mostly now scattered or lost |
| The ducal title | Created because Vanius refused both kingship and barony |
| The Great Hall | Built on the [[Wall of Earth]] where the founding battle was won — still the seat of the [[Free Leagues]] |

## Present-Day Footprint

Every Free City institution can trace a thread to a specific company or lieutenant. The most live ones:

- **The lost cards.** Each lieutenant received one of the 22 arcana. **Most are no longer in their founding family** — lost, stolen, scattered, sold. Sophet Drahas already holds two. [[Duke Den Azhir]] wants them recovered. The [[Bards of Destiny]] and [[The Tarot-Mages]] have been quietly tracking the diaspora for generations. *(see [[The 22 Arcana]])*
- **The graves.** [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]] is officially buried in the upper-city catacrypts. He is also widely rumored to be **alive in the [[Labyrinth]]**, reaching out through the cards. Both can be true.
- **The Steel Hearts.** If one returned from the maze 146 years late, others may still be down there — Dogs of War, centuries old, still fighting a battle that ended before Cadwallon was born.
