# Guilds

> "We do not deny the fine work you do for our city's economy, but we will not let the free leaguers be dispossessed of their rights."
> — Duke Den Azhir, before the Guilds Council

The guilds are one of the **three pillars of power** in Cadwallon, alongside the [[Houses of Peers|Houses of Peers]] and the [[Free Leagues]]. They sit on the **Guilds Council** (a meeting body of all guildmasters, not a guild in itself), elect representatives to the diplomatic council, and shape ducal policy through finance, infrastructure, and quiet leverage.

Most cataloged below operate openly. All have shadows.

## The Power Guilds

The "upper-hand" guilds that dominate Cadwallon's political economy:

- [[Guild of Usurers]] — debt, leverage, the shadow bank of [[Sophet Drahas]]
- [[Guild of Goldsmiths]] — legitimate banking, diplomatic finance, [[Var-Nokkt]] ally
- [[Guild of Thieves]] — sanctioned underworld, [[Soma]] founders, [[Morgue Street]] HQ
- [[Guild of Blades]] — arms and mercenaries, [[Ogrokh]] base
- [[Guild of Architects]] — engineering, urban planning, the [[Tractor]] project
- [[Guild of Ferrymen]] — shipping, harbors, airships
- [[Guild of Cartomancers]] — tarot, divination, the official calendar

## The Working Guilds

Smaller, more specialized — but each essential and each holding real power in its sphere:

- [[Guild of Blacksmiths]] — weapons and tools, [[Thunder Vale]] forges
- [[Guild of Scribes]] — copying, archives, the [[Drakaër|Great Scriptorium]]
- [[Guild of Tailors]] — daily fashion, Green Terrace flora
- [[Guild of Dressmakers]] — ceremonial fashion, the [[Var-Nokkt|Belvedere]]
- [[Guild of Favorites]] — courtesans and confidants, the [[Var-Nokkt|Glistenning]]
- [[Guild of Birders]] — Immobilis fauna, founded 992
- [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] — sewers, catacrypt edges, underground intelligence

## The Guilds Council

The Guilds Council is the formal meeting body where all guildmasters sit together. It is **not a guild**. It is a forum. It has limited formal power — it can pass guild-policy resolutions, formally protest ducal decrees, and propose changes to inter-guild rules — but its *informal* power is large. Every major guild rivalry, every shadow alliance, every leak runs through the Council in some form.

The Duke is invited to the Council annually to defend his policies. The free leaguers are mentioned in council debates as one of the recurring grievances.

## Rag'narok Alliance Map

Each major guild maintains formal commercial-military alliances with cultures across [[Aarklash]]. These run *across* the [[Three Alliances]] and are a major reason Cadwallon's neutrality holds. From the source:

| Guild | Allies |
|-------|--------|
| Architects | [[Cynwäll]], Griffins of [[Akkylannie]], Lions of [[Alahan]], dwarves of [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] |
| Blades | All peoples |
| Ferrymen | All peoples |
| Goldsmiths | [[Acheron]], Dirz, [[No-Dan-Kar]], [[Akkylannie]], [[Alahan]], [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] |
| Usurers | All [[Meanders of Darkness]] peoples |
| Thieves | [[Acheron]], [[Akkyshan]], Dirz, [[Vile-Tis]], [[No-Dan-Kar]], [[Alahan]], [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] |
| Cartomancers | [[Cynwäll]], [[No-Dan-Kar]], [[Akkylannie]], [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]], [[Alahan]] |

The Blades and Ferrymen sell to everyone. The Cartomancers and Architects favor the technologically-sophisticated cultures. The Thieves and Usurers run the Darkness trade. The Goldsmiths sit in the middle and skim every conversion.
