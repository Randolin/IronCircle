# Guild of Ferrymen

> "If you didn't pay us, it didn't move."
> — Old guild boast

The shipping guild. Controls **every commercial vessel** that enters or leaves [[Kraken Harbor]], every dock-crew that loads cargo, every airship that flies between the [[Cynwäll]] pillars, and every cart-train that comes overland from the [[Lanever]] interior. The least flashy of the upper-hand guilds. Possibly the most essential. When the Ferrymen strike, the city stops eating within a week.

## What They Do

- **Sea shipping** — the merchant fleet's vessels are Ferrymen-crewed and Ferrymen-flagged. The fleets sail at the start of the Time of Banners (spring) and return in the Time of Return (autumn).
- **Lake and river transport** — the canals and waterways of Cadwallon are theirs. Ferrymen barges link the upper city's mansions to the harbor.
- **Airship operations** — the **sky station** in [[Drakaër|the Pillars]] is Ferrymen-built and Ferrymen-run. Var-Nokkt's family airship, the **Senex III**, is Var-Nokkt-owned but Ferrymen-crewed.
- **Overland caravans** — the great cart-trains that resupply the city from Lanever and the Aegis foothills run under Ferrymen contract.
- **Harbor management** — pilotage, dock allocation, customs coordination with the militia

## Headquarters

- **The Lakeside Manor** in the [[Marina]] — large compound with a private dock. Hosts visiting port-masters from across [[Aarklash]] in the diplomatic suite.
- **Dockmasters' Hall** in [[Buoyancy Bay]] — operational center for the working harbor
- Branch offices at every major dock, the sky station, and the overland caravan gates

## The 991 Strike

The defining recent event in guild history. The Ferrymen struck for six weeks in 991 — the harbor stopped, food shortages began, the Usurers made fortunes lending against starving merchants. The Duke at the time negotiated personally. The strike ended with:
- Higher Ferrymen wages
- A new guild seat on the **diplomatic council**
- A quiet concession the public never saw — **the Ferrymen now have first refusal on any new airship technology developed in Cadwallon**

The dwarven engineers of [[Var-Nokkt]] still resent this. Several Tir-Nâ-Bor designs have stayed in the drawer because the Ferrymen would have controlled them.

## Allies in the Rag'narok

The PHB lists: **all peoples**. The Ferrymen ship for everyone. Cadwallon's commercial neutrality is in large part Ferrymen neutrality. They have crewed vessels flying [[Akkylannie|Akkylannian]] cargo on the same week as [[Acheron|Acheronian]] cargo. They charge by the hold, not by the flag.

## The Slave Question

The transport of slaves is *prohibited inside the city walls*. It is *permitted on board vessels entering the port*. The Duke taxes this traffic as "cargo." The Ferrymen execute the trade and take their cut. This is one of the moral compromises of Cadwallon's commercial freedom — Cadwës do not buy or sell slaves, but their port profits from those who do.

> [!secret] The Senex III's other cargo
> The [[Var-Nokkt]] family airship runs official routes to [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] for the Senex's diplomatic mission. It also runs *unofficial* routes — including a regular drop in the [[Quithayran]] mountains that the Ferrymen pilot has been paid to forget. **What goes up to those mountains is unknown.** What comes down, the pilot has seen, is occasionally a young dwarf who is not on the manifest.

## Relations

- **[[Var-Nokkt]]** — closest noble alliance. Joint operation of the Senex III and the sky station. Hildra Var-Nokkt is invited to Ferrymen council meetings.
- **[[Guild of Architects]]** — engineering partner. Architects design ships and airships; Ferrymen crew them. Sometimes amicable, sometimes feudal.
- **[[Guild of Thieves]]** — quiet arrangement. The Ferrymen don't search every barrel. The Thieves don't smuggle anything that could embarrass the guild publicly.
- **[[Guild of Birders]]** — operational partnership over Immobilis cargo
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — direct relationship. The Duke needs the Ferrymen functional. The Ferrymen need the Duke's customs policy.
