# Guild of Blacksmiths

> "Hammer, anvil, fire. Everything else is paperwork."
> — Workshop slogan

The arms and tool manufacturing guild. Less politically prominent than the [[Guild of Blades|Blades]] but the actual *makers* of nearly every weapon and tool the city consumes. Heavily ethnically mixed — dwarves from [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] hold the senior craft positions; goblin innovators from [[No-Dan-Kar]] hold the experimental positions; humans and orcs do the bulk production.

## What They Do

- **Weapons manufacture** — swords, polearms, dwarven crossbows, goblin firearms, ogre warhammers, armor across every weight class
- **Industrial tools** — forge equipment, mining gear, civic-works tools for the [[Guild of Architects|Architects]]
- **The thermo-forges** — Tir-Nâ-Bor-imported heated forges in [[Thunder Vale]] that allow superior metalwork
- **Experimental works** — the **Shooting Range** in Thunder Vale is the guild's R&D area for new firearms, siege engines, and explosive ordnance

## Headquarters

- **The Welding Arc** in [[Thunder Vale]] ([[Ogrokh]]) — the central guild seat. Massive forge complex spanning multiple workshops.
- **Vapordomes** — goblin-built workshops scattered through Thunder Vale for experimental firearm work
- Branch forges in [[Ka-Ân-Vor]] ([[Var-Nokkt]]) for jewelry-grade work and in [[The Docks]] for marine ironwork

## The Thunder Vale Crisis

The Blacksmiths are at the center of the same dwarf-vs-goblin succession crisis that's shaking the [[Guild of Blades]]. The senior dwarven smiths who built the guild are being supplanted by goblin craftsmen since Dazomet's regency. The dwarven masters have appealed to the [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] embassy via the [[Var-Nokkt]] family. The goblins have appealed to [[No-Dan-Kar]] via the embassy. **The next move triggers a fief-level conflict.**

## Relations

- **[[Guild of Blades]]** — primary buyer. Their fortunes rise and fall together.
- **[[Khaurik Argam]] / [[Ogrokh]]** — landlord. The fief takes a fee. The fief also calls in special-order weapons.
- **[[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] embassy** — strong, through the dwarven master-smiths
- **[[No-Dan-Kar]] embassy** — growing, through the goblin innovators
- **[[Guild of Architects]]** — operational partner for civil engineering tools

> [!hook] The vapordome explosion
> A goblin-built vapordome went up in flames last night. Five dead, including a senior master. The dwarven smiths are publicly grieving and privately celebrating. The Duke wants the cause confirmed before he's forced to make a public statement.

> [!hook] The custom commission
> A noble house quietly commissions a weapon with very specific, very strange specifications — a runed dagger meant to "cut through" something the buyer won't name. The senior smith refuses. A junior smith would take the contract if backed by free leaguers willing to deliver the materials.
