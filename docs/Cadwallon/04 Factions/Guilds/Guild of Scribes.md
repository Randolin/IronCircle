# Guild of Scribes

> "Ink lasts longer than gold and longer than blood. We are paid because we never forget."
> — Mottoof the Great Scriptorium

Cadwallon's official copyists, archivists, and contract-drafters. Anything that needs to be **written, witnessed, copied, sealed, or filed** runs through this guild. They are paid less than the Goldsmiths and have less muscle than the Blades — but every guild in the city owes them, because the Scribes hold their paperwork.

## What They Do

- **Document copying** — books, manuscripts, contracts. The Free University relies on them for textbooks.
- **Contract drafting and witnessing** — ducal contracts, inter-guild treaties, marriage settlements, fief charters. A Scribe's seal is recognized by every court in Cadwallon.
- **Archive management** — they maintain the **Great Scriptorium** in [[Drakaër]], one of the largest archives on [[Aarklash]] outside of Lanever and Akkylannie.
- **Letters and correspondence** — for the illiterate or the busy. Confidentiality is part of the contract; breaking it is a guild-level offense.
- **Ducal records** — the ducal archive itself is staffed by Scribes seconded to the Duke.

## Headquarters

- **The Great Scriptorium** — large complex in the [[Free District]] of Drakaër. Multiple wings: copying floor, archive vault, contract chambers, public reading room.
- Smaller scriptoria attached to the [[Free University of Cadwallon]] and to the ducal palace in the [[Ducal Enclave]].

## Why They Matter

The Scribes are not a *power* guild in the way the Usurers or Goldsmiths are. They are a **utility** guild — but every powerful guild keeps copies of their contracts, every noble family their genealogies, every ambassador their treaties at the Scriptorium. Anyone who can *read* the Scriptorium's holdings can compromise half the city.

> [!secret] The leaks
> The [[Drakaër]] family has been producing irrefutable documents that compromise the guilds in public debates for years. **The leaks come from the Scriptorium.** Specifically, from a small group of Scribes sympathetic to the [[Free Leagues]] who believe the guilds have overreached. The bard **Argos Brisandre** is the courier. Silnus Drakaër pays for the access. If the Scriptorium's senior council discovered this, the guilty Scribes would be exiled at minimum, killed at worst.

## Relations

- **[[Free University of Cadwallon]]** — symbiotic. The University trains future Scribes; the Scribes copy the University's books.
- **[[Drakaër]] family** — physically resident in the same fief. Officially neutral. *Unofficially*, as above.
- **[[Guild of Cartomancers]]** — strong: they hold each other's archives. Cartomancers maintain the calendar; Scribes copy and distribute it.
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — wary. The Usurers want every Scriptorium contract on the Var-Nokkts; the Scribes won't release them.

## Notable Members

- **The Chief Archivist** — name kept ceremonial; lifetime appointment; reads all sealed documents personally
- **Argos Brisandre** — bard, not officially a Scribe, but moves freely through the Scriptorium with the silent approval of certain copyists
- Multiple anonymous junior copyists with strong opinions about [[Free Leagues]]
