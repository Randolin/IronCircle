# Guild of Thieves

> "Once a year the Duke calls us criminals, in public, on behalf of the guilds. He pays the right price afterward."
> — A senior guildmaster, after one of Den Azhir's official speeches

The most paradoxical institution in Cadwallon: an **officially sanctioned guild for unofficial work**. The Thieves run organized lower-city theft, smuggling, pleasure houses, fencing, and the black market that the Usurers fund. Their members pay guild dues; their masters meet with the other guildmasters; the Duke condemns them annually. The arrangement has held since the founding.

## What They Do

- **Organized theft** — managed pickpocketing, burglary, and grift across the lower city. Members work assigned territories; freelancers are warned, then taught a lesson.
- **Smuggling** — illegal Immobilis goods, Akkyshan poisons, banned alchemy from Dirz, slave cargo not declared at the docks. The harbor underbelly is theirs.
- **Lower-city pleasure houses** — brothels, opium dens, gambling halls. The [[Guild of Favorites]] handles the upper-city market; the Thieves handle everything below.
- **Fencing** — high-value stolen goods are quietly resold through Thief-controlled markets in [[Morgue Street]]
- **Black-market hosting** — devourers of [[Vile-Tis]], Akkyshan elves, and other Cadwallon-discriminated agents stay in **Thief safehouses** and bring relics from Acheron raids, venoms from Ashinân, and contraband from Dirz in exchange.

## Headquarters

- **The Beggars Market in [[Morgue Street]]** — official seat. A walled district with one open gate. The plague-walls of 957 became their fortress when the [[Soma]] family allowed it.
- Branches in [[Petropolis]], [[The Skinner]] (Gamehead), [[Crimson Dress]] (Drakaër), and the [[Docks]]

## The Soma Connection — and the Wound

The Guild of Thieves was founded in part *by* the [[Soma]] family. **Aghovar Soma** — brother of [[Vanho Soma]] — was Grand Master of the Thieves until his death.

> [!secret] Aghovar's death
> [[Sophet Drahas]] killed Aghovar Soma directly. He took:
> - **The Devil card** — one of Vanius's 22 arcana
> - **The Talisman of Shadows** — the Thieves' founding artifact and the Soma family's hereditary protection
> The guild has never fully recovered. The Soma-Usurer vendetta intensified after this. [[Isabeau Soma]] hunts Drahas for both items.

The current Grand Master is unknown to the public — Aghovar's office was filled by a council. Some senior Thieves want a single Master again. Others want to dissolve the office permanently.

## Allies in the Rag'narok

The PHB lists: **Living-dead of [[Acheron]], [[Akkyshan]] elves, Alchemists of Dirz, devourers of [[Vile-Tis]], goblins of [[No-Dan-Kar]], [[Alahan|Lions of Alahan]], dwarves of [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]]**. The widest alliance roster of any guild — because the Thieves' work demands access to every kind of contraband.

## Relations

- **[[Soma]] family** — historical founders. Tense after Aghovar's death.
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — coordinated siblings. Usurer money flows through Thief operations.
- **[[Guild of Favorites]]** — territorial peace. Each stays in their market.
- **[[Guild of Ferrymen]]** — operational tension. The Ferrymen control the docks; the Thieves control what comes off them illegally. Quiet arrangements keep the peace; the 991 Ferrymen strike was partly a Thief negotiation play.
- **[[Guild of Birders]]** — antagonistic. Thieves smuggle uninspected Immobilis goods; the Birders are the one official body the Thieves cannot bribe.
- **[[Militia]]** — formal enemies. In practice, half the militia is on Thief payroll.
