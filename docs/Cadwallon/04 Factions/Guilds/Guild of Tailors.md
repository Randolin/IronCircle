# Guild of Tailors

> "The Dressmakers dress you for the ceremony. We dress you for the eleven months in between."
> — Senior tailor, in council

The day-to-day fashion guild. Where the [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers]] handle ceremonial work and high-end gowns, the Tailors handle **the everyday wardrobe of the upper city**: tunics, robes, traveling cloaks, riding leathers, working garments for nobility. Less glamorous than the Dressmakers. More profitable.

## What They Do

- **Daily fashion** for nobles, ambassadors, guild masters, and well-paid functionaries
- **Working-noble wear** — riding cloaks, hunting attire, day robes
- **The secret-plants trade** — the guild **cultivates and sells exotic plants** in addition to fashion. These are the same Garden-touched flora the [[Guild of Birders|Birders]] collect from [[Immobilis]] and the [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers]] weave into ceremonial fabric. The Tailors process them into ordinary-looking garments with quiet effects.
- **Refit and repair** — many Cadwës bring last year's wardrobe to a Tailor for restyling rather than buying new

## Headquarters

The Tailors have no single seat. The senior council meets at a different master atelier each session. Major ateliers are in [[Heaven Heights]] ([[Soma]]), the [[Dressmakers District]] ([[Var-Nokkt]]), and the [[Free District]] ([[Drakaër]]).

## The Dressmakers Alliance

The Tailors and the [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers]] share the **Green Terrace** rooftop garden in Var-Nokkt and the supply of Immobilis dream-flora from the [[Guild of Birders|Birders]]. They compete for clients while colluding on supply. The arrangement is older than living memory; nobody wants it disturbed.

## Relations

- **[[Guild of Dressmakers]]** — coopetitor. Sibling guild, eternal rival.
- **[[Guild of Birders]]** — supplier
- **[[Guild of Tanners and Furriers]]** — small leather-work guild, junior partner (not separately documented)
