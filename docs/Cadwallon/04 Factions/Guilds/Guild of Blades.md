# Guild of Blades

> "We sell weapons. We sell hands to wield them. We sell loyalty by the season. The cheapest of these is loyalty."
> — Mottoof the Span hall

The arms-and-soldiers guild. Sells weapons, armor, military goods, mercenary contracts, and private security across [[Aarklash]]. Cadwallon's most visible commercial muscle and the guild whose troops most often appear on Rag'narok battlefields. **The most ethnically diverse of the major guilds** — Blades hires everyone who can fight.

## What They Do

- **Arms trade** — weapons and armor from the [[Guild of Blacksmiths|Blacksmiths]] enter the Blade catalog and ship to every fightable culture on the continent
- **Mercenary contracts** — Blades fields its own companies under guild colors. The trading posts of the **guild's companies raise their oriflammes** at the start of the Time of Banners each year.
- **Private security** — bodyguards for nobles, embassies, and visiting dignitaries
- **Military training** — the [[Guild of Blacksmiths|Welding Arc]] and the **Shooting Range** in [[Thunder Vale]] are partly Blade-run. The Blades also run private salles d'armes in [[Crimson Dress]] (Drakaër).
- **The Field of Honor** — formal dueling ground in the [[Free District]] of Drakaër. Bladesmasters arbitrate.

## Headquarters

- **The Span hall** in [[Ogrokh]] — the central trade hall. Vast covered market, attached barracks, embassy of [[Akkylannie]] across the square. Blade companies muster here at the start of each campaign season.
- **Trade posts at the harbor** for outbound shipments
- **Salles** throughout the upper city for individual training

## The Khaurik Argam Arrangement

The Blades operate from [[Ogrokh]] under a **standing fee paid to the Khaurik Argam family** in exchange for control over military activities in the fief. The arrangement was unstable for years until the [[Khaurik|Khaurik Argam]] founder's policy of permanent loyalty proved the family wouldn't seize Blade property. The arrangement has held for over a century. The current succession crisis under [[Kurn Khaurik Argam]] threatens it.

## The Thunder Vale Crisis

> [!secret] The arms-makers' war
> Since the regency power-grab by **Dazomet** (Kurn's goblin godfather) in 1002, the Thunder Vale **Shooting Range** has shifted production from dwarven weaponsmiths to goblin craftsmen at Dazomet's encouragement. The Blades' senior council is divided:
> - One faction wants to back Kurn and restore the dwarven smiths (preserving traditional quality and the Tir-Nâ-Bor relationship)
> - One faction wants to side with Dazomet's goblin smiths (cheaper, faster, and politically protected by No-Dan-Kar)
> The Duke's intervention has frozen the situation. **The next move triggers a Thunder Vale civil conflict.** The Blade companies are quietly choosing sides. So is the [[Guild of Blacksmiths|Blacksmiths' guild]].

## Isabeau Soma — The Hunter

[[Isabeau Soma]], niece of [[Vanho Soma]] and daughter of the murdered Anon Soma, **joined the Guild of Blades** and works as a senior operative. Officially she handles diplomatic security contracts working with [[Brehnan of Alahan]], the Alahan ambassador, at Whitehaven in [[Heaven Heights]]. Unofficially she is the spear-point of the Soma vendetta against the Usurers and the hunt for [[Sophet Drahas]].

She owns (but rarely visits) the **Last Dance cabaret** in Heaven Heights.

## Allies in the Rag'narok

The PHB lists: **all peoples**. The widest alliance roster alongside the Ferrymen — Blades sells to anyone who pays. Cadwallon's commercial neutrality runs through the Blades as much as the Ferrymen.

## Relations

- **[[Khaurik Argam]] / [[Ogrokh]]** — long-standing landlord arrangement. Strained by the 1002 succession crisis.
- **[[Guild of Blacksmiths]]** — supplier and partner. Where the Thunder Vale war goes, both guilds follow.
- **[[Alahan]] embassy / [[Brehnan of Alahan|Brehnan]]** — strong working alliance. Isabeau Soma is the bridge.
- **[[Free Leagues]]** — primary professional rival and recruiting pool. Many free leaguers are former Blades; many Blades wish they were free leaguers. Mutual professional respect; occasional contract disputes.
- **[[Militia]]** — ambiguous. The militia regards mercenaries with suspicion; the Blades regard militiamen as undertrained civil servants.
- **[[Drakaër]] family** — strong. The Field of Honor and the [[Light Cavalry Academy of Lanever]] both operate under Blade arbitration.
