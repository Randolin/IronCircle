# Guild of Birders

> "Pardon me, my Lady. Could you please avoid touching this flower — we could lose you to raptures."
> — Discourse of a Provost of the Bird Catchers to visitors on Immobilis

The youngest of Cadwallon's official guilds. Founded **in 992** to regulate the catching, transport, and sale of exotic fauna from the **[[Immobilis]] archipelago** — the chain of islands east of the city where [[Desire]]'s dreams seed Garden-strange life into the world.

## What They Do

- **Bird Catchers' field operations** — capture, breed, and ship rare birds, mammals, reptiles, and arthropods from Immobilis to the Cadwallon market. The most valuable exports of the archipelago after slave-cargo and dream-wood.
- **Quarantine** — every specimen entering the city must be inspected. The Birders maintain holding pens at the [[Marina]] and the [[Buoyancy Bay]] docks.
- **Provost training** — the Provosts are the senior catchers, trained to handle Garden-touched specimens without falling into rapture. Most are former [[Voyagers]] or [[Daïkinee]] forest-walkers.
- **Botanical samples** — the same Provosts also collect the addictive flora the [[Guild of Tailors]] uses for noble fashion-fabrics. Officially this is "shared expertise"; in practice it's a small monopoly the Tailors pay handsomely to keep.

## Headquarters

- **The Provost's Hall on Immobilis** — fortified compound on the main island. Holds breeding pens, the central archive of species records, and the Provost council chamber.
- **Cadwallon branch office** — small, by the [[Marina]] docks. Mainly a customs and inspection post.

## Why They Exist

Before 992, Immobilis fauna was traded by anyone with a fast boat. Several catastrophic releases — addictive moths into [[Heaven Heights]], a Garden-vine into [[Petropolis]], and the infamous **rapture-flower incident at the ducal court in 991** — forced the Duke to charter a regulating body. **Den Azhir signed the founding writ as one of his early acts**; founding the Birders was popular and demonstrated competence.

## The Real Job

> [!secret] What the Provosts actually study
> Immobilis is **a magical-grove archipelago** — every island is a shoot from [[Desire]]'s prison. The Provosts are the only profession in Cadwallon with sustained, dispassionate exposure to **Garden-touched ecology**. They have *opinions* about what Desire is dreaming. They don't share them. But the senior Provosts are quietly the best living source of intelligence on Desire's current state — and the **rate of Garden activity has measurably increased since 999**. They know. The Duke knows they know.

## Relations

- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — direct patronage. The Duke considers the Birders his eyes on Immobilis.
- **[[Guild of Tailors]]** — close commercial alliance over the dream-flora.
- **[[Guild of Ferrymen]]** — operational partner. Every Birder shipment goes by Ferrymen vessel.
- **[[Voyagers]]** — recruiting overlap. Many Provosts are former Voyagers; the two organizations exchange information freely.
- **[[Guild of Thieves]]** — antagonistic. The Thieves smuggle uninspected Immobilis goods; the Birders confiscate and prosecute.
