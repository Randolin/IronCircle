# Guild of Goldsmiths

> "We deal in gold. Gold has no allegiance. Neither, therefore, do we."
> — Inscribed in the entrance hall of the Gilded Tower

The legitimate banking and luxury-metalsmithing guild. The richest single body in Cadwallon by paper assets. Where the [[Guild of Usurers|Usurers]] handle leverage, debt, and shadow finance, the Goldsmiths handle **diplomatic banking, treaty financing, foreign exchange, and the precious-metals trade**. They are the guild that visiting ambassadors meet first.

## What They Do

- **Banking for nobles, embassies, and major guilds** — deposit accounts, letters of credit, currency exchange
- **Goldsmithing and jewelry** — luxury work for the upper city and the embassies. Akkylannian ceremonial chains, Alahan signet rings, dwarven inlay work for [[Tir-Nâ-Bor|Tir-Nâ-Bor]] visitors.
- **Diplomatic financing** — when an embassy needs money to keep agents in the field, the Goldsmiths provide it. When a guild needs to deliver tribute to an ally nation, the Goldsmiths route it.
- **Currency standardization** — they enforce the **klû** (the goblin coin) as the international standard inside Cadwallon. They mint the local ducat. They quietly skim every conversion.

## Headquarters

- **The Gilded Tower** in [[Drakaër]] — sumptuous Var-Nokkt-funded tower with luxury mall, hotel, and the main banking floors. Prestigious foreign guests stay here when not at an embassy.
- Branch offices at every embassy, the Free University, and major guild halls
- A heavily fortified vault under [[Ka-Ân-Vor]] in [[Var-Nokkt]]

## The Var-Nokkt Alliance

The Goldsmiths and [[Var-Nokkt]] are closely interwoven. Var-Nokkt money keeps the Gilded Tower lit; Goldsmith deposits keep Var-Nokkt liquid; both families intermarry where allowed. The Var-Nokkts "permanently support the guilds' councils financially" through a Goldsmith conduit, and the Goldsmiths in turn vote with Var-Nokkt interests on most council questions.

This alliance has been the central spine of upper-city economic power for two generations. It is also now visibly cracking.

## The Master Ôdril Question

> [!secret] What Ôdril Haarken started
> **Master Ôdril Haarken** is a Goldsmith chapter master and a respected senior figure in the guild. He has begun publicly asking: *"What do the Usurers hold over [[Var-Nokkt]] that keeps the family compliant?"* This is the first time in living memory that a senior Goldsmith has publicly challenged the Var-Nokkt-Usurer leverage. The implications are massive:
> - If Ôdril is right, the Goldsmiths' alliance with Var-Nokkt is built on Usurer compromise
> - If Ôdril is silenced, the Goldsmith senior council will know it was the Usurers and the alliance ends in private war
> - If Ôdril continues unchecked, the entire upper-city power structure restructures itself in a year
> Free leaguers who can deliver evidence will reshape Cadwallon politics. Free leaguers who deliver evidence to the wrong side will not see the next year.

## The Tamarez Canal Scandal (966)

The Goldsmiths' historical low point. A faction of crooked Goldsmiths lured the [[Ghieronburg|Ghiérons]] into funding a canal project that was never going to work. The Ghiérons retaliated by making the scandal public — exposing not only the crooked Goldsmiths but tainting "even the most skillful speculators." The guild has not forgiven the Ghiéron family. **Adalban Ghiéron** is privately blacklisted from major Goldsmith services.

## Allies in the Rag'narok

The PHB lists: **Living-dead of [[Acheron]], Alchemists of Dirz, goblins of [[No-Dan-Kar]], Griffins of [[Akkylannie]], Lions of [[Alahan]], dwarves of [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]]**. The widest *legitimate* alliance roster — every nation that pays in coin rather than only blood. Notably *not* the [[Akkyshan]] elves, who use the [[Guild of Thieves|Thieves]] for finance.

## Relations

- **[[Var-Nokkt]]** — primary noble alliance. Strained by Ôdril's question.
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — competitor and parallel. Public competitors, private collaborators in some markets. Ôdril's challenge has converted this from rivalry into open suspicion.
- **[[Guild of Architects]]** — operational alliance. Architects design the towers; Goldsmiths fund them.
- **[[Guild of Ferrymen]]** — close. Goldsmith bullion shipments are the Ferrymen's highest-paid cargo.
- **[[Ghieronburg|Ghiéron]] family** — cold. The 966 scandal is alive.
