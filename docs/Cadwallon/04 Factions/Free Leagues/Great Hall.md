# Great Hall

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The headquarters of the [[Free Leagues]], standing on the [[Wall of Earth]] where Vanius won the founding battle. Leagues register their charter and blazon here; the roster of active (and waiting-to-be-"re-decked") leagues is displayed; new leaguers "consult Vanius" by hiring a [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-master]] at the adjacent Tavern of the Tarot.
