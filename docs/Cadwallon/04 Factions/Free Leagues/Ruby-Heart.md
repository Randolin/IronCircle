# Ruby-Heart

> The free league that produced a Duke. One of the city's prestige outfits.

## Canon Facts
- A standing free league with enough prestige and reach that Duke [[Duke Den Azhir|Den Azhir]] was a member before his elevation to the throne in 995.
- Den Azhir's continued favor toward free leaguers as a class stems in part from his Ruby-Heart history.

## Members of Note
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — former member, now Duke.

> [!hook] The old comrades
> Anyone who was in the Ruby-Heart during Den Azhir's tenure knew him before power. That access is worth more than gold to the right buyer.
