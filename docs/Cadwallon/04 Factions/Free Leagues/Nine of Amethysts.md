# Nine of Amethysts

> "Requisitioned by the Duke, by virtue of the leaguers' duty of protection of the city..."

A free league led by the woman called **Ambush**, currently under ducal requisition to patrol the rural [[Trophy]] fief and intercept the smugglers who slip goods into Cadwallon through the open farmland of [[The Fields]]. A working, mid-tier league — not the ducal-favorite prestige of the [[Ruby-Heart]], but a competent outfit doing exactly the unglamorous defensive duty the [[Free Leagues|leagues]] were chartered for.

## The Job

[[The Fields]] in eastern [[Trophy]] is the city's agricultural belt — and, at night when the farmers are gone, a **smuggling corridor**. Convoys move illegal goods across the open fields toward the city, avoiding the harbor customs and the [[Tractor]] gates. The Nine of Amethysts are the league **requisitioned by the Duke** to fight this traffic: patrolling, intercepting, and arresting smugglers, calling in other leagues when a convoy is too large to handle alone.

This is **duty work, not treasure-hunting** — the leaguers' protective obligation made concrete. It pays steadily and earns ducal goodwill, but it's dangerous, thankless, and puts the league crosswise with whoever is funding the smuggling.

## Ambush

The league's leader. A capable field commander with a strong grounding in [[Trophy]] (Fief knowledge 4), Barhan and Cadwë languages, and the leagues themselves. Her distinctive features — **Bravery, Infiltration, Cat's Paw** — mark her as a leader who fights from the front and moves unseen. The nickname is presumably earned.

## The Name

The "amethyst" suit is one of the six colors of the [[The 22 Arcana|tarot]] minor cards (amethyst, diamond, and so on), numbered one to ten. "Nine of Amethysts" is a card — the kind of name a league takes to tie itself, however loosely, to the legend of [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]]'s deck. Whether the league holds or seeks the actual card is unestablished — a usable hook.

## Relations

- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — current employer via ducal requisition
- **[[Trophy]] / [[Gwan Standing-wind]]** — operate in his fief; the Standing-Wind decline (three-quarters of the Fields' parcels sold off) is part of *why* the smuggling flourishes
- **Other [[Free Leagues|leagues]]** — called in as backup on large convoys; professional peers
- **The smugglers** — the standing enemy; their backers are unknown and worth investigating
- **The Fields cooperative** (under Podgy, a [[Bards of Destiny]] figure) — uneasy neighbors; the co-op's anti-merchant stance and the league's anti-smuggler stance sometimes align, sometimes don't

> [!hook] Who funds the convoys
> Ambush has noticed the smuggling convoys are too well-organized to be independent — someone with capital and routes is behind them. She'll bring in another league (the PCs) to trace the convoys back to their backer. The trail may lead somewhere politically radioactive: a Peer, a guild, or the [[Guild of Usurers|Usurers]].

> [!hook] The card
> A rumor reaches the league that the actual **Nine of Amethysts** — a minor card of [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]]'s deck — has surfaced in a smuggler's cargo. Ambush wants it, for the league's pride and for whatever power it holds. So, quietly, does the Duke's card-collection effort.
