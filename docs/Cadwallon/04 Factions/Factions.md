# Factions

The political life of Cadwallon runs on **three official pillars** and a thicket of unofficial powers.

## The Three Pillars of Power

The Duchy is built on a careful balance between three institutional powers. Each has formal standing, formal votes, and formal seats on key bodies.

### [[Houses of Peers]]

The eleven noble families granted fiefdoms by the original Charter of Vanius (835). They hold land, levy local justice in their fiefs, and supply the [[Duke Den Azhir|ducal seat]] (currently from the [[The Rampart|Orrkrk]] line via adoption). Their power is land, lineage, and military tradition.

See: [[Houses of Peers]], [[Fiefs]]

### [[Guilds]]

The commercial guilds. Eleven recognized guilds plus the **Guilds Council** that meets them all in one room. Their power is money, infrastructure, and quiet leverage across the upper and lower city.

See: [[Guilds]] for the full list and Rag'narok alliance map.

### [[Free Leagues]]

The heirs of the [[Dogs of War]] — independent adventurers who *elect* the Duke. The leagues are smaller than the guilds and less wealthy than the nobles, but they hold a formal veto on ducal succession that no other body can override. Some 300-500 active free leaguers across the city.

See: [[Free Leagues]]

## The Enforcement Arm

### [[Militia]]

The Duke's enforcement, peacekeeping, and city-defense corps. *Not* a guild. Run from the [[Fort Gentle|Militia's Club]] in Ghieronburg with garrisons in every fief. Reports to the Duke through [[Kal Rajghur|Constable Kal Rajghur]].

## The Unofficial Powers

Bodies that have no formal seat at any council but shape the city's life enormously:

### [[Other Organizations]]

- [[Circle of Explorers]] — the underground league; vestige hunters, cartographers of the deep
- [[The Tarot-Mages]] — semi-formal magical society overlapping the [[Guild of Cartomancers|Cartomancers]] and the [[Voyagers]]
- [[The Howlers]] — the outcast Wolfen kin of [[Krenald the Howler]] who have gone deeper underground than the [[The Sewers|sewers]]
- [[The Rags]] — the organized poor of [[Morgue Street]] and the [[Bog]]; not a guild, but a network

### The Shadow Powers

- [[Sophet Drahas]] and the **Court of Shadows** — Drahas's hidden "fief" in the catacrypts and his agents in the Usurers, the Thieves, and the noble houses
- The **embassies** — official seats of [[Alahan]], [[Akkylannie]], [[Syharhalna]], [[No-Dan-Kar]] (plus the secret Cynwäll ambassador [[Dyrsin]] in his tower)
- The **Bards of Destiny** — itinerant truth-tellers, intelligence brokers, and political agitators. **Argos Brisandre** is the most active in current events.

## Cross-Cutting

- [[Voyagers]] — magicians who walk the strange Realms; recruited and trained across many factions
- The [[Free University of Cadwallon]] — politically neutral, professionally entangled. Trains scribes, cartomancers, alchemists, and historians for half the guilds.
