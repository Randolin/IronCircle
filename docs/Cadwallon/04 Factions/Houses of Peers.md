# Houses of Peers

The eleven noble houses ruling Cadwallon's [[Fiefs|fiefdoms]]. Each rules a fief; one (the current Duke's house) elevates to the Ducal Enclave.

## The Math

There are **eleven fiefs** but the city talks about **ten Peers** — because the Duke is also nominally the peer of his own fief, but his work is done by a steward. So:

- 10 active Peers run their fiefs themselves
- 1 fief (currently [[Den Azhir]]) is the Ducal Enclave, run by the Duke's steward ([[Silent Scyrnyth]]) in his name

## Houses Without Fiefdoms

Many "noble" families in the [[Den Azhir|Ducal Enclave]] descend from Dogs of War lieutenants who didn't receive fiefdoms at the founding. They live in inherited mansions and Cynwäll vestige properties, often beyond their means, angling for ducal favor and hoping to be granted a peerage if one comes "free" — as Trophy may soon.

> [!hook] The peerage market
> Trophy's instability has these unfiefed houses circling. A peerage going up for reassignment hasn't happened in living memory for most of them — when it does, every old grudge surfaces.
