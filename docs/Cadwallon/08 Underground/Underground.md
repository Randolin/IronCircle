# Underground

> "The underground of Cadwallon is the hunting ground of many explorers looking for riches. The city's underground is buzzing with many important activities, with their own codes and own rules."

Beneath the [[Lower City|streets]] of Cadwallon lies a vertical history of the world: the Cadwë sewers atop the [[Cynwäll Ruins]] atop the [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]] city atop the [[Ophidians|Ophidian]] temple atop, at the very bottom, [[Desire]]'s prison and the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] that seals it. To dig in Cadwallon is to dig backward through time — and the deeper you go, the older and hungrier the things you find.

## The Three Types of Underground

The source classifies the underground into three kinds:

1. **Inhabited** — colonized sites: hideouts, meeting grounds, troglodyte communities. Quiet by default. (e.g. the [[Walled Library]], [[Glory-Hunt]], the [[Court of Ashes]])
2. **Unexplored** — the ancient ruins Cadwallon was built on. Most hazardous: ancient magical defenses, traps, wild creatures. Calm by default. (e.g. the deep [[Cynwäll Ruins]], the [[The Sewers|sewers]])
3. **Depths of the sea** — sunken treasures and ruins in the bay; largely unexplored; predators and drowning. (e.g. [[Shipwreck Bay]])

## The Vertical Layers

From the surface down:

- **The [[The Sewers|Sewers]]** — Cadwë and Cynwäll wastewater network; surface pipes in the lower city; the oasis (hidden Water Elementals); the Demijohn ([[The Rags]])
- **The [[Cynwäll Ruins]]** — the elven city Cadwallon was built on; the [[Walled Library]]; the quarries
- **The [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]] vestiges** — older still; Light-animated technology; the [[The Automaton|Automaton]]'s roots; the Tomb of the Masks
- **The [[Ophidian Vestiges]]** — the Serpent temple to [[Vortiris]]; coiling tunnels; the great ossuary; [[Ss'Alish]]'s crypt
- **The [[Stairway to Nowhere]]** — the threshold beyond time
- **The [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]** — Conscience's divine maze; the [[The Litiocs|litioc]] walls; at its heart, [[Desire]]'s prison and the [[Garden of Desire]]

## Who Goes Down There

- **Shrews** — [[Circle of Explorers|explorers]] seeking vestige-riches; pay "night rights" or work as [[Free Leagues|free leaguers]]
- **The [[Guild of Architects|Architects]]** — vestige study, catacrypt survey, the secret [[Tractor]] bore
- **The [[Guild of Sewer Workers|Sewer Workers]] and [[The Rags|Rags]]** — the shallow, working layers
- **[[The Tarot-Mages]]** — guarding the "corridors" to the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]
- **[[Sophet Drahas]]'s [[Court of Shadows]]** — dwelling in the [[Court of Ashes]]
- **The [[Ophidians]]** — [[S'ylice]] (living) and [[Ss'Alish]] (undead), surviving Serpent holdouts
- **Cynwäll spies** — watching what the shrews bring up

## The Standing Dangers

- **[[The Litiocs|Litiocs]]** — strike one and go blind for hours or years
- **Time-warping** — the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] runs on its own clock; [[Yelmor]] lost 146 years and thought it was days
- **The tide of the dead** — heavy rain floods corpses up from the necropoli; necromancers exploit the flow
- **Ancient defenses** — Sphinx traps and constructs still guard the deep ruins
- **Madness** — those who cross the Labyrinth threshold rarely return sane

> [!secret] The whole underground is one organism now
> Since **999**, [[Desire]]'s waking has begun to connect the layers. Grove-roots tunnel through the Labyrinth into the Cynwäll ruins; the litiocs surface in the upper catacrypts; warm water bleeds from outfalls under the [[Garden of Desire]]; the [[Tractor]] bore hits Sphinx barriers; the Ophidian remnants stir. The underground is no longer a stack of dead history — it is **becoming a single waking system**, and the city sits on its lid.
