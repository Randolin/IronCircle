# The Sewers

> "The sewers are often part of dark designs such as infiltrating the upper city, meeting grounds, or trapping enemies in the floodable rooms."

The working underworld of Cadwallon — the wastewater network that is also a highway, a hideout, a battlefield, and a archive. Built by the [[Cynwäll]], adopted by the [[Dogs of War]], and extended (along the surface) by the [[Guild of Architects|Architects]], the sewers are the **shallowest, busiest, most-traveled layer** of the underground. Everyone uses them. Almost no one controls them.

## Origin & Structure

When the [[Dogs of War]] settled the [[Cynwäll Ruins]], they found a network of pipes beneath the [[Upper City]] designed to carry wastewater down to the bay. They adopted it. As the [[Lower City]] grew on unstable ground, the [[Guild of Architects|Architects]] couldn't bury new pipes safely — so they **ran them along the surface.** This is why the lower city is crisscrossed with great exposed pipes, and why the poorest districts (not connected at all) dump waste in the streets.

Beneath, **great underground tanks** stock the overflow; the gates that open and close them are controlled by the [[Guild of Sewer Workers]]. Access points (manholes) are "many and very different" — guarded, locked, trapped, or disused.

## Notable Sites

### The Demijohn
Beneath [[Gamehead]] and [[Drakaër]]: a succession of decantation tanks that are the den of [[The Rags|the ragmen]]. They store and catalogue tens of thousands of salvaged items — the city's discards as a chaotic archive. Led by **Vytriol**; the **blowers** hunt vermin with toxic gas. The [[Militia]] consults the Demijohns for evidence. *(see [[The Rags]])*

### The Oasis
A network of warm, humid tunnels under [[Ghieronburg]] and [[Ogrokh]].

> [!secret] The hidden Elementals
> The oasis shelters **Elementals of Water** — the **Anfrebians** — hiding since their failed [[The Magical Invasion|invasion of 956]]. They are spies at the heart of the Free City, undiscovered for 47 years. Exposing them would spark a diplomatic crisis with the elemental Realm of Water. *(see [[The Magical Invasion]])*

### The Vegetal Entrails
Narrow tunnels of unknown origin, colonized by strange [[Magical Groves|grove]] vegetation, ending in [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian]]-marked walls. An emanation of [[Desire]] — those who reach the surface emerge in magical groves. The **strip-leaves** (controlled by [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers]] and [[Guild of Tailors|Tailors]]) grow purepale leaves for mood-changing fabric; the **Entrails of Desire** in [[Gamehead]] are where the [[Guild of Ferrymen|Ferrymen]] harvest the narcotic **elegratia**.

## What the Sewers Are Used For

- **Travel** — an "oft-used traveling way"; faster and more discreet than the choked streets
- **Infiltration** — the classic route into the walled [[Upper City]]
- **Meeting grounds** — for smugglers, conspirators, and worse (the [[Marine Ruins]] smuggler meets connect here)
- **Traps** — the floodable tanks make lethal ambush sites; close the gates and drown the room
- **Salvage & intelligence** — the [[The Rags|Rags]] mine the waste for objects and information

## The Tide of the Dead

> [!note] When the sewers turn lethal
> Heavy rain floods the underground and drives the dead to the surface — corpses from the [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian ossuaries]], the common graves, and the necropoli wash up through the sewers into the streets. This is the **tide of the dead**, the root of the Cadwë death-dread. The [[Guild of Sewer Workers|Sewer Workers]] do the grim recovery; necromancers exploit the flow to reinforce their troops. Days of heavy rain are the warning; Cadwës lock in, boil water, and wait. *(see [[Daily Life]], [[Ophidian Vestiges]])*

## Who Works & Lurks Here

- **[[Guild of Sewer Workers]]** — control the flood-gates; the official authority; know where the [[The Litiocs|litiocs]] surface and which outfalls run strange
- **[[The Rags]]** — the Demijohn ragmen and blowers
- **Smugglers** — the [[Marine Ruins]] meets and countless others
- **Water Elementals** — the oasis spies
- **Tiger-sharks and worse** — failed Dirz experiments released into the sewers, some reaching the bay

> [!secret] The warm outfall
> A junior [[Guild of Sewer Workers|Sewer Worker]] has found water flowing **warm from an outfall under the [[Garden of Desire]] district — in winter.** It connects, eventually, toward the deep layers where [[Desire]] sleeps. Warm water where there should be none is exactly the kind of tremor the [[Timeline|secret history]] warns about: Desire waking, heat and life bleeding upward through the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]'s roots. Someone in the upper guild leadership is hoping the junior worker stops reporting it.
