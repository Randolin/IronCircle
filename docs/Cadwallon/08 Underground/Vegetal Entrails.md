# Vegetal Entrails

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

Narrow tunnels of unknown origin beneath the city, colonized by strange [[Magical Groves|grove]] vegetation and ending in [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian]]-marked walls — an emanation of [[Desire]] where those who reach the surface emerge in magical groves. Source of the **strip-leaves** (purepale, for mood-fabric) and, in [[Gamehead]], the narcotic **elegratia** harvested by the [[Guild of Ferrymen]].
