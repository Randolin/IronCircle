# Glory-Hunt

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The [[Circle of Explorers]]' base camp — a great cave under the east district of [[Soma]] (with a second access from [[Gamehead]]), lit by a pale shaft thirty meters up, with a troglodyte village dug into its walls. The staging ground for deep expeditions; the militia-guarded portcullis here gates access to the foundations of the old [[Cynwäll Ruins|Cynwäll city]].
