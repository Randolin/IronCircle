# Stairway to Nowhere

> "His story tells of a flight of steps hanging over the void, which leads to a maze. He will not speak of what can be found beyond the stairs and becomes edgy when questioned about it."

A flight of steps somewhere beneath Cadwallon, **hanging over the void**, leading to the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]. A myth told among the Dark-ones of [[Acheron]]; a fragment of testimony from a dwarf who fell through time; one of the few *named* thresholds into the maze that seals [[Desire]]'s prison. To find it is to find a door into the place beyond time — which is exactly why almost no one who finds it comes back to give directions.

## The Myth

Among the Dark-ones of [[Acheron]], there is a legend: **somewhere under Cadwallon, a stairway leads to another [[Realms|Realm]].** A flight of steps hanging over nothing, climbing or descending into a maze. The Acheronians — who understand Darkness and old things — speak of it as real, and as located "beyond time."

## The Witness: Yelmor

The strongest evidence is the testimony of **[[Yelmor]]**, a dwarf warrior found wandering the catacrypts and interrogated by Captain Kelian Durak (whose report went to Constable [[Kal Rajghur]]):

- Yelmor claims he **left the city in 857**, serving in the **Steel Hearts**, one of the [[Dogs of War]]'s companies
- The ducal archives can't confirm him, but his knowledge and equipment are genuinely **of a time long gone**
- He describes **"a flight of steps hanging over the void, which leads to a maze"**
- He **will not speak of what lies beyond the stairs**, and becomes agitated when pressed
- He was found in **1003** — but is **persuaded he comes from the past** and that he wandered the maze "for no more than a few days." The revelation of the true date shocked him badly.

Yelmor's account "shares several elements with the myth of the 'stairway to nowhere.'" If he's telling the truth, the stairway is real, it leads into the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], and **it is located beyond time** — which is why Yelmor's "few days" were 146 years. *(see [[Timeline]], [[The Labyrinth]])*

## What It Means

> [!secret] A stable(ish) door into the maze
> Most intrusions into the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] happen by accident — someone crosses a threshold "often without knowing." The Stairway is different: it is a **recognized, named entrance**, described consistently across an Acheronian myth and a 146-year-displaced eyewitness. If it could be relocated, it would be **the most reliable known way into the Labyrinth** — and therefore the most reliable way toward [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]], toward [[Desire]]'s prison, and toward whatever the Open Hand [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]] want to map. It would also be the most reliable way to **lose a league forever**, since the stairway is "beyond time" and the maze swallows those who enter.

## Where It Is

Unknown. The clues:
- Yelmor was found in the **catacrypts** (the deep necropolis layers, originating under [[Ghieronburg]])
- The Acheronians know of it — suggesting a connection to the "way of the dead," Acheron's necromantic transport route into the bay
- It connects to the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], so it is near the **corridors** the [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]] guard
- It "hangs over the void" — implying a great vertical shaft or abyss, possibly near the [[Cynwäll Ruins|deep Cynwäll quarries]] or the Sphinx vestiges
