# Utopia of the Sphinx

> "The Sphinx developed a level of science so complex we are unable to grasp its basic laws."
> — A helianthe craftsman in the Old District

The vanished master-civilization whose ruins form one of the oldest layers beneath Cadwallon. Allies of the dragon [[Maloth]], enemies of the [[Ophidians]], the Sphinx were **technomancers who breathed life into machines using the power of Light**. They guarded [[Desire]]'s prison, lost their city to the Serpents, and finally vanished into the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] pursuing their enemies — leaving their wondrous, dangerous devices sleeping in the dark.

## Who They Were

The Utopia of the Sphinx was a society of extraordinary technological and magical sophistication during the **Golden Age**. Their defining art: **animating advanced technological items with conscience, using the powers of Light.** Taught by [[Maloth]] (the Dragon of Light), they built constructs, devices, and weapons of a science "so complex we are unable to grasp its basic laws."

Their alliance with [[Maloth]] was reciprocal: Maloth taught them to wield Light and animate their machines; in return they **dug the dragons' lairs and forged their armors.** When the Sphinx left to pursue the [[Ophidians]], they entrusted the future of their creations to the [[Cynwäll]] (via the helianthes and the Noesis).

## Their War and Disappearance

- A small **walled Sphinx city stood on the exact site of Cadwallon**, allied to the elves whose territory was then whole
- During the Golden Age, a **dreadful war** with the [[Ophidians]] (the Serpent people) razed the city to the ground
- Traces of that war scar the outskirts — notably the **[[Fangs of Vile-Tis]]**, a glazed plain where a Sphinx war-machine exploded to take its Ophidian attackers with it
- When the [[Ophidians]] later followed [[Vortiris]] into the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] (255), the **Sphinx pursued them in and vanished** — never to return

## What They Left Behind

The Sphinx legacy litters the Cadwallon underground and even the surface:

- **The [[The Automaton|Automaton]]** — the gigantic articulated statue, "the ultimate visible remnant still working." [[Elhan]]'s Cynwäll spirit later merged into the construct above it.
- **The faces on [[Fort Griffin]]** — Sphinx stonework on the outskirts
- **Aalshem (masks)** — ornate masks with complex functions, some hiding traps or locks; the **Tomb of the Masks** beneath [[Drakaër]] is full of them
- **Self-assembling armor** — torques that unfold into full-body protection (one helianthe craftsman owns a working example, missing two of its jewels)
- **Light-animated constructs** — sparklings, guardians, and devices still active in the deep ruins
- **The Gears Room** in the Tomb of the Masks — a chamber bound to the reflections of Cadwallon; the *departure point of the Sphinx exile*

## The Heirs: Cynwäll and Helianthes

The [[Cynwäll]] consider themselves the Sphinx's heirs, but possess only "a fraction of the knowledge." The **helianthes** — Cynwäll specialists — maintain Sphinx vestiges and study their secrets. The **Order of the Chimera** in the [[Walled Library]] and the brotherhood at the **Infinite Wisdom** (outskirts) both guard Sphinx portals and knowledge. The Sphinx art requires one "to unlearn": their power lived in the **Noesis** — the disciplined emptying of mind the Cynwäll still practice.

## Cosmological Role

> [!note] The prison-guardians
> The Sphinx were not incidental to Cadwallon — they **guarded [[Desire]]'s prison** on [[Conscience]]'s and [[Maloth]]'s side, against [[Vortiris]] and the [[Ophidians]] who came to free him. The fortress the Ophidians razed was the prison's guard-post. The Sphinx are, in the deep cosmology, the *original wardens* — and their disappearance into the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] left the post empty until the [[Dogs of War]] unknowingly inherited it. *(see [[Cosmology]], [[The Game of the Gods]])*

- The **Order of the Chimera**, the **Infinite Wisdom** brotherhood — modern guardians of their portals *(stubs pending)*

> [!hook] The Gears Room
> Deep in the Tomb of the Masks beneath [[Drakaër]], the Gears Room — locked by a frieze of complex Sphinx masks — protects a chamber bound to the *reflections of Cadwallon* and full of magical-research documents. It was the Sphinx's departure point into exile. A scholar named Baliak has sworn to open it. Whoever helps him may follow the Sphinx's road — to wherever they went.

> [!hook] The two missing jewels
> A helianthe craftsman in the [[The Old District|Old District]] has working Sphinx self-assembling armor — but it's missing two jewels, and incomplete it's only a curiosity. The complete set would be priceless and dangerous. The jewels are somewhere in the deep vestiges. He'll fund the expedition; he will *not* let the armor out of his sight.
