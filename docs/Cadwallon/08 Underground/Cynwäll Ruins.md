# Cynwäll Ruins

> "The ruins of an ancient Cynwäll city... they discovered the ruins and decided to settle there, hoping to unearth timeless artifacts left behind by the elves."

The buried elven city that Cadwallon is literally built on top of. When the [[Dogs of War]] arrived, they found the abandoned ruins of the [[Cynwäll]] [[Elhan|Elhan's]] city and settled in them. Today the [[Upper City]] sits in the old citadel; the lower city sprawls outside its walls; and the **deep ruins** below remain a hunting ground for shrews seeking elven artifacts — guarded by ancient defenses and watched by the Cynwäll themselves.

## The City That Was

[[Elhan]] and his followers, exiling themselves from the elven schism, settled the [[Lanever]] peninsula and built a stronghold at the mouth of the [[Ynkarô]] and [[Leâk Shear]]. Calling themselves *Cynwäll* ("the exiled"), they raised a city that became the great neutral trade-post of [[Aarklash]] — the one place foreign nations could walk Lanever's soil without provoking Cynwäll wrath.

The city's original name is forgotten (the Cynwäll, characteristically, refuse to say). It prospered, then fell: the necromancer **Sinlis Mantis** led an undead horde up through the old [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian tunnels]], and though the threat was repelled, the lake filled with corpses and plague emptied the city within days. The [[Cynwäll]] abandoned it. Centuries later, the [[Dogs of War]] moved in.

## What Survives Below

The Cynwäll ruins are the layer *directly beneath* modern Cadwallon — the foundation the Cadwës built on:

- **The quarries** — old Cynwäll stone-quarries, now the most densely populated slums of [[Soma]] (dwellings stacked in the cuttings) and the access points to deeper ruins
- **[[Glory-Hunt]]** — the [[Circle of Explorers]] base camp opens onto the foundations of the old Cynwäll city; "night rights" buy access past the militia portcullis
- **The [[Walled Library]]** — a vast Cynwäll archive carved from living stone, guarded by an Earth Elemental, accessed via three gates in [[The Automaton]], [[Drakaër]], and [[The Rampart]]
- **The original sewers** — the Cynwäll wastewater network the [[Dogs of War]] adopted and the [[Guild of Architects|Architects]] later extended *(see [[The Sewers]])*
- **Timeless artifacts** — elven work that "escaped the wear of time," the prize the Dogs of War came for and the shrews still hunt

## The Watchers

> [!secret] The Cynwäll still claim it
> The [[Cynwäll]] could reclaim this territory at any time — it is their ancestral city. They don't, because **they consider the land cursed** (the [[Desire]]-presence they sense but cannot name) and stay no longer than necessary. But they *watch*: [[Dyrsin]]'s tower oversees the city, and **Cynwäll spies permanently monitor the [[Circle of Explorers]]** to see what the shrews bring up from the deep ruins. Anything touching the Sphinx legacy, the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]], or the [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian]] layer gets confiscated or bought back. The Cynwäll do not want certain elven (and pre-elven) things leaving the ground. *(see [[Circle of Explorers]], [[Dyrsin]])*

> [!secret] What lies under the Cynwäll layer
> The Cynwäll built *their* city on top of the [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]] vestiges, which sit on the [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian temple]], which sits on [[Desire]]'s prison. The deepest Cynwäll shafts open into Sphinx ruins; the deepest Sphinx ruins open into coiling Ophidian tunnels; and below all of it is the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]. The Cynwäll know this. It is half of why they call the place cursed and keep their distance — and why [[Elhan]]'s spirit, merged into the construct above the [[The Automaton|Automaton]], still watches over his abandoned city. *(see [[Elhan]], [[The Automaton]])*
