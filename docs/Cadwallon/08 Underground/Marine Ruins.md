# Marine Ruins

> "The architecture of these structures is strange, difficult to identify. The alleys are covered with a slightly concave material... There's even a statue at one of the building's corners, representing some sort of reptilian divinity."

A flooded [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian]] site beneath [[Buoyancy Bay]] — the surviving fragment of [[Ss'Alish]]'s ancient community, sealed and forgotten for centuries until a recent explosion cracked it open and woke the lich sleeping at its heart. The setting of the introductory **Marine Ruins scenario**, and a self-contained gateway into the [[Ophidian Vestiges|Ophidian]] layer of the underground.

## What It Is

The Marine Ruins are all that remain of the community of [[Ss'Alish]] — an [[Ophidians|ophidian]] magician who, with his apprentices, lived in the Serpent city that stood where Cadwallon stands now. Unlike the well-picked ruins elsewhere, these were **spared**: the frescoes are intact, the strange metal fragments untouched, a reptilian-divinity statue still stands at a building's corner. The architecture is unmistakably Ophidian — concave-surfaced alleys, the coiling marks, the warm scale-tiles.

## How It Was Sealed and Reopened

- The [[Ophidians]] left in 255; [[Ss'Alish]] stayed behind and **died into undeath** by a special ritual, waiting in his crypt
- Centuries later the [[Cynwäll Ruins|Cynwäll city]] rose above; the crypt ended up below ground
- [[Cynwäll Ruins|Cynwäll]] and [[Dogs of War]] construction **partially flooded** the crypt
- When the [[The Sewers|sewers]] were built, the water receded and workers found the crypt — and prudently **bricked up the entrance.** Ss'Alish slept on.
- **Recently**, the alchemist **Methanol** caused a naphtha-machine explosion above the ruins. The tremors shattered the sealing wall and **woke Ss'Alish.** *(see [[Ss'Alish]])*

## The Layout (scenario)

The site lies beneath [[Buoyancy Bay]], reached through the [[The Sewers|sewers]]. Pitch dark, quiet, sickening, Darkness-aligned. Key areas:

1. **The sewers** — where smugglers meet (groups of five, ~500 D of illegal goods); shallow water
2. **The ruins** — the spared Ophidian structures; the reptilian statue; recognizable as a deep-city construction "always linked to dark stories and violent deaths"
3. **Ill omen** — a house knocked down not by time but by **Ss'Alish's passage**; tracks of a Large-Size snake
4. **Ruined houses** — everyday items of a Large-Size, five-fingered, non-humanoid people
5. **[[Ss'Alish]]'s tomb** — the heavy serpentine sarcophagus; the naphtha smell; the treasure; the **clawed-open lid** showing the thing inside got *out*

## The Smugglers

The pipes and conduits lead deep into the city and out to [[Kraken Harbor]], making the Marine Ruins a natural **smuggler meeting point** — outside traders swap goods for inside ducats, at night, in groups. Their silhouettes are the "strange shapes" the [[Guild of Sewer Workers|Sewer Workers]] glimpsed and fled from — half the "haunting" of the ruins is just contraband. The other half is the lich.

## The Treasure

In Ss'Alish's sarcophagus, wrapped in rotting cloth:
- **The Dark Catalyst** — an oil lamp shaped like a praying half-snake figure; grants bonus dice to Darkness incantation and Darkness-sensing; powered by a single gem of Darkness. At peak danger it "goes crazy," forcing a FEAR check on everyone present.
- **Two superior Tulwars** — enormous two-handed scimitars that escaped the wear of time (Ss'Alish wields these)

## What It Connects To

The Marine Ruins are a **shallow, accessible doorway into the [[Ophidian Vestiges]]** — the broader Serpent layer of the underground. A league that clears Ss'Alish hasn't reached the bottom of anything; they've opened a thread that runs down through the coiling tunnels toward the great ossuary, the Vortiris temple, and (far below) the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]. It also ties to [[S'ylice]], the *living* ophidian under the [[Walled Library]] — two Serpent holdouts stirring as [[Desire]] wakes.
