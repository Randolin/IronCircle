# Southaven

> *"To be down south: to be extremely poor."* — Trophy slang

Once an esplanade for visiting artists, built by [[Bran Standing-Wind]] with ambition for culture. Now a gruesome shanty town overrun by Rag'Narok war refugees, and firmly in the grip of a Drune faction operating under the direction of **[[The Claw]]**. Their violence is coordinated and expanding — the pressure is already being felt in neighboring districts. **Dominant attitude: Pugnacity.**

**Day:** Crowded, desperate, and dangerous. Refugees pack improvised shelters; Drune enforcers move openly; the nearby warehouse blocks have emptied.
**Night:** Worse. Fires burn in barrels; the Drune are active; rumors of ritual killing multiply.

## Locations

- **The Shanty Town** — the district itself: layer on layer of improvised dwellings, each occupied by people with no better option. The [[Drune]] tax what they can; those who resist disappear.
- **The Warehouse District** *(Opportunism / Weapon Bearers)* — once animated, now a no man's land emptied by Southaven's proximity. Occupied at street level by a group of orphans under a young goblin named **[[Nitrite]]**, along with various wretches and smugglers. Stolen goods are fenced here nightly before surfacing at the garden market in [[Little Klûne]] by morning.
- **The Temple of Darkness** *(rumored)* — the Drune are said to be sacrificing to dark gods somewhere in the district. This rumor is treated with skepticism by those who know the Kelts well — the [[Drune]] abhor gods of any kind. What they are actually doing in whatever building they've claimed as a center is, as yet, unknown.

## NPCs

- [[The Claw]] — Drune warlord holding the district; the coordinating force behind its violence
- [[Nitrite]] — young goblin leader of the orphan gang in the warehouses; resourceful and careful

## Hooks

> [!hook] The Claw's Expansion
> Incidents are spilling over into [[Comedy Garden]] and [[Little Klûne]]. A merchant consortium is quietly funding anyone willing to identify The Claw's operations, map the Drune positions, and bring back something that can be used against them before the [[Trophy]] Peer is forced to act publicly — and badly.

> [!hook] What the Orphans Know
> [[Nitrite]]'s gang has been moving goods through the warehouses for months and has seen things the Drune don't know they saw. Getting that information means earning their trust — and protecting them from what the Drune will do if they find out the orphans have been watching.

> [!hook] The Real Ritual
> A [[Diviner]] insists the rumors of sacrifice are wrong — but something is happening in the deep structure of one of the old esplanade buildings. Not a dark god. Something older and stranger than the Drune know how to name. It's been getting louder.
