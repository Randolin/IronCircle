# Districts

The neighborhoods of Cadwallon, organized by fief. The PHB map keys **44 districts** across the eleven fiefs, plus a handful of edge-features beyond the walls. Every district below has its own note (most are stubs — populated from the fief notes; deeper detail to be pulled from the PHB district gazetteer as the campaign engages with them).

District notes live under `02 City/Districts/<Fief>/`. Edge-features and harbor zones live under `02 City/` directly.

> [!note] How to use this
> The **fief** each district belongs to is the authoritative parent; see the fief note for political context. When a session is about to touch a district, expand its note from the PHB district entry.
