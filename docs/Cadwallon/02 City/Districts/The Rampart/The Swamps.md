# The Swamps

> *"Better alive and standing than kneeling and dead!"* — Orrkrk family motto

The waterlogged southern district of [[The Rampart]], named for the many ponds and waterholes fed by rain and dirty runoff. **Repulsive smell, disease-ridden vermin, the wretched of the wretched.** The militia raids periodically to remove the terminally ill before epidemics start. **Dominant attitude: Pugnacity / Opportunism.** A district of [[The Rampart]] in the [[Lower City]].

## Atmosphere

- **By day:** deafening — cannonballs from [[Ogrokh]]'s **Shooting Range** pound the great wall, and chunks of stone routinely fall into the Swamps from the [[Ogrokh]] side
- **By night:** strangely beautiful. The noise stops; **the stars reflect in the pools like a thousand mirrors**

## Landmarks

- **Lil'cad** — a former tomb-raider encampment turned outlaw lair, home to **about a hundred** illegal independent shrews evading the [[Guild of Thieves]]' taxes. Run by **the Archduke** (an underground figure of contested authority). [[Camelia Orrkrk|Camelia's]] renovation plan to fill in the Swamps must go through Lil'cad — **Camelia is rumored to have met with the Archduke** about this, though no witnesses can be found. *(Discipline / Archduke)*

> [!hook] The Archduke's compromise
> Camelia Orrkrk wants Lil'cad cleared so she can fill in the Swamps and revitalize her fief. The Archduke holds a hundred illegal shrews she'd need to displace. **Their negotiation is ongoing**, hidden from the [[Duke Den Azhir|Duke]], the [[Guild of Thieves]], and Camelia's own Orrkrk rivals. A free league brought into either side of this conversation lands in the middle of a deal that could go several ways.

> [!hook] Cannonball fallout
> Stone blocks falling from [[Ogrokh]]'s shooting range have killed Swamps residents for decades. **A goblin sweep-team** (paid by the Orrkrks) collects the iron from the cannonballs for resale. Recently, the team has been recovering **shaped fragments that aren't from Ogrokh ammunition** — older, stranger, deeper. The Swamps' waterholes may go deeper than anyone thought.
