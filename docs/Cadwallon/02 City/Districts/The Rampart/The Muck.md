# The Muck

> *"Better alive and standing than kneeling and dead!"* — Orrkrk family motto

The largest district of [[The Rampart]] — so much of the fief, in fact, that **most Cadwës confuse the Muck with the Rampart itself**. Mucky streets, unfit houses, a population surviving by unrewarding labor and begging during the day; deadly silence by night, broken occasionally by shrieks of despair. **Dominant attitude: Pugnacity.** A district of [[The Rampart]] in the [[Lower City]].

## Landmarks

- **The Tavern** — formerly the **Kennel**, original meeting place of the [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]]. A 903 blaze (suspected revenge by the disgraced Merul family, never proven) left only the blackened front. Rebuilt with a Wolfen-height door, the first-floor knocked out for large patrons, and **permanently heated** — the only such building in the district. Owner: **Lady Allya**, a female [[Wolfen]] respected and feared in equal measure. **The best information source in the lower city.** *(Subtlety / Neutral)*
- **The Rotten Luck** — a cursed mossy patch in the street where **Rodrigue "Rotten Luck" Merul** landed when he leapt from the rampart after gambling the fief away in 902. Passers-by walk around it; some spit on it. *(Opportunism / Neutral)*
- **The Sitting Square** — an old market square turned into a building of a hundred benches, where roughly **500 of the district's poorest sleep tied upright** so they don't fall. A ducat buys a bench, militia security, and a loaf of bread. Members of **[[The Rags]]** often meet here. *(Opportunism / The Rags)*
- **The Leprosy** — a large old building sheltering the mentally handicapped, lepers, and consumption sufferers abandoned by their families. Underfunded; **the doctors sell bodies to Mid-Nor collectors.** *(Style / Mid-Nor)*
- **The Khelekera** (Repentants' House) — the district's only place of worship. An Orrkrk property, run by **Synaïya the Convert**, an [[Akkyshan]] elf. Officially open to all but the Akkyshan presence spooks most Cadwës; Synaïya is nonetheless appreciated for the **free medical care** she provides daily. *(Subtlety / Light)*
- **The Temple of Pleasure** — Camelia Orrkrk's recently-renovated brothel. All tastes satisfied; can host [[Wolfen]]; some employees are [[Daïkinee]]. The Akkyshan **Ienâ** wanders outside looking for prey or lover. *(Style / Neutral)*
