# The Pillars

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The founding district of [[Drakaër]] — named for the feet of the Cynwäll Tower. Home to **Dyrsin's Tower** (the unofficial Cynwäll ambassador's residence, with a dragon landing area on one of the pillars), **Fort Drakaër** (the family seat), the sky station (the [[Guild of Ferrymen|Ferrymen]]'s dwarf/Cynwäll-built airship platform), the garrison and prison, the **Perfect Tower** ([[Guild of Architects]] HQ), and the **Gilded Tower** ([[Guild of Goldsmiths]] luxury mall and hotel). A district of [[Drakaër]] in the [[Upper City]].
