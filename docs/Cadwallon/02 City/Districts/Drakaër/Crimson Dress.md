# Crimson Dress

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The wild edge of [[Drakaër]] — Mardi-Gras-by-night, drunkards-by-day; brothels, gambling, the **Dragon's Pool** bathhouse, and the discreet brothel "Under The Dress." The only part of Drakaër that stays alive after dark, and a standing insult to the rest of the fief's righteousness. A district of [[Drakaër]] in the [[Upper City]].
