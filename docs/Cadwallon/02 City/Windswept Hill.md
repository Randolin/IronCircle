# Windswept Hill

> A lonely rise where the outcast Wolfen keep their crumbling pack house.

A lonely rise on the [[The Outskirts|outskirts]], between the Nomadic Lands and [[Ghieronburg]], where the outcast Wolfen of [[The Howlers]] keep their crumbling pack house. Its mills sit on ley lines where Rebirth-magic runs unusually strong, drawing [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mage]] experiments. *(see [[Mana & Magic]])*
