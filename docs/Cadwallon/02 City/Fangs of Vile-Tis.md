# Fangs of Vile-Tis

> A glazed, fused plain — the scar of a Sphinx war-machine's last stand.

A glazed, fused plain on the [[The Outskirts|outskirts]] — the scar where a [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]] war-machine exploded during the Golden Age to take its [[Ophidians|Ophidian]] attackers with it. Named for the dark Wolfen devourer [[Vile-Tis]]; its melted ground still bears Sphinx traces.
