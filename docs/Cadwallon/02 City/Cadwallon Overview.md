# Cadwallon Overview

> "Cadwallon. The name itself embodies the majesty of the free city state. Outside there is only a world thrown off-balance by the Rag'narok, slowly slipping towards total war."
> — Vladar the Arrogant

A free city of roughly 300,000 on a peninsula in **Lanever**, neutral in [[The Rag'narok|the Rag'narok]] and prosperous on the bones of an older civilization. Two-thirds of its inhabitants are native **Cadwës**; the rest are foreigners — refugees, diplomats, merchants, scholars, and worse — drawn from every nation on [[Aarklash]].

## Geography

The city sprawls across a peninsula between two seas. Three waters define its access:

- **Kraken Harbor** — to the north, the continent's biggest merchant port. Squadron of goblin pirates ensures "protection."
- **Buoyancy Bay** — to the east, defended by an Akkylannian fort on a small island. The Ways of Light's preferred approach.
- **The Port of Ondine** — to the west, small and rich. Nobles and diplomats land here.

A great lake — the **Little Sea** — sits at the heart of the upper city.

## A Double City

The city has two faces, walled apart and economically inseparable:

- **[[Upper City]]** — walled citadel built atop the ruins of an ancient Cynwäll city. Aristocrats, embassies, guild halls, the Ducal Palace. Cynwäll towers reach into the sky; Lanever dragons glide past to visit their titanic kin in the Dyrsin Tower.
- **[[Lower City]]** — the sprawl outside the walls. Where most Cadwës live, work, and die. Crowded, cheap, vibrant, dangerous.

## Power

Three forces share the city, in uneasy balance:

- **The Duchy** — currently Duke [[Duke Den Azhir|Den Azhir]], who lays down decrees. The **ten Peers** administer the eleven [[Fiefs|fiefdoms]] in his name (the Duke's own fief is run by his steward).
- **The Guilds** — trade and craft guilds controlling commerce, infrastructure, and most of the city's day-to-day work. See [[Factions]].
- **The [[Free Leagues]]** — heirs of the [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]]. Adventurers, mercenaries, treasure hunters. The political force that elects the Duke and protects the city's neutrality.

Then there are the **embassies** — four official ([[Alahan]], [[Akkylannie]], [[Syharhalna]], [[No-Dan-Kar]]) and a secretive Cynwäll ambassador in his tower. Every nation on Aarklash has unofficial representation somewhere.

## Daily Life

- **Motto:** *"My kingdom for a ducat!"*
- **Currency:** the **ducat**, originally goblin coinage, now the trade standard across Aarklash.
- **Language:** **Cadwë** — a creole of mercenary slang, Akkylannian grammar, and loanwords from every nation that has business in the city. The upper city speaks a more formal register seasoned with Cynwäll terms.
- **The Tide of the Dead** — periodic supernatural event when corpses surface in the lake and water sources turn fetid. Cadwës board up and stockpile clean water until it passes.
- **Magical Groves** — small jungles that sprout spontaneously anywhere in the city. Destroying one is a crime.
