# The Outskirts

> The surface lands beyond the walls — fields, smuggling shores, and old ruins.

The surface lands beyond Cadwallon's walls — fields, smuggling shores, and old ruins. Includes **Darkshore** and **Bloody Creek** (site of the [[The Akkyshan Offensive|983]] massacre and the Akkyshan witches' ongoing ritual), the [[The Watching Tower]], the [[Fangs of Vile-Tis]], [[Fort Griffin]], and [[Windswept Hill]].
