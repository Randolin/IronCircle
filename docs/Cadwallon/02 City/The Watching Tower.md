# The Watching Tower

> A Cynwäll watchtower guarding the landward road to Lanever.

A [[Cynwäll]] watchtower on the [[The Outskirts|outskirts]], guarding the landward road between Cadwallon and the [[Lanever]] interior. One of the surviving elven structures overseeing the approaches to the Free City.
