# Immobilis

> A grove-grown archipelago, every island magical — the Birders' domain.

An archipelago off Cadwallon, every island grove-grown and magical — the domain of the [[Guild of Birders]] (founded here in 992), who manage its fauna and the nearby **Sea of Roots**. A dense concentration of [[Magical Groves|grove]] activity and thus a barometer of [[Desire]]'s stirring; a source of exotic (and smuggled) cargo.
