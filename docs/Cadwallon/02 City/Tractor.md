# Tractor

> A civic-engineering marvel — and the cover for a secret bore toward the Labyrinth.

An ambitious [[Guild of Architects|Architect]] civic-engineering project begun in **999** — and the cover for a **secret deep-bore** into the underground. The bore has hit [[The Litiocs|litioc]] (blinding a surveyor), suggesting it is approaching [[The Labyrinth]]. Tied to the Architects' suspicion that the [[The Great Flood|Great Flood]] was a symptom of [[Desire]] stirring.
