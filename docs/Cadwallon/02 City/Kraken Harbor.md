# Kraken Harbor

> Cadwallon's great northern port — tentacles of dock reaching into the sea.

Cadwallon's principal northern port — the tentacle-shaped harbors of the [[Kraken]] fief reaching into the sea, where goblin exiles first settled in **255** (the earliest Cadwë date). The working harbor managed by the [[Guild of Ferrymen]] and watched by goblin Harbor Office officials.
