# Shipwreck Bay

> The treacherous eastern approach — wreck-strewn and fort-guarded.

The eastern sea approach to Cadwallon — treacherous, wreck-strewn waters guarded by the Akkylannian island fort of [[Fort Griffin]]. One of the city's harbor approaches; the "depths of the sea" and the way of the dead. (Closely associated with the adjacent, wreck-littered [[Buoyancy Bay]].)
