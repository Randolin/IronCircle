# City Homebrew

Our table's additions, replacements, and overrides for Cadwallon — its fiefs, districts, customs, and laws. **Authoritative over source material.** When canon and this file conflict, this file wins.

## Additions

### Cynwäll Spires (unified mental image)
Our table treats the Cynwäll towers across the [[Upper City]] as one interconnected hollow root-ball structure with twisting spire branches reaching skyward. Each upper-city fief has its own "root" entrance, guarded by its noble house.

*(Source canon has separate towers — Dyrsin Tower, the pillars over [[Drakaër]], etc. The unified-structure framing is a simplification for our table's mental geography. Lanever dragons gliding through the city skies and their "titanic kin" inside Dyrsin Tower are themselves canon — only the spires-as-one-thing framing is ours.)*

## Overrides

### Weapon-carrier law (dropped)
Source canon requires citizens to hire a weapon-bearer to follow them around carrying their sword. Our table drops this — too much friction at the table for marginal flavor.
