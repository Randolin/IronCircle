# Gamehead

> Half city, half wild. Wolfen-ruled. The old alpha is cloistered; his deputy holds the line.

## Canon Facts
- **Peer: [[Krenald the Howler]]**, repentant Wolfen, nominated Peer in 963.
- **Acting deputy: [[Hadrax]]** — runs day-to-day affairs while Krenald is cloistered.
- Krenald's last mate is [[Kyx]], an exiled vestal.
- Two factions within the Howlers: Hadrax's traditionalists vs. a "contentious group" whose nature is for our table to define.
- The fief's name comes from **the Gamehead** itself — the giant skull of an ancient beast defeated by Krenald's pack, mounted atop a ziggurat in the Bog.

## Districts
- **West**: cattle pastures, faerie groves, ancient standing stone circles
- **East**: the Skinner (slaughterhouse and grim industry), the Pit of Claws arena, the weapons-market arenas
- **The Bog**: swamp with ruins, including the Gamehead ziggurat

> [!hook] Krenald's death
> The succession is unresolved. When Krenald goes, the two factions clash, and the city may have to intervene. The other Peers will not look kindly on a Wolfen civil war inside the walls.
