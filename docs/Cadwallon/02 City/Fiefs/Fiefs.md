# Fiefs

> "After declaring the independence of Cadwallon, Vanius split up the city in separate fiefdoms, which he entrusted to his lieutenants."

Cadwallon comprises **eleven fiefdoms** — eight in the [[Lower City]], three in the [[Upper City]]. Each is administered by a **Peer**, a vassal of the Duke who handles taxation, militia provisioning, and aristocratic justice within their fief. The Duke's own fief (currently [[Den Azhir]]) is administered by a steward in the Duke's name, which is why the city talks about "the ten Peers" even though there are eleven fiefs.

Fief names often double as the ruling house's name. When a Duke is elected from a house, that house's fief becomes the **Ducal Enclave** — the seat of city government. Right now that's Den Azhir.

## Lower City (8)

- [[Soma]] — Peer **Vanho Soma**. The bustling heart of downtown. Sailors meet merchants meet pickpockets by day; gambling, drinks, and forbidden pleasures by night.
- [[The Automaton]] — Peers **Xär-Lilith** and **Yalin Aoue**. Built in old Cynwäll quarries around a colossal gilded construct whose face turns with the hours with a clang heard across the district.
- [[The Rampart]] — Peer **Camelia Orrkrk** (Den Azhir's adoptive mother). A claustrophobic stack of brick and poverty, named for the ruined great wall overlooking Ogrokh.
- [[Kraken]] — Peer **Bismuth von Kraken of Odazzur**. Cadwallon's port fief — land reaching into the sea like tentacles, the upside-down tower of The Krak guarding the family's secrets underground.
- [[Ogrokh]] — Peer **Kurn Khaurik Argam**. Built at a scale for big creatures. Ogres, dwarven thermo-forges, the Guild of Blades, the Akkylannian embassy, the city's militant heart.
- [[Trophy]] — Peer **Gwan Standing-wind**. Cadwallon's breadbasket. Fields, orchards, silos, and irrigation canals that feed the rest of the city. The Standing-winds are losing ground to creditors.
- [[Gamehead]] — Peer **Krenald the Howler**, with **Hadrax** as acting deputy. Half city, half countryside. Wolfen rule, cattle pastures and faerie groves to the west, the Skinner's grim industry and arenas to the east.
- [[Ghieronburg]] — Peer **Bernadil Ghiéron**. A rural fief dotted with old ruins.

## Upper City (3)

- [[Den Azhir]] — the **Ducal Enclave**. Duke **Den Azhir** himself; opulent mansions, the embassies district, the Marina, the Ducal Palace, airships coming and going.
- [[Drakaër]] — Peer **Albanne Drakaër**. The aristocrats' fief. Dyrsin Tower's shadow, the Free University, the Field of Honor, the Crimson Dress district where decorum goes to die after dark.
- [[Var-Nokkt]] — Peer **Torguir IV Var-Nokkt**. The merchant fief. The Great Market at Ka-Ân-Vor, the Dressmakers' District, mansions built across suspended bridges as the rich run out of ground to build on.
