# Kraken

> Cadwallon's port fief. Tentacles of land reaching into the sea, and an upside-down tower full of family secrets.

## Canon Facts
- **Peer: [[Bismuth von Kraken of Odazzur]]**, head of House Bismuth.
- Named for the tentacle-shape of its harbors reaching into the water.
- **The Krak** — the squat ancient round building, House Bismuth's family seat — runs deep underground. Their secrets are kept there.
- **Buoyancy Bay** (to the east) is littered with shipwrecks. Treasure-seekers and salvagers brave its waters.

## Districts
- **The Docks** — typical port operations, watched by goblin Harbor Office officials and Ferrymen's enforcers
- **The Krak** — the upside-down tower, Bismuth family seat
- **Nomadic Lands** — tent city and shanty town on a hill; refugees, criminals, escapees; guild recruitment offices troll for talent and marks
- **Buoyancy Bay** — wreck-strewn waters
