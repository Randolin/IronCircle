# The Rampart

> A claustrophobic stack of brick and poverty. Ruled by the woman who adopted the Duke.

## Canon Facts
- **Peer: [[Camelia Orrkrk]]**, matriarch of House Orrkrk and adoptive mother of [[Duke Den Azhir]].
- Named for the ruin of a great wall overlooking [[Ogrokh]].
- Densely packed, very poor, mostly quiet at night.
- House Orrkrk maintains a secondary villa on the Bridges in [[Var-Nokkt]] — keeping a foothold among the upper-city merchants.

> [!hook] The hidden lever
> Camelia's adoption of the future Duke gives her significant indirect leverage. The terms of the adoption are a city-wide secret. If they surface, Rampart's politics change overnight.
