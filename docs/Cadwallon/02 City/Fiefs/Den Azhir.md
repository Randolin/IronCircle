# Den Azhir

> The Ducal Enclave. Where the Duke lives, where the embassies cluster, where airships dock.

## Canon Facts
- The **Ducal Enclave** — by tradition, the current Duke's home fief is renamed after him and serves as the seat of city government.
- **Peer: Duke [[Duke Den Azhir]]** himself; administered day-to-day by Steward [[Silent Scyrnyth]].
- [[Vanius the Founder]] originally held this fief. He died heirless and donated it to the ducal office, establishing the rolling-name tradition.
- Annexed an island after the [[The Great Flood|Great Flood of 900]]; in 950 a Duke built a summer residence there, breaking with tradition.

## Districts
- **The Core** — most luxurious district, opulent mansions
- **The Embassies** — gated district with the four official embassies, ringed by guild of Goldsmiths troops
- **The Marina** — lake, summer estates, market for magic gems on a rocky island
- **Heatons** — largest lake island, the Ducal Summer Palace
- **The North Gate** — main road from lower city; the prison; one of the Free Leagues' main HQ buildings; the "Alliance" monument (a gift of Lanever at the city's founding)

## Power & Politics
- Many "noble" families without peerages live here — descendants of Dogs of War who didn't get a fief at the founding.
- They live beyond their means, angling for ducal favor and hoping for an office or a peerage if one comes "free" (as [[Trophy]] may soon).
- The Duke rules through his "faithful companions" rather than blood family: [[Kal Rajghur]], [[Silent Scyrnyth]], and others from his [[Ruby-Heart]] days.
