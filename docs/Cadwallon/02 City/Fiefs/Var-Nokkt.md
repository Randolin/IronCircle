# Var-Nokkt

> A dwarven merchant family that runs itself like a guild. Tendrils in everyone's pockets. Castel hanging off a Cynwäll pillar, only reachable by airship.

**Vibe:** Architecture and urban planning are pure Ægis Mountains — wide streets rarely winding, imposing buildings that look squat. Less educated than [[Drakaër]], less precious than the Ducal Enclave. The fief supports talent and money-making. Many shop signs help visitors track their location. By night, inhabitants lock themselves up fearing burglary; the fief takes on a sinister cast. Night-birds are not welcome. Dominant Attitude: **Sleight**.

## Canon Facts

- **Peer: [[Torguir IV Var-Nokkt]]** — descendant of Duke Koliandre. The family is dwarven, ~20 strong, run like a guild with a Council of Elders.
- **Founder: Rækar Nokkt** — one of Vanius's lieutenants. The family has been prosperous since founding, partly by **shamelessly exploiting Tir-Nâ-Bor dwarf refugees** — whom the Var-Nokkts consider "frustrated barbarians born under the Ægis and destined to live under the upper city."

## The Var-Nokkt Family

The family is **run like a guild**. Torguir IV is the Peer; he and his closest advisors form the **Council of Elders**.

- **Torguir IV Var-Nokkt** — Peer; overall direction
- **Hildra** — Torguir's wife; supervises judiciary activities of the fief
- **Arkan** — Torguir's brother; was in charge of family treasure; **mysteriously disappeared after a scandal linking his name to a network of corruption**
- **Torguir V** — heir; internal affairs, relations with the duchy, guilds, and leagues
- **Lyfha** — daughter; relations with the embassies
- **Ryankha** — daughter; married to the first son of the **Senex** (dwarf king of the Ægis); guarantees relations with [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]]. Mother of **Ardhun**, the young dwarf sent from Tir-Nâ-Bor to learn about the city
- **Krondnir** — Arkan's son; leads the family's troops under Torguir V's authority

## Districts
- [[The Dressmakers District]] — Cadwë dressmakers across several tower floors; Daïkinee insects and Syhar mutagenic science feed their originality; the **Green Terrace** (vegetal-garment garden tended by orc gardeners, notably Arkabast); the **Belvedere** (Dressmakers' guild seat); the **Glistenning** (delight house and Guild of Favorites' discreet seat); **Castel Var-Nokkt** clinging to a Cynwäll pillar, accessible only by air via family balloons and the airship **Senex III**
- [[Ka-Ân-Vor]] — the **Great Market**, the merchant heart of the upper city; the Rækar Gate connecting to [[Trophy]]; closed craft streets (Smithy, Jewelry, Alchemy, Goldsmith, Lapidary); the temple of **Uren** (dwarven god); the Var-Nokkt-run barracks
- [[The Shades]] — flood-prone district below the dressmakers; the Grand Canal of Shû-Az; the Sewer Workers' HQ; the Circle of Explorers' HQ; underground-vestige access points; private cellar concessions (rented out for digs)
- [[The Bridges]] — wealthy mansions on suspended bridges along the Cynwäll spire; gardens irrigated by a dwarven pipe system; the **old Tir-Nâ-Bor embassy** (currently maintained by Senex emissaries pending a new ambassador); temple of **Caradoc** (dwarven patron of honest craftsmen); the Orrkrk villa

## Power & Politics

- The family runs **interweaving relations within the guilds (notably the Goldsmiths) through a skillful retribution system** — they permanently support the guilds' councils financially.
- Ryankha's marriage gives them strong Tir-Nâ-Bor connections, often used to broker transactions between the Ægis and the Architects or Alchemists.
- The family irritates everyone else. **[[Drakaër]] is openly critical**; only **[[The Rampart|Orrkrk]] mediation has prevented incidents.**
- A local Goldsmith chapter master, **Ôdril Haarken**, has been publicly questioning the Var-Nokkts' grip — and wondering aloud what secrets the **Usurers** know about Var-Nokkt practices that keep the Usurers compliant.
