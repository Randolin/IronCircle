# Ghieronburg

> A rural fief built on cursed ground that turned out to be the most magically active terrain in the city. The Ghiérons punch above their weight — and above their station — to prove they belong.

**Vibe:** Built on enchanted ruins. Two rivers (the Asp and the Adder) run through, yet the fief suffers a strangely dry microclimate. Long lying fallow because only fayrees, Elementals, and "slogres" (the slow apathetic ogre cousins of Ogrokh) lived there. Now urbanized but still rural at the edges. Day work, night unsavory. Dominant Attitude: **Opportunism**.

## Canon Facts

- **Peer: [[Bernadil Ghiéron]]** — eldest grandson of Commander Denerac. Charismatic, ~30 years old.
- **Founder: Commander Denerac Ghiéron** — ennobled in **956** at age 58 for crucial role against the Anfrebian (water elemental) invasion. Land was thought sterile and worthless when given. Died in 984.
- **Population uniquely includes "Others: Fayrees, Elementals and magical Familiars..."** — the fief is genuinely magical.
- 956 — Fief founded
- 958 — Villa Denerac built
- 961 — Catacrypts developed
- 962 — Mysterious **Baron Mardi** appears, introduces **his Truce**, regulating the magical creatures of the fief
- 966 — Tamarez Canal scandal (Goldsmiths-orchestrated humiliation)
- 973–987 — Shaproa project launched, aborted
- 1002 — current year context, recent events

## The Ghiéron Family

- All human. Descendants of Commander Denerac. Lacking culture and elegance; their attempts at "coup d'éclat" usually come off clumsy and tacky.
- A serious **complex of inferiority** masks great ambition and a cunning mind.
- Each Ghiéron has relative independence in their domain:
  - **Bernadil** — Peer, central authority
  - **Gowayn** — finance
  - **Isabess** (Gowayn's wife) — relations with noble houses
  - **Martolbe** — relations with guilds and leagues
  - **Thismee** and **Ombeline** — cousins, receptive to magical phenomena; manage relations with Sihirs and Voyagers
  - **Adalban (or "Adaldan")** — military expert, the last of Denerac's living children. Runs Camp Valiant on Wyvern Road. **Long-running personal hatred with Silnus Drakaër.**

## Districts
- [[Parchedale]] — village-like, Villa Denerac, **Scales Square** (pre-Cadwallon Ophidian scale-tiles cause the dry microclimate), the gossip-rich Great Wash House, catacrypts entrances
- [[Petropolis]] — supernatural enclosure of petrified plants; orc gangs, magical phenomena, the **basiliege** (oak or statue that gives "unfailing fortitude" to those who sleep under it), the **Fairslime** goblin farm, **Cal Ka Dum** orc worship grove
- [[Arcadia]] — magical creatures' district under Baron Mardi's Truce
- [[Shaproa]] — the failed/aborted 973 project
- [[Wyvern Road]] — military road, Camp Valiant (Adalban Ghiéron's elite training ground for "Wyvern" soldiers, also the prison), Fort Gentle (Militia's Club HQ)

## Power & Politics

- The Ghiérons are **dynamic and demagogue** with a wide popular following — they stay in contact with subjects.
- Vulnerable to flattery, charlatans, and crooked guild investment schemes that exploit their inferiority complex.
- They are **looked down on** by older noble houses. The Tamarez canal scandal made them so vindictive even the most skillful speculators got tainted.
- **Baron Mardi** — mysterious figure who appeared in 962. Wicked pleasure in humiliating the Sihirs and Voyagers Thismee and Ombeline. His Truce regulates magical creatures of the fief; what he wants in exchange is unclear.
