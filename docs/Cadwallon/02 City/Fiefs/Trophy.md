# Trophy

> Cadwallon's breadbasket. A peerage in slow collapse.

## Canon Facts
- **Peer: [[Gwan Standing-wind]]**, of House Standing-wind.
- The Standing-winds gained the fief through a contest — **Bran Standing-wind**, a Sessair Kelt, won it by organizing a triumph.
- The family is in serious decline. They have sold off most of the fief's farmland to creditors — ogres and human farmers now own roughly three-quarters of the original holdings.
- **Other Peers consider the Standing-winds barely legitimate.** They are isolated, ignored, and possibly soon to lose the fief entirely.
- The Trophy peerage has been "freed" (re-assigned) in the past — there's precedent for stripping a Peer who can't hold their fief.

## Districts
- The Fields — agricultural belt, smugglers' road by night
- Little Klûne — market garden, shops
- Comedy Garden — interconnected taverns and artist mansions
- Southhaven — refugee shantytown
- The Square — small fortress district

> [!hook] The vultures
> Several houses without peerages — especially Den Azhir-adjacent families in the ducal enclave — are angling to inherit Trophy if the Standing-winds collapse. Open competition could become open war.
