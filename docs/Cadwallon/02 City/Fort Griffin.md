# Fort Griffin

> The Akkylannian island fort where disgraced militiamen work off their sins.

The Akkylannian island fort guarding [[Shipwreck Bay]] — roughly 300 men, many posted there as punishment, home to the **legion of repentance** (disgraced [[Militia|militiamen]] working off their offenses) under Commander Velion. Its stonework bears [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]]-era faces.
