# Wall of Earth

> The cliff where Vanius made his founding stand — now the leaguers' shrine.

The three-kilometre rocky cliff on the city's [[The Outskirts|outskirts]] where Vanius made his founding stand at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]]. Today a historical site, frequented by tourists and by [[Free Leagues|free leaguers]], who keep their [[Great Hall]] here. The falls below are where Cadwë funeral ashes are scattered.
