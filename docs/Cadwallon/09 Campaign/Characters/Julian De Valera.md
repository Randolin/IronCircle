> "Another insult, another dawn appointment. I never seem to run out of either."

**Concept:** A peerless young duelist who has no idea he is the true-born heir to a crown — treated as a commoner, challenged like one, and quietly hunted by assassins dressed as offended gentlemen.

## Background
Julian is the son of a common girl and a crown prince who married her in secret — proof of the union lost in a papal archive to the ineptitude of a drunken monk. The King forced the prince to remarry a rival kingdom's princess to secure a border. Only the king, the prince, and the princess know Julian's true lineage; his mother kept her silence in exchange for a small stipend to raise, train, and educate her son.

The princess, fearing for her own children's position, has sent a long parade of assassins. Murder in the street draws attention — but **a duel?** People die every day for slights of honor. Fortunately, Julian was trained well, and killed his first man at ten. Each death haunts him; he drinks to blur the faces that follow him. A young man who, for some reason, seems to offend so very many people. **The true first-born son of the prince — and he knows nothing of it.**

## Drives
**Wants:** to be left alone long enough to figure out who he is.
**Bond:** his mother, and the few who treat him as a person rather than a problem.
**Flaw:** drink, and a sword arm that answers every insult before his judgment can.

## Connections

The same offended nobles who challenge the apparent-commoner Julian to a dawn duel won't dare challenge the openly-noble Longshadow, however much he deserves it. *("Does your mother know you are so unrestrained?")* They will absolutely keep bumping into each other.
- [[Half Moon]] — a Seraph detective is exactly the sort who could tell a *real* duel of honor from a contract killing dressed as one — and perhaps glimpse Julian's hidden blood.
