> "The master would not be pleased!"

**Concept:** A genuine imp of the Nine Hells — once an archmage's familiar, experimented upon, now loose in Cadwallon — **crackling and humming with wild magic** and passing himself off as a harmless familiar to dodge servitude and deportation back to Dis.

## Background
Summoned centuries ago as familiar to the archmage **[[Vorr Kraxxus Drakaër]]** — "Vorr Blackcloak," of the wealthy and powerful House [[Drakaër]]. Vorr hungered for forbidden magics and **experimented on Nog**; to this day he **crackles and hums with wild magic**, and it's not unusual for nearby objects to fly across the room, vanish, or explode. Blackcloak used Nog to steal arcane secrets, spy on and sabotage rival wizards — and to keep his study immaculate.

Nog *cannot abide* untidiness. He mutters "the master would not be pleased!" and frantically sets things in order. He produces a small metal cube he puzzle-fidgets and holds whispered conversations with, glancing over his shoulder to be sure no one sees his moves. He sleeps in fireplaces, hung upside-down by his tail, wings wrapped around him like a soot-stained pod — or in the pre-soup boiling pot, to relax. The little devil savors his freedom, and genuinely treasures his new friends — it helps enormously that [[Longshadow]] actively *wants* to do naughtiness.

> [!note] The cover story
> Nog's pose as Longshadow's familiar isn't whimsy — it's survival. An unchained denizen of the Nine Hells loose in the city would be deported back to **Dis** (or worse, returned to servitude). "Familiar of a Drakaër" is the paperwork that keeps him free.

## Infernal Strictures
A devil on the mortal plane lives by rules. Among the known ones: he needs **permission to cross running water**; **salt across a doorway** bars him until someone breaks the line; **magic circles** are to be avoided. How many rules are there, exactly? Too many. And does anyone know his **true full name**?

## Drives
**Wants:** to stay free — uncaught, undeported, unbound — and to wring a little sin out of the world along the way.
**Bond:** the crew, [[Longshadow]] especially; he'll defend his friends ferociously.
**Flaw:** compulsive tidiness, compulsive mischief, and a devil's relationship with the literal terms of any bargain.

## Connections
- [[Longshadow]] — poses as *his* familiar; gleefully eggs on every bad idea, darting at his side like an evil thought.
- [[Vorr Kraxxus Drakaër]] — his late master; the source of his wild magic and, perhaps, of secrets Nog isn't telling.
- [[Callow]] — did Nog help the Halfling steal his soul back from the devil that held it? *Allegedly.* The devil in question should have known better than to deal with an imp.
- [[Half Moon]] — a buddy and a buzzkill; all that goody-two-shoes dedication. Nog is *sure* he'll squeeze a little sin out of him eventually.

## Hooks
> [!secret] The true name
> Whoever learns Nog's full infernal name holds a leash on him — and so does whatever he stole his freedom *from*. There is a ledger in Dis with his name still on it.

> [!hook] What Blackcloak made
> The experiments that broke Nog's magic open weren't idle. [[Vorr Kraxxus Drakaër]] was building toward something before he "died defending the city." Nog kept his study clean. Nog knows where things were kept. *(ties to [[Longshadow]]'s grandfather and [[Drakaër]] canon.)*
