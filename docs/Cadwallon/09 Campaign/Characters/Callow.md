> "I died with my hands full of secrets. The song called me back. I'm not finished listening."

**Concept:** A [[Halfling]] arcanist who delved the great tower-maze beneath the city, died down there, and bargained his way back — now a **ghost** clinging to a second chance with fragile fingers.

## Background
The heart of Cadwallon is the fabled tower-maze that sprouts from deep in the earth — the thing delvers call **Caer Dath**, the **Demon's Hand**, the **Amber Tower**, **Augerblot**, and a dozen other names. The wiki calls it [[The Labyrinth]]. Callow listened to its song and followed: braved its dangers, touched riches, beheld terrors he can't unsee. Some expeditions were celebrated. Others were not. Eventually his luck ran out and he **died** down there.

But he'd found secrets. Nothing is free — and the deal he took was not the deal he wanted — but he came back from the land of the dead to hear that sweet song one more time.

## Drives
**Wants:** to keep delving — to hear the song to its end, whatever that means.
**Bond:** the few who pulled him back, or didn't let him stay gone.
**Flaw:** a borrowed second life spends recklessly; he's already died once, so what's the worst that happens?

## Connections
- [[Nogzop]] — did a certain imp help Callow steal his soul back? *Perhaps.* If so, Nog will cheerfully remind him that one should always be cautious of trickery when dealing with the lower planes.

## Hooks
> [!secret] What the song actually is
> The tower-maze's "song" is the dream-pull of [[Desire]] bleeding up through [[The Labyrinth]] — the same Call that draws fortune-hunters to the city. Callow has been *closer to the bottom than almost any living Cadwë*, and a dead man hears the song more clearly than a living one. He may know — without knowing he knows — something about the [[Garden of Desire]].

> [!hook] The terms of return
> Whatever bargain returned Callow has terms, and terms have a holder. Someone, or some*thing*, expects to be paid. The clock is the campaign.
