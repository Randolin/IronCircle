# Troll

> "The ogres got [[Ogrokh|a fief]]. We got a hill outside the walls. Both of us came here on the same day, with the same captain. You explain it to me."
> — Gretha-Ironbridge, eldest Troll of [[Windswept Hill]]

The **giant kindred of the Behemoth Mountains** — distantly related to ogres by **mountain heritage** but distinct by lineage, temperament, and Cadwë legal status. The Trolls came to Cadwallon **with [[Khaurik|Khaurik's]] ogre exodus** in the founding era. They were **not granted the same protection**. The ogres got [[Ogrokh]]. The Trolls got told they could settle the outskirts. **A hundred and seventy years later, that is still where most of them live.**

## What They Are

- **Giant kin** — taller than ogres (2.5-3m), leaner, with rougher skin (rocky to greyish-green by lineage), heavier brow-ridges, and tusked lower jaws
- **From the [[Aarklash|Behemoth Mountains]]** — the same range that the rebel orcs fled to in 852. Trolls and Behemoth-Mountain orcs **share a region**, not a kinship.
- **Strong but not invulnerable** — Trolls bleed normally; the legends about troll regeneration are *Cadwë exaggeration*, encouraged by the Trolls themselves because it has kept casual aggression down
- **Lifespan**: 80-120 years; their elders remember the founding era directly
- **Came with [[Khaurik|Khaurik's]] ogres** in 832 as **camp followers, porters, and second-tier muscle**. The agreement Vanius offered the ogres did not extend to them.

## Where They Live

- **[[Windswept Hill]]** — the largest Troll community in Cadwallon, just outside the city wall; ~600 individuals living in the cluster-village the Trolls built when they arrived 170 years ago
- **[[The Nomadic Lands]] ([[Kraken]])** — Troll labor for the docks and the refugee economy; the population is high single thousands, mostly working
- **The edges of [[Ogrokh]]** — a small Troll community lives in the ogre fief by quiet agreement, serving as **secondary muscle** in the fief's enforcement structure
- **[[The Howlers]]' Howling pack house** — small Troll presence; the cross-species community appeals to a few of the younger Trolls

## In Cadwallon

- **The founding grievance.** The Trolls came with Khaurik. The Trolls were not given a fief. **Gretha-Ironbridge has petitioned every Duke since Lothar** for recognition as a founding population. Every Duke has declined. The grievance is real. It is not, by Troll standards, *urgent* — they have time.
- **Second-tier labor economy** — Trolls work the docks, the slaughterhouses, the warehouse routes, the construction crews. The work is paid; the pay is *less than ogre rates for the same work*. The Trolls have noticed.
- **No guild membership** — by tradition, not by law. **[[Guild of Architects|The Architects]] have started hiring Trolls** for the [[Tractor]] heavy-labor work; this is a *new* development and the other guilds are watching it.
- **The [[The Howlers|Howler]] experiment** — [[Krenald the Howler|Krenald]] has been negotiating with [[Gretha-Ironbridge]] about **bringing Troll muscle into the Howlers' bounty-hunter operations** as cross-species pack members. This would be unprecedented in Wolfen tradition. The negotiation is *active*.

## What They Remember

> [!secret] The orc rebellion
> Gretha-Ironbridge is **ninety-three years old** by Troll reckoning, which makes them old enough to remember **the immediate aftermath of the [[Orcs|orc rebellion of 852]] from the Behemoth Mountains side**. As a young Troll, Gretha saw the **first orc settlers arrive in the Behemoth range** and personally negotiated their accommodation with the local Troll clans. Gretha was an *important person* in that period and remembers what kind of intervention [[Syharhalna|the Syhars]] sent after the runaway orcs. Cadwallon's [[Orcs|orc community]] does not know Gretha exists. **[[Kal Rajghur]] would benefit enormously from knowing this.** Gretha is not advertising.

> [!hook] The Howler cross-species pack
> [[Krenald the Howler]] is offering Gretha-Ironbridge a deal: send **three young Trolls** to join the Howlers as cross-species pack members, in exchange for **public Howler endorsement of a Troll petition for founding-population recognition**. Gretha is considering it. The deal is good. The Howlers' political standing in [[Gamehead]] is, however, *low*, and Hadrax may **block the endorsement** the moment Krenald formalizes it. A free league brought into this negotiation may be asked to broker, witness, or *expose the cross-currents* in Krenald's faction that Gretha doesn't yet see.

> [!hook] The founding grievance resurfaces
> A young Troll named **Vord-of-the-Bridge** has begun making *speeches* in [[The Nomadic Lands]] about the Troll grievance, attracting [[Kraken]] dockworkers (Troll and otherwise) and beginning to look like the start of an actual labor movement. The [[Guild of Ferrymen]] are concerned. The Duke is being briefed. Gretha-Ironbridge is *embarrassed* by Vord and would like Vord *quietly silenced* — not killed, but **discredited or relocated** before the situation escalates and the Duke responds with militia. The free league hired for this job has to navigate Troll politics, Ferrymen interests, and a real underlying grievance that Vord is not *wrong* about.
