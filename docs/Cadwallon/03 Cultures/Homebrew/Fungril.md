# Fungril

> "We have always been here. The city does not know this because the city does not look down."
> — Soft-Wet-Listening, a Fungril elder in the Vegetal Entrails

The **fungus-folk of the Underground** — sapient, slow, soft-bodied people of fruiting bodies and mycelial networks, **native to the [[Vegetal Entrails]]** and emerging into Cadwallon's lower spaces over the past several centuries. Cadwës who have heard of them at all assume they are creatures rather than people. The [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] know better. **The Fungril are the city's quietest population and its best-informed.**

## What They Are

- **Sapient mushrooms** — humanoid in arrangement (head, torso, four limbs) but composed of fruiting bodies extending from a vast subsurface mycelial network. *An individual Fungril is more accurately a fruit of the larger organism* — multiple Fungril speaking together are one being thinking aloud.
- **Soft-voiced and slow** — speech is breathy, sometimes synced across multiple bodies, often takes a beat to start. *The pause is not thinking; the pause is the network propagating the question.*
- **Light-averse** but not light-injured. They can walk Cadwallon's streets at night. They prefer not to.
- **Networked memory** — what one Fungril knows, the local colony knows by the next moonrise. **Crossing colony lines requires effort**; deep knowledge can be siloed.
- **Long-lived as individuals (~100 years); essentially immortal as colonies** — a Fungril network may date back to the pre-Cadwallon era

## Where They Live

- **The [[Vegetal Entrails]]** — the founding home. The strip-leaf tunnels and the Fungril mycelium are co-evolved; the Entrails *are* the Fungril network's surface vasculature.
- **[[The Shades]] ([[Var-Nokkt]])** — the largest above-ground Fungril community, in the flood-prone lower section; the [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] HQ is also here, and the relationship is **deeply cooperative**
- **The [[Sewers]]** — Fungril stewards live in many sewer junctions, paid quietly by the [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] to monitor flow and report blockages
- **The [[Litiocs|Labyrinth approaches]]** — at the deepest reach, the Fungril network thins; *no Fungril has ever returned from a Labyrinth-direction expedition*

## In Cadwallon

- **The [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] alliance** is generations old and informal. The Guild employs Fungril couriers, listeners, and rescue-finders; in return, the Guild **protects Fungril colonies from Cadwë interference** and **brokers the rare necessary contact with the surface**.
- **[[Circle of Explorers|Shrew]] relations** — most shrews don't know about Fungril; the deep-tier shrews know and pay for guidance
- **The [[Tractor]] is a slow disaster.** The deep-bore drill has cut through **three confirmed Fungril colony cores** since 999. The Architects do not know this. The colonies have been quietly relocating, but the network is fraying.
- **What they know** — Fungril memory extends underground. **A Fungril elder may know what was happening in [[Cynwäll Ruins|the Cynwäll city]] when [[Feyd Mantis|Sinlis Mantis]] arrived**, because their colony was already there.

## Relations

- **[[Guild of Sewer Workers]]** — primary surface ally
- **[[Circle of Explorers]]** — deep-tier shrew clients
- **[[S'ylice]]** — the [[Ophidians|Ophidian]] holdout's chamber has Fungril growth in it; *they have been in conversation for centuries*
- **[[Vortiris]]** / [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] — the Fungril network *stops* at a definite line near the maze; they will not say why

> [!hook] The relocation
> A Fungril elder approaches a free league quietly — through the Sewer Workers — with a desperate ask: **the next [[Tractor]] bore will destroy a colony that has been continuous for over 600 years**. The elder needs the bore redirected. The Architects will not listen to a mushroom. They might listen to Cadwës with the right credentials. **The reward is information** the colony has been accumulating for six centuries.

> [!hook] What [[S'ylice]] told them
> The Fungril network bordering [[S'ylice|S'ylice's]] chamber has, over the past year, **been receiving a sustained communication** from her — a story she is telling slowly, in installments. The colony does not understand most of it; they have the sense it is *for someone else*. They are looking for the someone else.
