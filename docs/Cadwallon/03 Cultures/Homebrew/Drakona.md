# Drakona

> "Johann of Drakaër was not the first to ride one of us. He was only the first to ride one of us *in public*. The arrangement between our people and yours is older than your city."
> — Athrellis Drake-Born, Drakona elder of the Drakaër ancestral wing

The **dragon-kin of Cadwallon** — scaled humanoid people of true draconic lineage, descended from the **Lanever dragons** that have soared the peninsula since before [[Elhan]] arrived. The [[Drakaër]] fief is **named for the human-Drakona alliance** that produced [[Johann of Drakaër]]'s 843 dragon-riding privilege. The "titanic kin" of the Lanever dragons that visit [[Dyrsin]]'s Tower are not all the dragons Cadwallon contains — **the smaller, walking-around kind live in [[Drakaër]] proper**, and a few of them are among the most politically connected individuals in the upper city.

## What They Are

- **Scaled humanoids** with strong draconic features — scaled hide (colors vary by lineage), elongated faces, sharp claws, partial wings (functional for gliding rather than full flight in most lineages), and a constitutional connection to a **breath weapon** (fire, frost, lightning, acid, or shadow, by lineage)
- **Three lineages in Cadwallon** by tradition:
  - **Lanever-blood** — gold, bronze, and silver-scaled; the Drakaër-allied majority; politically conservative and dynastic
  - **Shadow-blood** — black, deep purple, and grey-scaled; descended from the **ancient dragons that allied with the pre-Cynwäll bloodthirsty people** referenced in [[Daïkinee]] legend; a small, secretive community
  - **Wild-blood** — red, green, blue-scaled; less common, descended from non-Lanever dragon lines (eastern Aarklash, the Behemoth Mountains, Avagddu); often refugees rather than natives
- **Very long-lived** — 250-350 years; the eldest Drakona predate [[Johann of Drakaër|Johann's]] dragon-riding privilege of 843 personally
- **Proud, formal, slow to anger** — Drakona social custom is **drenched in ceremony**; a slighted Drakona will spend a decade composing a response

## Where They Live

- **[[Drakaër]]** — the largest Drakona population, perhaps 400 individuals, concentrated in the family wings of the **Drakaër ancestral compound** and the connected **[[Wyvern Road]]** estates. The [[Drakaër]] family is **part Drakona by blood** — [[Silnus Drakaër]] is fully Drakona; [[Eric Drakaër]] is half, by his mother's line.
- **[[The Embassies]] ([[Den Azhir]])** — a small group of Drakona serve in **informal diplomatic roles**, often as intermediaries between [[Drakaër]] and the embassy nations; their long lifespans make them ideal multi-generational contacts
- **[[Shaproa]] ([[Den Azhir]])** — the **Drakaër dragon stables** are technically a Shaproa institution; several Drakona keepers live above the stables and tend the actual Lanever dragons
- **[[Dyrsin]]'s Tower** — *at least three* Drakona of the Shadow-blood lineage have been **inside the Tower** in the past century, by Dyrsin's invitation; what they discussed has not left the Tower

## In Cadwallon

- **The Drakaër alliance.** [[Johann of Drakaër|Johann's]] 843 dragon-riding privilege was the *public* face of an arrangement that already existed: the Drakona community had been quietly living in the human settlement at the founding-era Drakaër estate, and the dragon-riding privilege was their formal entry into Cadwë politics. **The fief and the people are co-constitutive.**
- **The Lanever dragons of Dyrsin's Tower.** When Lanever dragons "glide across the sky of Cadwallon to visit their titanic kin in the Dyrsin Tower," **at least some of those dragons are escorting Drakona riders or are themselves Drakona in dragon-form**. The transformation is a real lineage gift in the Lanever-blood line, though only a handful of contemporary Drakona can perform it.
- **The Shadow-blood question.** The [[Daïkinee]] legend of a "powerful yet bloodthirsty people allied to the most evil dragons of the whole Creation" refers to the **pre-Cynwäll Ophidian-era civilization** that called Lanever home before [[Elhan]] arrived. **The Shadow-blood Drakona are the dragon-side descendants of that alliance.** They know this. The Lanever-blood Drakona know this and find them embarrassing. The Cynwäll know this and **have, several times, asked Drakaër to expel them**. Drakaër has refused, every time.
- **Guild ties** — the [[Guild of Architects]] and the [[Guild of Cartomancers]] both have Drakona advisors; the [[Guild of Blades]] has only one (a Wild-blood refugee from the Behemoth Mountains); the [[Guild of Thieves]] has zero (Drakona consider thievery beneath them — *publicly*)

## What They Know

> [!secret] Eric Drakaër's swing vote
> [[Eric Drakaër]]'s political indecision is, in part, **Drakona internal politics breaking through**. Eric is half-Lanever-blood by his mother [[Hildra Var-Nokkt|Hildra]]'s line (which she does not know in full detail). The Lanever-blood elders of the Drakaër compound have been **lobbying Eric quietly** about the [[Den Azhir|Duke's]] policies in ways the rest of Cadwallon's political class cannot see. The Shadow-blood community has been **lobbying separately and against**. Eric is genuinely torn — *not just over the political issues, but over which dragon-blood faction he is more loyal to*. **A free league that figures this out** has a lever on the most important swing vote in the upper city.

> [!secret] The Cynwäll-era alliance
> The Shadow-blood Drakona preserve **oral history of the pre-Cynwäll alliance** that no other Cadwallon population can match. This includes:
> - What the pre-Cynwäll bloodthirsty people *called themselves*
> - Why the dragons of that period allied with them
> - **What the alliance was preparing for** when the [[Ophidians]] arrived and disrupted it
> - The names of three of the most-evil dragons, two of whom are *still alive* and live in distant lairs
>
> The Shadow-bloods will not share this freely. They will share it for **service** — concrete, dangerous service against the descendants of the same disruption. The [[Marine Ruins]] are interesting to them. The [[Walled Library]] is *very* interesting to them.

> [!hook] The dragon in the stables
> One of the Drakaër dragon stable's Lanever dragons has, in the past month, **begun refusing food and breathing in long uneven sighs that crack the stable's stone walls**. The Drakona keepers (led by **Vassil Drake-Tender**) believe the dragon is *responding to [[Desire]]*. They cannot ask the dragon directly — the dragon is older than any of them and considers direct questioning rude. They need free leaguers willing to **bring the dragon into proximity with the [[Garden of Desire]]** so it can sense the source clearly, **without anyone in Cadwallon noticing**. This is the kind of job that is paid handsomely *if it works* and gets the party permanently banished from Drakaër *if it doesn't*.

## Relations

- **[[Drakaër]] family** — co-constitutive; the fief and the people grew together
- **[[Dyrsin]] / [[Cynwäll]]** — formal acquaintance; Shadow-blood Drakona have been inside the Tower
- **[[Ophidians]] / [[Ss'Alish]] / [[S'ylice]]** — *deep historical entanglement*; the Shadow-bloods consider the Ophidian withdrawal "the disruption that ended our age"
- **Lanever dragons of [[Dyrsin]]'s Tower** — kin, by literal blood; the relationship is private and the Cynwäll mostly do not interfere
- **[[Acheron]]** — the Shadow-blood community has historic ties they are *not advertising*; [[Sophet Drahas]] has reached out twice, and twice been turned down
