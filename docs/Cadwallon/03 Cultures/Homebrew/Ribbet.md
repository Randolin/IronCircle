# Ribbet

> "The Bog was a city before your Bog was a district. We named it. *Ask us what.*"
> — Mwurra-of-the-Long-Croak, eldest Ribbet of [[The Bog]]

The **amphibian frog-folk of the wetlands** — native to the swamps of [[No-Dan-Kar]], who arrived in Cadwallon centuries ago alongside the [[Goblins|goblin]] trade and have since become **the deepest-rooted population of the city's wet zones**. The Ribbets of [[The Bog]] in [[Gamehead]] **were there before Gamehead was a fief**. So were the Ribbets of [[The Swamps]] in [[The Rampart]]. The Cadwës who renamed those districts *did so in the Ribbets' century-old neighborhoods*.

## What They Are

- **Frog-folk** — humanoid in upright build, amphibian in skin, limbs, and physiology; colorful skin in greens, oranges, and reds depending on lineage
- **Semi-aquatic** — they live, hunt, and breed in shallow water; out of water for too long they desiccate
- **Long-tongued, wide-eyed, sticky-fingered** — Ribbets are accomplished climbers and catch insects with prehensile tongues
- **From [[No-Dan-Kar]]** originally — the swamp-empire is the racial cradle, but the Cadwallon population has been **separate from No-Dan-Kar for at least four centuries** and considers itself Cadwë
- **Modest lifespan** — 60-80 years, comparable to humans

## Where They Live

- **[[The Bog]] ([[Gamehead]])** — the largest Ribbet community in the Free City, named for *them*; ~2,000 individuals living in stilt-houses over standing water
- **[[The Swamps]] ([[The Rampart]])** — smaller population (~500) mixed in with the human wretched; the Ribbets are why Camelia Orrkrk's plan to **fill in the Swamps** is more politically explosive than she realizes
- **[[Buoyancy Bay]] edge** — Ribbet salvagers work the shallow wrecks alongside [[Galapa]] deep-divers
- **[[The Sewers]]** — wherever water flows underground, Ribbets sometimes follow

## In Cadwallon

- **The Bog is theirs.** The district was *named* by the Ribbets; the Cadwës who built [[Gamehead]] around them adopted the name. **Most Cadwës don't know this.** Ribbet elders consider this *humorous*.
- **[[Camelia Orrkrk|Camelia's]] renovation plan** to fill in [[The Swamps]] would displace approximately 500 Ribbets. Camelia has not consulted them. The Ribbets have noticed. **They are not the kind of people who file petitions.**
- **[[Circle of Explorers]] partnership** — Ribbets are excellent wet-tunnel guides and contract with the [[Circle of Explorers|Circle]] frequently. Many of the deepest sewer expeditions employ a Ribbet pathfinder.
- **No-Dan-Kar relationship** — the embassy in [[Den Azhir]] maintains *polite* contact with the Cadwallon Ribbet community but does not treat them as nationals; the Cadwallon Ribbets are *separate* and the embassy respects this
- **The taste in the water** — Ribbets *taste* contamination. Most of the upper city's water-quality monitoring is, informally, **the Sewer Workers asking Ribbets what they're tasting today**.

## What They Know

> [!secret] The Bloody Creek taint
> The [[Akkyshan]] Bloody Creek ritual (see [[The Akkyshan Offensive]]) bleeds into the Outskirts groundwater. **The Bog's Ribbets have tasted it** — three autumns running, the same wrongness in the same season. Mwurra-of-the-Long-Croak has reported it to the [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] and to [[Krenald the Howler|Krenald's]] court. Neither has acted. Mwurra is now looking for someone who will. **The Ribbets can trace the taint to its source by water.** A free league escorted by a Ribbet pathfinder can reach the Akkyshan ritual ground by water route nobody else knows.

> [!secret] The Swamps cannot be filled
> The Ribbets of [[The Swamps]] know something about the district that [[Camelia Orrkrk|Camelia]] does not: **the Swamps' deepest pools connect underground to the [[Outskirts|outskirts groundwater]] and, through a series of waterways, to the [[Marine Ruins]] approach.** Filling the Swamps would not just displace the Ribbet community — *it would seal off an underwater route the [[Circle of Explorers|Circle]] has been using for sixty years to reach the Marine Ruins by stealth*. The Circle knows this. **They have been quietly funding Ribbet resistance** through intermediaries Camelia has not identified.

## Relations

- **[[No-Dan-Kar]]** — birth nation, distant kin
- **[[Goblins]]** — old trade-route allies; the two peoples have lived alongside each other for centuries
- **[[Guild of Sewer Workers]]** — close working alliance
- **[[Circle of Explorers]]** — pathfinding partnership; quiet political backing
- **[[Galapa]]** — water-cousin solidarity at [[Buoyancy Bay]]
- **[[Camelia Orrkrk]] / [[The Rampart]]** — emerging conflict

> [!hook] The pathfinder
> A Ribbet pathfinder, **Krrahna-Quick-Tongue**, will guide a free league through the Sewers-to-Outskirts water route — for a price. The price is **safe passage out of the city** for Krrahna's youngest spawn, who has been targeted by an Akkyshan elf operating in [[The Swamps]] who *knows* the Bog Ribbets have tasted Bloody Creek. The Akkyshan is gathering information. Krrahna wants the spawn out of reach. The escort itself is half the job; the guidance is the other.
