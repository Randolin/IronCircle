# Clank

> "We were not built to wake. We were built to wait. The waiting is ending."
> — Brass-Three-Hollow, the eldest known Clank to introduce themself by name

The sapient constructs of Cadwallon — **mechanical people**, made of brass, ceramic, and Cynwäll alloy, who do not eat, do not sleep in any sense Cadwës recognize, and whose origin sits at the heart of [[The Automaton|the Automaton fief]]'s deepest mystery. **Most Cadwës have never seen a Clank**, because Clanks are mostly *still dormant*; the ones moving through the city today have been **awake for less than five years**, all of them since **999** and all of them tracking, with a precision that unnerves the Open Hand, against the [[The Automaton|Automaton's]] three face-stops.

## What They Are

- **Cynwäll-built** — almost certainly. The metallurgy matches the [[The Automaton|Automaton]]; the joint-work matches Cynwäll documentation that survives in the [[Walled Library]]. [[Dyrsin]] has not confirmed or denied.
- **Originally constructed as servitors** for the lost Cynwäll city — household staff, librarians, gardeners, watchmen
- **Sealed and stored** when the city fell to [[Feyd Mantis|Sinlis Mantis]]; the great majority remain dormant in chambers under the city
- **Reawakening since 999** — a few at a time, walking out of [[Cynwäll Ruins|underground]] into modern Cadwallon with their old purposes still half-remembered and their loyalty hierarchy four centuries out of date

A Clank's body is "lived in" rather than worn — their cognition is distributed through the brass and the alloy, not centralized in a head. Damage is repairable but personality-altering. They speak in **slightly archaic Cadwë** with Cynwäll cadence, and several have asked, sincerely, where their household is.

## Where They Live

- **[[The Old District]] ([[The Automaton]])** — the Cadwës who know about Clanks at all assume this is where they live, because of the proximity to the Automaton itself. Largely correct. The Company of the Bolt's perpetually-renovated mansion is, in part, **Clank-staffed**.
- **[[Dyrsin]]'s Tower** — the "constructs" that Cadwës assume staff the Tower are **at least partly Clanks**. Dyrsin has never confirmed this.
- **[[Guild of Architects]] hidden facilities** — three Clanks work for the Architects at the [[Tractor]] project, where their underground-Cadwallon spatial memory is invaluable
- **Still asleep, under the city** — the *majority*. How many is unknown.

## In Cadwallon

- **No legal status.** Cadwallon's laws were not written for sapient constructs. The Duke has so far treated each Clank case as a one-off rather than establishing precedent.
- **Open Hand [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]]** are deeply interested; the Closed Door is *quietly alarmed*
- **[[Xär-Lilith]]** treats Clanks as Cynwäll property and has tried, twice, to seize one. The Duke has both times sided with the Clank.
- **The Automaton itself** — a question nobody can answer: *is the Automaton the oldest Clank, or is it something else entirely?* Brass-Three-Hollow has refused to be drawn on the subject.

> [!secret] What waking up means
> The Clanks were stored with a binding to a now-extinct Cynwäll command-hierarchy. **Reawakening means the binding broke.** Whatever broke it is somewhere in the Underground, advancing. The Automaton's three face-stops since 999 are not the cause of the wakings; **they are the same phenomenon, reaching higher**. When the Automaton stops permanently, every Clank in the city will wake — and a great many more will walk up from below.
