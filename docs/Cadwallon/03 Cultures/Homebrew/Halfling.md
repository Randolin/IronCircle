# Halfling

> "Yes, we look like small humans. *We are not small humans.* We came from somewhere else. Ask politely and I will tell you."
> — Mosie Tarrowfoot, baker of Heaven Heights

The **small folk of Cadwallon** — humanoid, roughly human-shaped, **half human height**, distinct from goblins by build and bearing, distinct from human dwarves by temperament. The Halflings are **the deepest-rooted human-adjacent lineage on Aarklash** — direct descendants of populations that **fled the lost continent of [[Kel]]** alongside the proto-humans, but who arrived in Cadwallon's region centuries before the founding and **kept their separate identity**.

## What They Are

- **Small folk** — adult height 0.9-1.1m; sturdier than goblins, finer-featured than dwarves, broadly human in proportion
- **Kel-blooded** — they share an ancient origin with [[Humans|humans]], but their lineage took a different path from Kel and they remember it as their own
- **Agricultural and mercantile by tradition** — farmers, bakers, brewers, tinkers, scribes, accountants; the **mid-tier merchant class** of much of the lower city
- **Long lifespan** — 90-120 years, comparable to dwarves; their elders carry oral memory that goes back to **before the [[Cynwäll]] city**
- **No Alliance allegiance** — they pre-date the Three Alliances and have never picked one. Halflings are **definitionally neutral**.

## Where They Live

- **[[The Fields]] ([[Trophy]])** — the largest Halfling community in the city, ~6,000 individuals; mostly small-holder farmers, brewers, and Standing-wind tenants alongside the [[Faun|Fauns]]
- **[[Heaven Heights]] ([[Soma]])** — Halfling bakeries, cafés, and bookshops; the **bohemian quarter's caterers** and one of the [[Last Dance|Last Dance's]] best suppliers
- **[[Little Klûne]] ([[Trophy]])** — surprisingly small Halfling presence (the goblin district is *not* Halfling territory; the two peoples coexist but do not blend)
- **[[Parchedale]] ([[Ghieronburg]])** — the gossip-rich Great Wash House is mostly Halfling-run; **the wash-house knows everything**

## In Cadwallon

- **The Kel question** — Halfling elders preserve **fragmented oral histories of [[Kel]]** that no human source can match. The [[Akkylannie|Akkylannian]] embassy has been quietly buying Halfling family histories for two generations. **The Halflings know what they are doing.**
- **Guild ties** — strong representation in the [[Guild of Tailors]], [[Guild of Scribes]], and the trades-and-bakers councils; **almost no Halflings in the [[Guild of Blades]] or the [[Free Leagues]]** by tradition (the Halflings consider warfare a Cadwë mistake)
- **The [[Bards of Destiny]] are half Halfling** — Argos Brisandre's collaborator **Podgy** is a Halfling who runs the Fields cooperative; the Bards' information networks run on Halfling kitchen-table relationships
- **No homeland to return to** — Kel is gone; the Halflings have nowhere else. Cadwallon's neutrality is, for them, *the entire deal*.

## Relations

- **[[Humans]]** — distant kin; relationship is polite and slightly distant ("they took a different road than us, and a worse one")
- **[[Goblins]]** — coexistent but separate; the Halflings find the goblin embrace of guile *exhausting*
- **[[Faun|Fauns]]** — partners in the Fields; arrangements between the two peoples are smoother than between either and the human Standing-wind aristocracy
- **[[Akkylannie]]** — quiet, ongoing tension over the buying-up of Kel histories
- **[[Bards of Destiny]] / [[Podgy]]** — institutional collaboration

> [!hook] The Akkylannian envoy
> A senior Akkylannian embassy official has made an unusual offer to **Mosie Tarrowfoot** (a Heaven Heights baker who happens to be a senior Halfling family elder): a generous "research grant" for a complete oral history of the Tarrowfoot line. Mosie has refused twice. The third request came with **an implicit threat**. Mosie is now looking for a free league to either *return the message firmly* or *help her relocate her family to the Fields under [[Vanho Soma|Vanho's]] protection*. Mosie knows what's in the family history. **It includes the name of the place in Kel where the proto-humans turned away from their kin** — a name that, if it became known in Aarklash, *would reshape Akkylannie's foundational mythology*.

> [!hook] The wash-house archive
> The Halfling-run Great Wash House in [[Parchedale]] has been collecting **overheard conversations** for thirty years and writing them in a cipher. The archive is in a locked room above the wash-house. **The Halflings consider it shared property** — anyone of standing can request information from it. **[[Bernadil Ghiéron]] has used this resource personally.** A free league that does the wash-house a favor can pull from the archive afterward.
