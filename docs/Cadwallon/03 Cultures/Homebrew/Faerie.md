# Faerie

> "We are not Fayrees. We are *related to* Fayrees the way you are related to apes."
> — Pellithra of the Arcadia court, to a Cadwë journalist who never published the interview

The **larger, civilized cousins of the [[Fayrees]]** — winged humanoid people of the magical groves, indigenous to Cadwallon, who stand at roughly Cadwë height and conduct their own diplomacy with the Free City. **Baron Mardi's Truce of 962 was negotiated for them.** The Fayrees just got included.

## What They Are

- **Sapient, social, individually named** — unlike fayrees, who are mostly anonymous and population-level. Faeries have houses, lineages, and reputations.
- **Winged** — translucent insect-like wings (dragonfly, moth, beetle by lineage), functional but used sparingly in the city
- **Roughly human-sized** but lighter — a tall Faerie reaches 1.6m; weights under 30kg
- **Long-lived** — comparable to elves, 200+ years; the Arcadia court contains Faeries who remember the [[The Magical Invasion|956 invasion]]
- **Native to Cadwallon's groves**, not imported from elsewhere on Aarklash — the [[Magical Groves]] are *home*, not a colony

## Where They Live

- **[[Arcadia]] ([[Ghieronburg]])** — the Faerie court, formally protected under [[Bernadil Ghiéron|Baron Mardi's]] Truce. The court itself is a layered grove-structure in the heart of the district, accessible to Cadwës only by **invitation**.
- **The [[Magical Groves]]** — scattered Faerie individuals live in groves across the city; most groves with a fayree population also have at least one Faerie acting as **steward**
- **The [[Outskirts]]** — pre-Truce population, semi-feral by court standards, occasionally raiding [[Trophy]] orchards

## In Cadwallon

- **Mardi's Truce was theirs first.** The 962 negotiation was between Baron Mardi and **Queen Selithré of the Arcadia court**. The smaller Fayrees were added at the Faeries' insistence — they were unwilling to enter a compact that excluded their lesser kin.
- **[[Thismee Ghiéron]]** is the Cadwë most-trusted by the Arcadia court. *Whether she has Faerie ancestry is the subject of polite Ghiéron family non-comment.*
- **[[Voyagers]] / [[The Tarot-Mages]]** — both factions have Faerie correspondents; the Faeries do not play favorites
- **The Faerie population is in measurable decline.** [[Thismee Ghiéron|Thismee's]] ledger has flagged this for [[Bernadil Ghiéron|Bernadil]] but nobody has confronted Mardi.

## Relations

- **[[Fayrees]]** — kin and dependents; the Faeries treat their smaller cousins protectively
- **[[Daïkinee]]** — the only mortal people the Faeries treat as full peers; [[Yalin Aoue]] has standing in the Arcadia court
- **[[Ghieronburg]] / [[Bernadil Ghiéron]] / [[Baron Mardi]]** — the political triangle
- **[[Magical Groves]] / [[Garden of Desire]] / [[Desire]]** — the metaphysical taproot; **Faeries can feel [[Desire]] more clearly than any other surface population**

> [!secret] What the harvest takes
> Mardi's slow Faerie decline is the **dream-harvest reaching deeper**. Mardi started with [[Bernadil Ghiéron|Bernadil's]] line and worked outward; the Faeries' decade-of-renewal Truce ritual is now part of the harvest infrastructure. **Queen Selithré knows.** She has not yet told her court. She is deciding whether to break the Truce — which would unleash the Faeries' century-and-a-half of cooperation back at Mardi, and ruin Ghieronburg in the process.
