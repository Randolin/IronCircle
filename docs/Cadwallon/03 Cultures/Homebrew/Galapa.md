# Galapa

> "I was a child when the Ophidians left. I remember the water tasting different the day after."
> — Sho-Ren, eldest of the Kraken-docks Galapa, on the Ophidian withdrawal of 255

The **turtle-folk of Cadwallon's coastal waters** — slow, long-shelled, philosophically patient, and **so long-lived that their elders are walking historical archives**. The eldest Galapa in the city personally remember **the [[Ophidians|Ophidian]] withdrawal of 255**. The middle-aged remember [[Khaurik|Khaurik's]] ogre exodus. The youngest were children when [[Duke Den Azhir]] took the title. **Their lifespan makes them quietly the most important historians in the Free City**, and almost nobody asks them anything.

## What They Are

- **Turtle-folk** — bipedal humanoids with carapaces (shells) along back and shoulders, leathery skin, broad faces, gentle voices
- **Slow** — physically and conversationally. Galapa do not interrupt. They do not raise their voices. They take meal-long pauses in mid-sentence and resume.
- **Extremely long-lived** — 300–400 years is normal; the eldest documented Galapa, **Sho-Ren of the Kraken docks**, is approximately **820 years old**, born before the Ophidian withdrawal
- **Aquatic capability** — they swim well and can hold their breath for hours; the [[Marine Ruins]] are *not foreign territory* to them
- **Refugees** — the Galapa came to Cadwallon from a now-submerged coastal nation south of Aarklash, lost to rising seas during the Steel Age. **They have nowhere to go home to.**

## Where They Live

- **[[The Docks]] ([[Kraken]])** — the largest Galapa community in the Free City; many work as **harbor masters, ship-keepers, and underwater salvagers** for the [[Guild of Ferrymen]]
- **[[The Marina]] ([[Den Azhir]])** — wealthier Galapa families who serve in upper-city diplomatic and translation roles
- **[[Port of the Ondine]] ([[Soma]])** — small population, mostly aged, who appreciate the quiet
- **[[Buoyancy Bay]]** — Galapa salvagers work the wrecks; **a handful have entered the [[Marine Ruins]] and returned**

## In Cadwallon

- **Living history.** A Galapa elder's testimony is treated by the [[Bards of Destiny]] as primary-source documentation. [[Argos Brisandre]] has interviewed Sho-Ren three times.
- **[[Guild of Ferrymen]] alliance** — long, mutually respectful, professional. The Ferrymen handle anything that requires Galapa skills underwater.
- **[[Circle of Explorers]] relationship** — Galapa salvagers are sometimes consulted on Marine Ruins approaches, but **the Galapa do not freely enter [[Ss'Alish|Ss'Alish's]] domain** and have warned against it for centuries
- **No Alliance affiliation.** The Galapa watched the Three Alliances form and concluded that *taking sides shortens lifespans*. None of them are wrong.

## What They Know

> [!secret] Sho-Ren's testimony
> Sho-Ren of the Kraken docks **personally met [[Ss'Alish]] before the Ophidian withdrawal**. They were on speaking terms. Sho-Ren has not raised the subject in eight centuries. **If asked correctly**, Sho-Ren can describe:
> - What [[Ss'Alish]] looked like alive
> - What the Marine Ruins were called in their proper Ophidian name
> - The reason most of the Ophidians left and a few stayed
> - The single fact about [[Vortiris]] that Ss'Alish told Sho-Ren over a meal, four months before withdrawal
>
> Sho-Ren has never been asked. Asking requires standing — *correctly correctly*. Sho-Ren is the central potential ally on every Marine Ruins thread, and almost no Cadwë knows they exist.

## Relations

- **[[Ophidians]] / [[Ss'Alish]] / [[S'ylice]]** — pre-existing diplomatic relationships, centuries cold but not broken
- **[[Guild of Ferrymen]]** — primary professional alliance
- **[[Bards of Destiny]] / [[Argos Brisandre]]** — interviewed many times, deeply trusted
- **[[Marine Ruins]] / [[Buoyancy Bay]]** — physically familiar territory

> [!hook] The grandchild
> Sho-Ren has a great-great-grandchild, **Nelu**, who is *young* (forty-something) and *impatient*. Nelu wants to enter the Marine Ruins formally and ask Ss'Alish for an audience on behalf of the family. Sho-Ren is opposed. **Nelu is looking for a free league to escort.** Sho-Ren will not protect them. Ss'Alish may receive Nelu as kin. Or not. The Marine Ruins do not forgive misjudgment.
