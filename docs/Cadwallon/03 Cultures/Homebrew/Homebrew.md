# Peoples Homebrew

Our table's additions, replacements, and overrides for the peoples of Aarklash. **Authoritative over source material.** When canon and this file (or any note in this folder) conflict, the homebrew wins.

This folder hosts the **Daggerheart ancestry slot-ins** — thirteen Daggerheart ancestries with no direct Cadwallon analog, each integrated into the city's history, fiefs, factions, and active plot threads as though they had always been there. These are *not* the eight canonical peoples; they are populations the city has always quietly contained, now made legible.

## Design Notes

- **Each ancestry has at least one "secret" or "hook" callout** — these are live plot seeds, not flavor; many connect to existing threads (Bloody Creek, the Tractor, Mardi's harvest, the Marine Ruins, the Walled Library, the Court of Shadows, Desire waking, Eric Drakaër's swing vote) and several seed new ones (Lior's Drahas-dreams, Yarrun's denied access, Vord's labor movement, the Firbolg ducal audience, the dragon in the stables)
- **No ancestry is "decorative"** — each has a concrete factional position, a guild relationship, and an NPC the party can find
- **The hooks favor agency** — players can broker, sabotage, witness, expose, or escort, rather than just listen to lore
- **Mechanical Daggerheart conversion** — not in scope for these notes; ancestry features map per Daggerheart core book. These notes establish the *Cadwallon-native fiction*; the mechanics carry over as-written
