# Faun

> "I was born under a Quithayran oak. I have lived two-thirds of my life on Trophy soil. I do not know which is my home anymore. Most days, I do not care."
> — Velhar of the South Field, in a Standing-wind tenancy interview

The **sylvan goat-folk of [[Quithayran]]** — distant cousins of the [[Daïkinee]] by long forest alliance, half their height by horn but twice their height by hoof. Came to Cadwallon in waves during the magical disruptions of the mid-900s, settled in the agricultural fiefs as **tenant farmers and shepherds**, and have, in the half-century since, become **the backbone of the city's food supply**. Most Cadwës don't realize this. The [[Gwan Standing-wind|Standing-wind decline]] is therefore not just a peerage problem — it's a Faun problem.

## What They Are

- **Goat-folk** — cloven hooves, short curling horns (longer in males), goat-like ears, otherwise sylvan in build (slim, woodland-quick)
- **Native to [[Quithayran]]** — they share the elven cradle as **the elder forest race that predates the elven schism**, though they refused the Light/Darkness/Destiny split entirely (similar to but distinct from the [[Daïkinee]])
- **Strongly grove-affiliated** — Fauns feel a [[Magical Groves|grove]] before they see it; a Faun farmer can predict which field will sprout a grove next season
- **Vegetarian by tradition**, omnivorous in practice; the dietary law lapses by the second generation in the city
- **Lifespan**: ~150 years; their elders remember Quithayran personally

## Where They Live

- **[[The Fields]] ([[Trophy]])** — the largest Faun population in Cadwallon, perhaps 4,000 individuals, mostly tenant farmers on Standing-wind land. They are why Trophy has a food surplus.
- **Western pastures of [[Gamehead]]** — Faun shepherds work the [[Wolfen]]-edge pastures (a precarious arrangement that has somehow worked for forty years)
- **[[Petropolis]] ([[Ghieronburg]])** — the **Cal Ka Dum orc worship grove** is co-tended by a small Faun community
- **The [[Outskirts]]** — the older, wilder Faun population that came pre-fief, still semi-sylvan

## In Cadwallon

- **The Standing-wind alliance is two generations old.** Bran Standing-Wind's grandson Gwan inherited a Faun-staffed fief and **does not fully understand how much depends on Faun goodwill**. The decline in [[Gwan Standing-wind|Gwan's]] standing has been quietly cushioned by Faun loyalty.
- **Markropet's 100-year buyout target** is land — but it is also the *Fauns living on that land*. The Fauns have not been asked their opinion.
- **[[Yalin Aoue]]** has invited the senior Faun elder Hethriel to the [[The Automaton|Automaton]] court four times. Hethriel has declined every time. The Daïkinee-Faun alliance is *old* and *not for show*.
- **Guild relations** — the [[Guild of Tailors]] grow their famous "magical wood" galleys with Faun consultation; the [[Guild of Ferrymen]] use Faun rivermen on the lake

> [!hook] The shepherd's witness
> A Faun shepherd in the western [[Gamehead]] pastures has seen the **Bloody Creek ritual smoke** rising from the [[Outskirts]] for three consecutive autumns. The shepherd has reported it to the Howlers (who didn't believe him) and to Gwan Standing-Wind (who couldn't act). **The shepherd is now offering the same testimony to free leaguers**, in exchange for protection if the Akkyshans realize who has been watching.

> [!hook] The eviction
> Markropet has purchased a Fields parcel with **160 Faun households on it**. The eviction notice is for the end of the season. The Faun community has not appealed to Gwan, the Duke, or any Cadwë institution — they are quietly preparing to **walk back to [[Quithayran]]**. If they leave, Trophy's grain supply collapses within a year. **The party can broker, sabotage, or witness.**
