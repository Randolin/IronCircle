# Infernis

> "I did not ask for the eyes I have. I did not ask for the dreams. I am asking now: *what do I do with them?*"
> — A young Infernis in the Last Dance, to Alizarine, after a reading

The **Darkness-touched humans of Cadwallon** — people of human stock with **demonic, devil, or Darkness-Realm heritage** somewhere in the bloodline, visible to look at and felt at the edges of perception by anyone with magical sensitivity. Most Infernis in the city are **refugees fleeing the very Darkness powers they descend from**. Cadwallon's neutrality is the only place on the continent they can safely exist. [[Sophet Drahas]] **can sometimes sense them**, and *some of them are his unknowing eyes*.

## What They Are

- **Human in basic biology**, with visible Darkness-touched traits — horns (in pairs or singly), eye colors that catch light strangely, skin tones in unnatural reds or greys, sometimes tails, sometimes vestigial wings
- **No singular origin** — Infernis bloodlines trace to:
  - **[[Acheron]] survivors** whose ancestors were touched by living-dead contact, several generations back
  - **Dirz alchemical experiments** that crossed humans with Darkness-Realm subjects
  - **Drune sacrifice survivors** whose lineage was marked but not destroyed
  - **Spontaneous emergence** in lineages that have never knowingly touched Darkness, but somewhere in the family tree someone did
- **Magical sensitivity** — most Infernis have a low-grade Darkness magic affinity, whether they want it or not
- **Lifespan**: roughly human (50-60 years), occasionally longer in those with deeper heritage

## Where They Live

- **[[Morgue Street]] ([[Soma]])** — the largest Infernis community, under tacit [[Guild of Thieves]] protection (the Thieves house "individuals who are generally discriminated against in Cadwallon," and Infernis qualify)
- **The edges of the [[Court of Ashes]]** — a smaller population that has been pulled inward by [[Sophet Drahas|Drahas's]] influence; some have become low-level [[Court of Shadows]] operatives
- **The [[Last Dance]] cabaret** — disproportionate Infernis presence among dancers and night-staff (their visible difference becomes performance asset); [[Alizarine]] reads cards for them often
- **Scattered through the city** — many Infernis pass for human in low light and live "ordinary" lives nobody questions

## In Cadwallon

- **The Drahas problem.** Cadwallon's protector-shadow [[Sophet Drahas|Sophet Drahas]] can sometimes *feel* Infernis through their Darkness affinity. Most Infernis don't know this. **A small number have been recruited as unknowing Court of Shadows assets** — Drahas plants suggestions in their dreams that they act on without realizing the source.
- **The Akkylannian Inquisition** — present at the [[Solitary Lodge]] in [[Heaven Heights]]; they keep informal lists of Infernis. So far the Duke has refused to hand the lists over.
- **The [[Guild of Thieves]] protection** is real but conditional. Infernis in Morgue Street trade information for sanctuary; the arrangement works for both sides.
- **No representation** in the Peers, the Houses, or any major guild leadership. **Aldenor Orrkrk** ([[Camelia Orrkrk|Camelia's]] frustrated cousin) is rumored to have Infernis ancestry. The rumor may be true.

## Relations

- **[[Sophet Drahas]] / [[Court of Shadows]] / [[Feyd Mantis]]** — the threat. Most Infernis fear them; some serve unknowingly.
- **[[Guild of Thieves]]** — the patron
- **[[Akkylannie]] / Akkylannian Inquisition** — the watcher
- **[[Last Dance]] / [[Alizarine]]** — the gentle eye
- **[[Possessed]]** — *not kin*. The Possessed are dwarves shaped by a Mid-Nor ritual; Infernis are humans marked by bloodline. The two peoples are sometimes mistaken for each other by lazy Cadwës; both populations resent the comparison.

> [!secret] What Drahas hears
> A young Infernis named **Lior**, working as a stage-hand at the [[Last Dance]], has been **dreaming Drahas's strategic deliberations** for the past six months without realizing what they are. Lior has confided in [[Alizarine]], who recognized the dream-content immediately and has **not told Lior** because the realization would crush them. Alizarine is gently feeding the dream-content back to **[[Isabeau Soma]]**. *Drahas does not yet know his channel is compromised.* If Lior is killed, the channel closes — and Isabeau loses her best line into the Court of Shadows.

> [!hook] The Inquisitor's list
> An Akkylannian Inquisitor at the [[Solitary Lodge]] has put together a comprehensive list of Cadwallon's Infernis and is preparing to formally request the Duke hand them over for "Darkness-vetting." The Duke will refuse, but the list itself is dangerous. A free league hired by the [[Guild of Thieves]] to **steal and destroy the list** before it reaches the Duke's desk is doing the city a service. The Inquisitor will not stop with one list.
