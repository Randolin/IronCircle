# Simiah

> "I have spent forty years annotating the [[Cynwäll]] ruins. I have spent forty hours, total, in [[Dyrsin]]'s presence. **He has not yet refused me an answer.** He has, on three occasions, given me answers I have spent the rest of my career disproving."
> — Professor Yarrun-of-the-Reach, holder of the Antiquarian Chair, Free University of Cadwallon

The **ape-folk of the southern jungles beyond Aarklash** — a refined, scholarly people who came to Cadwallon over the past several centuries as **traders, scholars, and cartographers**, and have, in the academic generations since, become **the city's foremost authorities on Cynwäll antiquity**. The Antiquarian Chair at the Free University has been held by a Simiah for the past two centuries. Almost every serious study of the [[Cynwäll Ruins]] published in Cadwallon was written by a Simiah scholar.

## What They Are

- **Ape-folk** — broadly humanoid with primate features (variable; some lineages are gracile and chimpanzee-like, others are robust and gorilla-like; the diaspora has diversified considerably)
- **From the southern jungles** beyond [[Aarklash|Aarklash's]] continent — a homeland Cadwës know almost nothing about, which is *very much how the Simiah want it kept*
- **Three jungle-cradle nations** sent representatives: scholars (the largest Cadwallon population), traders, and a small **diplomatic-religious caste** who serve as cultural anchors
- **Strongly literate culture** — Simiah arrived in Cadwallon already keeping written records; their archives in [[The Free District]] are *older than the Free University itself*
- **Modest lifespan** — 70-100 years; their institutions are long because their *records* are long

## Where They Live

- **[[The Free District]] ([[Drakaër]])** — the largest Simiah community, centered on the [[Free University of Cadwallon]] and the Great Scriptorium ([[Guild of Scribes]] HQ); ~1,500 individuals, almost all academic, scribal, or scholarly
- **[[The Old District]] ([[The Automaton]])** — Simiah scholars who work for the Company of the Bolt and the artistic-loot estimation trade; some of the best [[Cynwäll Ruins]] artifact authenticators in the city
- **[[The Embassies]] ([[Den Azhir]])** — small Simiah diplomatic-religious presence that liaises informally between the southern jungle nations and Cadwallon (no formal embassy)
- **[[The Free University of Cadwallon]]** — a Simiah scholar holds the Antiquarian Chair, has done so for two centuries, and the chair is widely understood to **remain Simiah for the foreseeable future**

## In Cadwallon

- **The Antiquarian Chair.** Held by **Professor Yarrun-of-the-Reach** since 988. Yarrun's research focuses on the [[Cynwäll Ruins]], the [[Walled Library]], and the pre-Cynwäll Ophidian period. **Yarrun has been to [[Dyrsin]]'s Tower three times.** Almost no one else has been once.
- **Guild ties** — strong representation in the [[Guild of Scribes]] (foundational alliance); informal advisors to the [[Guild of Cartomancers]] on historical reconstruction; *occasionally* consulted by the [[Guild of Architects]] on Cynwäll structural questions
- **Trade beyond Aarklash** — Simiah merchant houses maintain trade routes from Cadwallon to the southern jungle cities that **no other Aarklash population can travel**. The routes are not formally documented; the merchants are family-tied.
- **The southern jungle reticence** — Cadwës who ask Simiah about their homeland get *polite, articulate, complete-sounding answers* that, on examination, **never name a single river, city, or ruler**. This is deliberate.

## What They Know

> [!secret] The denied access
> **Yarrun-of-the-Reach has just been formally denied entry to the [[Walled Library]].** The denial came from [[Dyrsin]], through a Drakaër intermediary, in *Yarrun's third pass at researching the [[Elhan]]-sealed chamber*. Yarrun is the only Cadwë scholar who has gotten close enough to the chamber for Dyrsin to bother denying entry. Yarrun is **furious** and currently writing a paper that argues, on Simiah documentary evidence, that **the sealed chamber contains material Dyrsin personally has reason to keep hidden**. The paper, if published, will damage Cynwäll-Cadwë relations. Yarrun is looking for a free league to either:
> 1. Help publish the paper safely (the Cynwäll will try to stop it)
> 2. **Get Yarrun into the chamber** by routes Dyrsin will not see coming
> 3. Confirm or refute the Simiah documentary evidence Yarrun is relying on
>
> Any of the three is a long, dangerous job. All of them route through the [[Free University of Cadwallon]] and the [[Walled Library]]. **Yarrun has tenure, a network, and a grudge.**

> [!secret] The cartographers
> A small Simiah cartographer-guild operates from the upper rooms of the Great Scriptorium. They maintain the **only accurate map of the [[Outskirts]] beyond a five-day march from the city**. The map is private. **[[Bernadil Ghiéron]] has a copy**, paid for in a way the Scribes do not want examined. The map shows several features Cadwë cartography has never recorded — including a structure in the deep peninsula interior that may pre-date the Cynwäll.

## Relations

- **[[Free University of Cadwallon]]** — institutional home
- **[[Guild of Scribes]]** — foundational alliance
- **[[Dyrsin]] / [[Cynwäll]]** — academic counterpart, now strained
- **[[Bards of Destiny]]** — research collaborators
- **The southern jungle nations** — their unspoken backstop
- **[[Halfling|Halflings]]** — sibling refugee population in spirit; the two communities collaborate on language-preservation work for [[Kel]]

> [!hook] The cartographer's mistake
> A junior Simiah cartographer has **lost a map**. The map is one of the [[Outskirts]] interior maps and includes the pre-Cynwäll structure. The map was *taken*, not lost — the cartographer's apartment was searched discreetly and only the one document was missing. The Simiah elders want it recovered before whoever took it figures out what to do with it. **Several parties might want this map**: [[Sophet Drahas|Drahas]] (the structure may be near a Court of Shadows hold), [[Baron Mardi]] (the Outskirts route passes through his territory), or Akkylannian intelligence (the structure may be useful to the Inquisition).
