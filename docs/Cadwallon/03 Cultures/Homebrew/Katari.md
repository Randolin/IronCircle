# Katari

> "We were here when this peninsula was a forest. We were here when the elves arrived. We will be here when you have all forgotten what came before."
> — Rrhash the Old, eldest of the Krak Katari, to a Cynwäll envoy who never reported the conversation

The **cat-folk of the Lanever peninsula** — sapient feline humanoids who are, by every credible measure, **the oldest continuously-resident population in Cadwallon**. They were here when [[Elhan]] arrived. They were here when the [[Ophidians]] withdrew. They may have been here when the [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]] war happened. **Most Cadwës have no idea any of this is true**, because the Katari do not advertise.

## What They Are

- **Cat-folk** — humanoid in build, feline in features (slit pupils, ears, fur in various coat patterns, prehensile tails of varying length)
- **Pre-Cynwäll natives** — the Katari predate [[Elhan|Elhan's]] settlement of the peninsula. **They were the peninsula's people when the Cynwäll arrived.** The Cynwäll did not displace them; they reached an accommodation that has held, in some form, for over a thousand years.
- **Long-lived** — 150-200 years; their elders preserve oral traditions that *go back further than any other surface population's records*
- **Diaspora** — substantial Katari populations exist elsewhere on Aarklash (the southern coasts, particularly), but the Cadwallon population is the **founder community**

## Where They Live

- **[[The Krak]] ([[Kraken]])** — the spiritual heart of the Cadwallon Katari. The Krak's "upside-down tower" architecture is **Katari design**, not Bismuth design — House Bismuth inherited the building when their ancestor purchased it from the previous Katari steward. The Katari still live in the building's middle floors, by arrangement.
- **[[Heaven Heights]] ([[Soma]])** — older Katari trading families with mercantile ties going back to the founding era
- **[[The Free District]] ([[Drakaër]])** — Katari scholars at the [[Free University of Cadwallon|Free University]], particularly in the Antiquarian Chair's adjacent departments
- **[[The Outskirts]]** — semi-feral Katari clans on the peninsula's interior, who have *never* moved into the city

## In Cadwallon

- **Pre-Cynwäll memory.** Senior Katari elders preserve **oral traditions about the Ophidians, the Sphinx war, and Elhan's original arrival** that no other Aarklash population can match.
- **The Cynwäll accommodation** — [[Dyrsin]] knows the Katari are there; the Katari know Dyrsin is there. The two have had **perhaps a dozen formal conversations in the past five hundred years**. The accommodation is real and unsentimental.
- **House Bismuth relationship** — *complicated*. The Katari rent space to the Bismuths in their own ancestral building. Bismuth pays. Bismuth also defers to Katari elders on questions of [[The Krak]]'s deep architecture.
- **No guild penetration** — the Katari sit outside the guild structure almost entirely. They trade independently.
- **Diaspora trade ties** — Katari merchants connect Cadwallon to the southern Aarklash coastal cities through family-network commerce that bypasses formal trade routes

## What They Know

> [!secret] Elhan's seal
> The Katari were present when [[Elhan]] sealed the chamber in the [[Walled Library]] that bears his name. **Rrhash the Old of the Krak Katari was a child at the sealing.** Rrhash remembers what was put inside. Rrhash remembers *who else was present*. Rrhash will not say without an extraordinary reason. The Cynwäll know Rrhash holds this; they have asked, more than once, in their oblique way. Rrhash has declined.

> [!secret] The Sphinx war fragment
> Katari oral tradition contains an **alternate, older account of the [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx war]]** that contradicts both the Daïkinee record and the Cynwäll reticence on the same period. The Katari version names parties on the Ophidian side that *no other source names*. Sharing this would reshape Cadwë understanding of the deep past. **The Katari have not been asked correctly in eight centuries.**

## Relations

- **[[Cynwäll]] / [[Dyrsin]]** — the formal accommodation
- **[[Ophidians]] / [[Ss'Alish]] / [[S'ylice]]** — pre-existing diplomatic memory (mostly cold)
- **[[Bismuth von Kraken of Odazzur|House Bismuth]] / [[The Krak]]** — ongoing tenancy relationship
- **[[Free University of Cadwallon]]** — academic representation
- **[[Daïkinee]]** — quiet respect; the two oldest local peoples acknowledge each other

> [!hook] The Antiquarian Chair
> A Katari scholar, **Lyrrish of the Free District**, holds the Antiquarian Chair at the Free University. Lyrrish has just submitted a paper to the academic council on **"Pre-Cynwäll Lineages of the Peninsula"** that contains, in coded footnotes, fragments of the Katari Sphinx-war account. The paper is scheduled to be reviewed. *Dyrsin has formally requested the council not publish it.* The council will probably comply. **Lyrrish wants to publish anyway** and is looking for free leaguers who can protect the manuscript through the publication process.
