# Ophidians

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The ancient Serpent people created by the dragon [[Vortiris]] — humanoid-and-serpentine technomancers' enemies who razed the [[Utopia of the Sphinx]], occupied the Cadwallon site, and built a great temple that unknowingly tortured the sleeping [[Desire]]. Most followed Vortiris into the [[The Labyrinth]] in **255**; a few holdouts remain (the living [[S'ylice]], the undead [[Ss'Alish]]), now stirring with the Rag'narok.
