# Fayrees

> "The fayrees came with the city. Or the city came with the fayrees. We argue about it every Day of Flowering, and we never decide."
> — Thismee Ghiéron, hostess of the [[Arcadia]] court

Small, native magical creatures of Cadwallon's groves and Realms-adjacent spaces. **Not one of the eight "great peoples"** but a recognized population of the Free City — counted in [[Ghieronburg]]'s census alongside Elementals and magical Familiars, ceremonially protected by Baron Mardi's Truce in [[Arcadia]], and brushed up against in any [[Magical Groves|grove]] from [[The Automaton]] to the [[Outskirts]]. Where humans see the city, fayrees see a thinner version of it.

## What They Are

Fayrees are the petite, sapient face of Cadwallon's overlap with the [[Garden of Desire]] and the [[Realms]]. They are **distinct from**:
- **[[Magical Groves|grove]] flora and fauna** — fayrees are *people*, not vegetation or beasts, though they live entwined with both
- **Elementals** — Elementals are tied to a specific Realm (Water, Fire, etc.); fayrees are Cadwallon-born
- **Familiars** — Familiars are bound to a master; fayrees are free
- **the *animae*** — a singular forest spirit (like the one advising [[Yalin Aoue]]) is one being; fayrees are a population

## Where They Live

- **[[Arcadia]] ([[Ghieronburg]])** — the formal "magical creatures' district"; the largest known fayree population in the city, protected (and quietly catalogued) under Baron Mardi's **Truce**
- **The western pastures of [[Gamehead]]** — the *faerie groves* of the Wolfen fief; smaller, wilder population, less regulated
- **The [[Magical Groves]] across the city** — most groves have at least a few, though Cadwës rarely realize it
- **The [[Vegetal Entrails]]** — fayrees move freely along the strip-leaf tunnels; the [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] occasionally encounters them

## In Cadwallon

- **Baron Mardi's Truce (962)** regulates fayree-Cadwë contact in [[Ghieronburg]] and is the closest thing to formal recognition any non-human magical population has in the city. The fayrees keep their side scrupulously; what Mardi gets in return is the **central question of [[Bernadil Ghiéron|Bernadil's]] reign**.
- **Sihirs and Voyagers** — Cadwallon's relations-with-magical-creatures specialists (Thismee and Ombeline Ghiéron, and the wider [[Voyagers]] network) work most closely with fayrees of any non-human population
- **The "Fairslime" goblin farm** in [[Petropolis]] — name is suggestive; relationship to actual fayrees is local rumor rather than established fact
