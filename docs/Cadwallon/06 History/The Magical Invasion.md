# The Magical Invasion

> "In 956 magical beings launched an attack on the city from their elemental realm. In the face of this unexpected and unknown enemy, only the original tactics of a foreign general allowed them to triumph."

The **Water Elemental invasion of 956** — an attack on Cadwallon from an elemental [[Realms|Realm]], repelled by the foreign general **Denerac Ghiéron**, whose reward was a fiefdom of his own: [[Ghieronburg]]. One of the three great external threats the Free City has survived (with the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth|founding war]] and the [[The Akkyshan Offensive|Akkyshan raid]]).

## What Happened

In 956, **magical beings — Water Elementals, the Anfrebians** — launched an assault on Cadwallon **from their elemental Realm**. This was an enemy unlike any the city had faced: not an army marching overland, but an incursion from one of the elemental [[Realms]], exploiting the peninsula's thin boundaries between worlds (the same metaphysical weakness that makes [[Magical Groves]] and [[The Labyrinth]] possible).

The city was unprepared for a foe that didn't fight by conventional rules. Conventional defense failed.

## Denerac's Victory

A **foreign general named Denerac** — not a Cadwë, an outsider with no fief and no standing — devised the **original tactics** that turned the invasion back. The details are not recorded in the public sources, but the result was decisive: the Anfrebians were repelled, and Denerac became a hero of the Free City.

His reward was ennoblement and a fiefdom: the former goblin colony near the harbor became **[[Ghieronburg]]**, and Denerac founded the [[Ghieronburg|Ghiéron]] line. The fief's foundational pride — and its inferiority complex, being a "new" all-human house among older lineages — both date from this moment.

## The Lingering Threat

> [!secret] The Elementals never fully left
> The Anfrebian invasion was repelled, not eliminated. **Water Elementals still hide in the Cadwallon underground** — specifically in "the oasis," a network of warm, humid tunnels beneath [[Ghieronburg]] and [[Ogrokh]]. They have been there since 956, undiscovered, **spies at the heart of the Free City**. If their presence were exposed, it would spark a diplomatic crisis with the elemental Realm of Water (the "reflection of Water"). The [[Guild of Sewer Workers]] may have noticed warm outfalls; nobody has connected the dots. *(see [[The Sewers]])*

## Significance

- **Created [[Ghieronburg]]** and the Ghiéron line ([[Bernadil Ghiéron]]'s family)
- **Proved Cadwallon's vulnerability to Realm-incursion** — the city sits on thin metaphysical ground; enemies can come from *other worlds*, not just over the walls
- **One of the three great defenses** — alongside the founding war and the Akkyshan raid, it's a pillar of Cadwë civic pride and a lesson the [[Militia]] and [[Free Leagues]] still study
- **Left a hidden enemy underground** — the Elementals of the oasis remain
