# Battle of the Wall of Earth

> "The sudden appearance before the battle of a deck of 22 tarot cards endowed with magical powers was surely linked to this miraculous victory."

The decisive battle of Cadwallon's founding. The [[Dogs of War]] under [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]] broke a [[Acheron|Acheronian]] army of the living dead, commanded by [[Sophet Drahas]] — and won only because, on the eve of battle, [[Desire]] woke and turned Vanius's ordinary tarot deck into 22 weapons. The victory created the Free City. The secret deal struck *after* it created the city's enduring shadow.

## The Place

The **Wall of Earth** is a three-kilometre rocky cliff on the city's outskirts where Vanius made his stand. Today it's a historical site frequented by tourists and — far more — by [[Free Leagues|free leaguers]], who keep their [[Great Hall]] there. The falls below the cliff are where Cadwë funeral ashes are traditionally scattered.

## What Happened

The buried secrets of Cadwallon drew the attention of the [[Meanders of Darkness]] — the same powers that had destroyed the Cynwäll city centuries before. **[[Sophet Drahas]], commander of the armies of [[Acheron]] and baron of Taer Haez**, requested reinforcement from his overlord **[[Feyd Mantis]]**, the most powerful lord of his barony of origin.

Hordes of walking dead assaulted the Dogs of War's camp. The assault was bitter; the battle dreadful. The Dogs of War — 200 outlaws against an army of the dead — should not have held.

> [!secret] Why they actually won
> When Drahas's spells nearly broke through, [[Desire]] — sleeping in his prison directly beneath the battlefield — **woke briefly and mistook the necromancy for an [[Ophidians|Ophidian]] intrusion** (the old enemy who once tortured him). Terrified, he chose [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]] as his [[Incarnates|Incarnation]] and spread his **22 divine elixirs into the 22 cards of the tarot deck Vanius happened to carry.** Before the offensive, Vanius dealt one card to each lieutenant and kept the rest. The empowered [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]] turned the tide. *(see [[The 22 Arcana]], [[Desire]])*

At the battle's end, the troops of the **Alliance of Light arrived** to provide support, and the ruins were retaken from the enemy.

## The Aftermath

Two things came out of the victory, one public and one hidden:

**Public:** The Alliance of Light treated the Dogs of War as mere outlaws despite their bravery. Vanius, refusing to become a king (offending the Alliance) or a baron (submitting to it), took the old Barhan military rank of **Duke** — "my kingdom for a ducat!" — and founded the Free City.

> [!secret] The Pact of the Arcana
> After the battle, Vanius secretly met the defeated [[Sophet Drahas]]. He revealed the power of the cards and — proud and overreaching — **gave Drahas the Emperor card** (which depicted Drahas himself) in exchange for Drahas's support against [[Feyd Mantis]]. Feyd Mantis discovered the betrayal first and **cursed Drahas to remain in Cadwallon forever**, chained to a Throne of Darkness. This is the founding original sin of the city: the reason Drahas has fought every Duke since, the root of the [[Soma]] vendetta, and the seed of the [[Court of Shadows]]. *(see [[Sophet Drahas]], [[Vanius the Founder]])*

## Significance

- **The founding** — no battle, no Free City
- **The arcana enter history** — the 22 cards are first wielded here; they scatter across [[Aarklash]] afterward
- **Drahas's curse** — the permanent shadow over Cadwallon begins here
- **The leagues' shrine** — the Wall of Earth is sacred ground to the [[Free Leagues]]; their [[Great Hall]] stands on it; new leaguers "consult Vanius" (hire a tarot-master) there
