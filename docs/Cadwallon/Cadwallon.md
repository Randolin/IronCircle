# Cadwallon

> "These remains please you so little that I make them my kingdom for a ducat!"
> — [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]], throwing a battered gold coin at the feet of Alahan's emissary, 832

The **City of Thieves**. The **Jewel of Lanever**. A free city of roughly 300,000 souls on a peninsula at the heart of [[Aarklash]], surviving the slow apocalypse of [[The Rag'narok|the Rag'narok]] by being prosperous enough, well-connected enough, and dirty enough to bribe its way out of every war.

Founded in **832** by [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]] and his [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]] — mercenaries who took the ruined Cynwäll city for themselves, beat back the armies of [[Acheron]], and refused to bow to any kingdom. Now in **1003**, the city is ruled by **Duke [[Duke Den Azhir|Den Azhir]]** — himself a former [[Free Leagues|free leaguer]] of the [[Ruby-Heart]] — through a precarious balance of three powers: the **Duchy**, the **Guilds**, and the **Free Leagues**.

> [!note] Where we are
> **Year 1003.** Reigning Duke: **Den Azhir** (eight years in office). Motto: *"My kingdom for a ducat!"*
