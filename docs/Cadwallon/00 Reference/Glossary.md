# Glossary

> "When in doubt, the Cadwë word for it is whichever sounds rudest."

Working glossary of Cadwallon-specific terms. In-world usage, not Daggerheart mechanics. Wikilinked where the term has its own note.

## A

- **AF** — *After the Founding.* Year-counting from 832. Current year **1003 AF**. *(see [[Calendar]])*
- **[[Aarklash]]** — the continent.
- **[[Akkyshan]]** — the dark-elven branch ("Spider-culture") of the [[Meanders of Darkness]].
- **animae** — a wild magical spirit, particularly of a forest or grove. [[Yalin Aoue]] is advised by one.
- **[[The 22 Arcana|arcana]]** — the 22 tarot cards Vanius's deck became at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]], each infused with an elixir of [[Desire]].

## B

- **Barhan** — of [[Alahan]]; one of the three tongues that shaped the Cadwë language.
- **basiliege** — a magical oak (or statue) in [[Petropolis]] that grants unfailing fortitude to those who sleep beneath it.
- **Bran-Ô-Kor** — the desert orc nation; the larger of the two orc homelands, where the rebel orcs fled in 852. *(see [[Orcs]])*

## C

- **Cadwë** — a citizen of Cadwallon. The adjective and the noun.
- **[[Cadwë Dream]]** — the feedback loop between the city's longings and [[Desire]]'s dreams.
- **catacrypts** — the developed underground crypts beneath [[Ghieronburg]] (built 961).
- **[[Coiling Emissaries]]** — the six hooded figures who have visited the Duke every [[Day of Ashes|Day of Ashes]] since 999.
- **[[Cynwäll]]** — "the exiled." The ancestral elven branch of the [[Ways of Light]], whose lost city Cadwallon is built upon.

## D

- **Day of Ashes** — annual; the dread anniversary on which the [[Coiling Emissaries]] appear. *(see [[Calendar]])*
- **Day of Flowering** — annual; commemorates the [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]] clearing the bewitched jungle of [[Lanever]]. *(see [[Calendar]])*
- **dramatis** — an [[Incarnates|Incarnate]] who has fully integrated the divine spark and become a unique character role rather than a mortal. [[Duke Den Azhir]] is dramatis, and *unincarnable* — the divine cannot leave him.
- **ducat** — Cadwallon's local coin. Minted by the [[Guild of Goldsmiths]]; the Goldsmiths skim every conversion to the continental [[#K|klû]].

## E

- **elegratia** — narcotic harvested from the [[Vegetal Entrails]] in [[Gamehead]]; managed by the [[Guild of Ferrymen]].
- **elixir** — the divine substances Desire imbued into Vanius's tarot cards. *(see [[The 22 Arcana]])*

## F

- **fayree** — a small, native magical creature of Cadwallon's [[Magical Groves|groves]], concentrated in [[Arcadia]] under Baron Mardi's Truce. *(see [[Fayrees]])*
- **fief** — one of the **eleven** administrative divisions of Cadwallon. Each is ruled by a [[#P|Peer]]. *(see [[Fiefs]])*
- **Free Calendar** — Cadwallon's 22-month year. *(see [[Calendar]])*
- **Free City** — what Cadwallon calls itself.
- **[[Free Leagues|free leaguer]]** — a chartered member of the leagues; the institutional descendants of the [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]].

## G

- **[[Garden of Desire]]** — the Unfinished Realm at the heart of [[The Labyrinth|the Labyrinth]]; [[Desire]]'s prison-grown jungle.
- **groves** — see [[Magical Groves]].

## H

- **[[Houses of Peers]]** — the noble lineages descended from Vanius's 21 lieutenants.

## I

- **[[Incarnates|Incarnate]]** — a mortal carrying a divine spark; one of the gods' workarounds for being exiled from Aarklash.

## K

- **Khorda** — the original name of the orcs in [[Syharhalna]]'s cloning tanks. *(see [[Orcs]])*
- **klû** — the goblin coin of [[No-Dan-Kar]]. The continental currency standard inside Cadwallon.

## L

- **[[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]** — the divine maze around [[Desire]]'s prison; the deepest layer of the [[Underground]].
- **[[The Litiocs|litioc]]** — a grey-walled creature/feature of the Labyrinth; touching one blinds the toucher.

## M

- **[[Magical Groves|grove]]** — a surface-shoot from the Garden of Desire; a thin spot between the city and the god beneath it.
- **[[Meanders of Darkness]]** — one of the three Alliances.
- **[[Militia]]** — the Duke's standing enforcement arm (distinct from the [[Free Leagues]]).
- **mutagen** — a Syhar specialty; [[Syharhalna]]'s alchemists lead the continent in this discipline.

## N

- **Noesian** — a Cynwäll sage; one of the keepers of pre-Cadwë memory.

## O

- **omnimancer** — a Cadwë mage who works across multiple magical disciplines (as opposed to a specialist like a [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mage]] or [[#S|Sihir]]).
- **[[Onyrs|onyr]]** — a dream-stone; magical material used to channel emanations of [[Desire]].
- **[[Ophidians|Ophidian]]** — one of the Serpent people created by [[Vortiris]]. Most followed him into the Labyrinth in 255; a few holdouts remain.

## P

- **[[Paths of Destiny]]** — one of the three Alliances.
- **Peer** — a vassal of the [[Duke Den Azhir|Duke]] ruling a fief. Cadwallon has eleven fiefs but talks of "the ten Peers" because the Ducal Enclave is administered by a steward.
- **[[Possessed]]** — the renegade Mid-Nor dwarves; bodies sewn from victims' skin.
- **purepale** — strip-leaf fabric harvested from the [[Vegetal Entrails]]; used by the [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers]] for mood-fabric.

## Q

- **[[Quithayran]]** — the Eternal Forest; ancestral home of the [[Daïkinee]].

## R

- **[[The Rag'narok|Rag'narok]]** — the prophesied apocalyptic war the gods have been preparing for through their [[Incarnates]].
- **[[Realms]]** — the elemental/mystical pocket-worlds the gods were exiled to.
- **[[Ruby-Heart]]** — a famous Free League; Duke Den Azhir's former league.

## S

- **shrew** — the old (now-derogatory) term for free leaguers, used before the [[The Akkyshan Offensive|983 offensive]] when they were poor catacrypt-pickers. Still used by the [[Circle of Explorers]] for the deepest underground specialists.
- **Sihir** — a Cadwë magic-user, particularly one working with magical creatures.
- **slogre** — the slow, apathetic ogre cousins of [[Ogrokh]]'s warrior clans; concentrated in [[Ghieronburg]].
- **Steel Hearts** — one of the named [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]] companies; [[Yelmor]]'s unit.
- **strip-leaves** — see *purepale*.

## T

- **[[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mage]]** — a specialist in the tarot magic Vanius's cards established. Split between **Open Hand** and **Closed Door** factions.
- **technomancer** — particularly a Syhar one; creators of the orcs and other mutagenic projects.
- **[[The Three Alliances|Three Alliances]]** — the three philosophical blocs of [[Aarklash]] (Light, Darkness, Destiny).
- **[[Tractor]]** — the Architects' civic project begun 999; secret deep-bore toward the Labyrinth.
- **Truce** — Baron Mardi's compact regulating the magical creatures of [[Ghieronburg]], renewed each decade since 962.
- **Twilight** — the spirit said to have been sacrificed into [[The Automaton|the Automaton]] when the Cynwäll repaired it in 834.

## V

- **[[Vegetal Entrails]]** — narrow underground tunnels colonized by grove-vegetation; source of *purepale* and *elegratia*.
- **vestige** — a pre-Cadwallon ruin or relic (Ophidian, Cynwäll, or Sphinx). The [[Ophidian Vestiges]] are the most famous.
- **[[Voyagers|Voyager]]** — a magician who walks the [[Realms]] beyond mortal senses.

## W

- **[[Ways of Light]]** — one of the three Alliances.
- **[[Wolfen]]** — the three-meter lupine race; split between the moon-faithful of [[Yllia]] and the devourers of [[Vile-Tis]].
