# Calendar

> "Twenty-two months for the twenty-two wounds. The lieutenants knew what they were marking. They just didn't tell us."
> — a [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mage]] of the Open Hand, on the Free Calendar's deep structure

Cadwallon's reckoning of time. The **Free Calendar** is a 22-month year, an institution as old as the city — every fief, guild, and embassy works to it, and **everyone in Cadwallon does it without thinking about why there are 22**. The few who *have* thought about it are mostly worried.

## The Year

- **22 months.** No coincidence: this matches [[Desire]]'s **22 wounds**, the **22 [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]]**, and the structure of the [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]] (21 lieutenants + Vanius). The Calendar is the city's quietest cosmological tell — the underlying number of [[Cadwallon]] is the number of the god beneath it. *(see [[Mana & Magic]])*
- **Year-counting from the founding.** **832** is Year 1 of the Free City. The current year is **1003 AF** ("After the Founding"). Older Cadwë documents from the Cynwäll and Vanius eras use **Imperial** or **Cynwäll** dates that must be converted.
- **Original month names** came from the **21 free companies** of the Dogs of War (with the 22nd named for Vanius). Most of those names did not survive: under **Duke Lothar** the months were reformed into the modern set. Two of the original company-names are still occasionally heard in Cadwë idiom ("when the Steel Hearts march" = late autumn).

## Known Festivals & Days

The full festival cycle is not catalogued here — these are the days that appear in current events and existing notes.

- **Day of Flowering** — commemorates the [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]] deforesting the bewitched jungle of [[Lanever]] at the city's founding; the [[Magical Groves|groves]] that grew back are honored on this day. The single most public holiday in the lower city.
- **Day of Ashes** — annual day on which **the [[Coiling Emissaries]] have appeared before the Duke every year since 999**. Once unremarkable; now anticipated with dread by anyone briefed on what the Emissaries are. The Duke has not yet missed one. *(see [[Duke Den Azhir]])*
- **The anniversary of Bloody Creek** — not a city festival but a **[[Akkyshan]] ritual day** observed at [[The Outskirts|Darkshore]]; slaves go missing in the days before. *(see [[The Akkyshan Offensive]])*
- **Mardi's Truce renewal** — held **once a decade** in [[Ghieronburg]]; private ceremony, the next renewal is approaching and Baron Mardi has requested [[Bernadil Ghiéron]]'s personal attendance for the first time.

## Calendar Use in This Wiki

- **Dates in note frontmatter and the [[Timeline]]** are given as plain four-digit years AF (e.g. *832*, *956*, *1003*). No month/day precision unless specified.
- **The current campaign year is 1003 AF**. Any "now" reference points here.
- **Pre-832 dates** (Cynwäll era, Ophidian era, Golden Age) are best given in narrative form rather than numerical, because the Cynwäll calendar's epoch is uncertain to Cadwë scholars.
