# Mana & Magic

> "Mana rather than its essence..."

The practical layer of magic in Cadwallon — how spellcasters actually work, what powers them, and why the Free City is such strange ground for magic. The cosmological *why* lives in [[Cosmology]]; this note is the working *how*.

## Mana — The Raw Substance

**Mana** is the raw magical substance of the [[Creation]]. All magic is, at bottom, the shaping of mana. Gems hold mana; magicians spend or channel it; the world is saturated with it in varying concentrations. Cadwallon's concentrations are warped by the buried presence of [[Desire]].

## The Schools of Magic

### Elemental Magic (the Six Elements)
The structured, "civilized" magical tradition parses mana through **six Elements**: the four classical (air, earth, fire, water) plus **Light** and **Darkness**. This is the magic of:
- [[Cynwäll]] and [[Akkyshan]] elves
- [[Akkylannie|Akkylannian]] templars
- Trained academic magicians (the [[Free University of Cadwallon]] teaches the theory)

Each Element corresponds to one of the elemental [[Realms]] and (for Light/Darkness) to the dragons [[Maloth]] and [[Vortiris]].

### Instinctive Magic
Used by **orcs and shakas**. Instead of parsing the six Elements, instinctive magic draws on **raw mana directly through totemic affinity** — "drawing its strength from the mana rather than its essence." Orc magicians "can use any kind of gem to cast their primitive magic." Effective, flexible, but crude compared to elemental discipline. The orcs' totemic connection to the [[Magical Groves]] is part of this.

### Tarot Magic
The signature magic of Cadwallon. See [[The 22 Arcana]] and [[The Tarot-Mages]] for the full treatment. In brief: a tarot-mage's enchanted deck is **a re-enactment of the struggle between [[Conscience]] and [[Desire]]**, which draws some of Desire's power to the cards. The most advanced practitioners catch fragments of Desire's dreams and reproduce, at human scale, slivers of the gods' early powers.

### Faith Miracles
Divine magic — but with a unique Cadwallon restriction (below).

## The Onyrs

> [!note] Dream-stones
> The **Onyrs** are stones used to anchor and channel [[Desire]]'s emanations during certain castings — particularly tarot magic and divination touching the sleeping god. They are a Cadwallon-specific magical tool, tied to the city's proximity to Desire's prison. *(Mechanical detail in Secrets vol 1, Incantation chapter.)*

## Why Magic Is Strange in Cadwallon

The Free City is **anomalous ground** for magic, all because of what's buried beneath it:

> [!secret] The faith restriction
> **Only worshippers of an avatar of [[Conscience]] or [[Desire]] can call miracles in Cadwallon.** Faith from any other god simply *fails* here. The sleeping presence of Desire (and Conscience's lock upon him) cancels the attempts of any enemy faithful. The Cadwës rationalize this with their three home-grown cults (**Felicity, Pain, Destiny**) without realizing they're worshipping just two gods. A visiting priest of an unrelated god finds his miracles inexplicably dead. *(see [[Cosmology]], [[Conscience]], [[Desire]])*

> [!secret] Spiritual geography
> Certain districts of Cadwallon are **aligned to Light or Darkness** — not by their inhabitants' faith, but by **where [[The Labyrinth]] brushes the physical city**. [[Maloth]]'s domains within the maze brighten the spiritual map; [[Vortiris]]'s darken it. A magician feels the difference: Light magic comes easier in some districts, Darkness in others, regardless of who lives there. The Duke and senior [[Militia]] know which districts pull which way. Most Cadwës just notice that some places "feel off." *(The Windswept Hill mills, for instance, sit on ley lines where Rebirth-magic runs unusually strong — the [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]] experiment there.)*

## Magical Materials & Trade

Cadwallon's magical economy runs on:
- **Gems** — mana storage; gems of Darkness (hard to find here, smuggled from [[Acheron]] via the "way of the dead") are especially prized
- **Grove flora** — the [[Magical Groves|magical groves]]' addictive and effect-bearing plants, harvested by the [[Guild of Birders|Birders]], [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers]], and [[Guild of Tailors|Tailors]]
- **Vestige artifacts** — [[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]]-era devices animated by Light, recovered by [[Circle of Explorers|shrews]] from the [[Underground]]
- **The elegratia** — the flower of desire; narcotic; [[Guild of Ferrymen|Ferrymen]] monopoly
