# Maloth

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The **Dragon of Light** — one of the six original wyrms born of [[Desire]], who sided with [[Conscience]]. Patron and teacher of the [[Utopia of the Sphinx]], whom he taught to animate machines with Light. Drove back [[Vortiris]] and the [[Ophidians]] from Desire's prison, then helped seal its threshold beyond time. Still contests the maze with Vortiris inside [[The Labyrinth]].
