# Realms

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The elemental and mystical pocket-worlds beyond [[Aarklash]] — where the gods were imprisoned after the [[Creation]] exiled them. Magic thresholds (groves, vestiges, portals) on Aarklash link back to every Realm, including the elemental Realm of Water that launched the [[The Magical Invasion|956 invasion]].
