# Desire

> "He rests at the bottom of the vale, His body shaken by His dreams. All around Him a magical jungle sprouts, covering Him in a green shroud. He wants to awaken, He is calling us from his slumber, but the echoes of His dreams struggle with our conscience: we cannot believe in His existence which is what keeps Him captive."
> — Vision of a cartomancer

The sleeping god beneath Cadwallon. The reason this peninsula has been a battleground for ten thousand years. The most dangerous secret in [[Aarklash]].

## What He Is

Born from the union of the first gods, Desire was **branded with deformity and recklessness** from birth. His destructive rage in his waking hours killed innumerable living beings. He eventually found peace in a cave at the bottom of a vale on the [[Lanever]] peninsula — and there, while sleeping, he revealed his **second nature**: an enchanting dream-state that births marvelous gardens and addictive life.

[[Conscience]], to protect the [[Creation]], tried to lock him in. Desire rebelled. They fought without mercy. Conscience won — but only by inflicting **22 dreadful wounds** on his son and **sacrificing part of his own divine essence** to seal the prison.

The prison sits where Cadwallon stands today.

## How He Is Now

He sleeps. Mostly. He has been sleeping since before any Cadwë reckoning.

But sleep is not absence. **He dreams.** And his dreams are the engine of nearly every magical and metaphysical phenomenon in and around Cadwallon:

- **The [[Garden of Desire]]** — an Unfinished Realm spawned by his dreaming, full of seductive fauna and addictive flora. Every species the [[Guild of Birders]] catalog on the [[Immobilis]] archipelago is a dream-shoot.
- **[[Magical Groves]]** — when his dreams agitate, magical groves spring from the ground. **Each grove is a shoot from his prison**, a portal to the Garden. The [[Fayrees]] gather there; mystics commune there; the unlucky vanish into the Garden through them.
- **The "Call"** — Desire's emanations create a supernatural attraction to the peninsula. Exiles, dreamers, and fortune-hunters from every corner of [[Aarklash]] feel pulled here. The [[Cadwë Dream]] is real, and it's his.
- **The [[Cadwë Dream]]** itself — felicity (the upper-city promise), pain (the lower-city trap), and destiny (the freedom found by the few). These three categories are unconscious echoes of Desire's nature funneled through Cadwë culture and myth.

The exchange is two-way: he feeds the city with magic and longing, and **the city feeds him with the impressions and yearnings of its inhabitants**. Every wish made in Cadwallon strengthens his grip on the Creation.

## What He Has Done

Beyond the static condition of sleeping-and-dreaming, Desire has acted *intentionally* on the world only a few documented times. Each was a near-catastrophe.

- **The Birth of the Wyrms.** While walking Aarklash before his confinement, he created the six original dragons — the four elemental wyrms plus [[Vortiris]] (Darkness) and [[Maloth]] (Light). Vortiris sided with him; Maloth turned and sided with Conscience. *(see [[Cosmology]])*
- **The Vortiris Intrusion.** Vortiris led the [[Ophidians]] to assault the prison and nearly freed Desire. The pain and combat woke Desire briefly; he made [[Conscience]]'s defenses waver. [[Maloth]] drove Vortiris back, and Conscience built [[The Labyrinth]] to prevent a second attempt.
- **The Ophidian Mistake.** The Ophidians, occupying the surface after the Sphinx fled, built a temple to call Vortiris home and **inadvertently drew Desire's mana for their rituals**. This tortured him without waking him — Ophidian dark magic at the cost of a god's dreams.
- **The Awakening of c. 832.** When [[Sophet Drahas]] and the Acheronian forces were about to take the Cynwäll ruins, Desire **woke briefly**, mistook Sophet for an Ophidian threat, and chose [[Vanius]] as his [[Incarnates|Incarnation]]. He spread his **elixirs** into the 22 cards of Vanius's tarot deck — *(see [[The 22 Arcana]])*. The cards broke the Acheronian army at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]]. Desire returned to sleep.

## The 22 Wounds

Conscience inflicted exactly **22 wounds** on Desire before binding him. The number is not symbolic — it is *literal*. **It is the same number as the cards of Vanius's tarot deck**, and that is not a coincidence: when Desire infused those cards with his elixirs, each card came to bear one mark of one wound. The cards illustrate his life, his dreams, and his personality. The full deck, reassembled, is in some sense **Desire himself made portable**.

This is also why **tarot-mages' decks** work: they use copies of the original illustrations. Their incantations re-enact the Conscience/Desire struggle in miniature, drawing on the god's powers through ritual sympathy.

## What Wakes Him

Pain, blood, and worship aimed wrongly. Specifically:

- **Sustained dark ritual using stolen mana** (as the Ophidians did)
- **Battle near the prison** (as Vortiris did, and as the Acheronians nearly did)
- **The full force of the [[The Rag'narok|Rag'narok]]** spreading from elsewhere on Aarklash and brushing the Labyrinth
- **An [[Incarnates|Incarnation]] event** — when he reaches out and takes a mortal as a vessel of his will

> [!secret] He is waking
> Since 999, Desire has been **slowly waking again**. The signs:
> - The [[Coiling Emissaries]] manifest on the Day of Ashes
> - The face of the [[The Automaton|Automaton]] has twice stopped in a between-position during the Rag'narok
> - Magical-grove activity in [[Petropolis]], [[Arcadia]], and the [[Garden of Desire]] district has increased measurably
> - The Duke is having strange dreams (see [[Duke Den Azhir]])
> - The mysterious underground parcels in [[Soma]] — someone is moving something *up*

## Who Knows

- **Public face:** Desire is a cult name in lower-city religion, a folk concept of yearning. Most Cadwës would not recognize him as a god, let alone *the god under our feet*.
- **Rumored knowledge:** [[Voyagers]], some [[Guild of Cartomancers|Cartomancers]], [[Daïkinee]] sages, and a few [[Cynwäll]] Noesians know there is "something" beneath Cadwallon. The Daïkinee version of the legend calls him **Wisshard**.
- **GM-tier knowledge:** A handful of figures know the whole story: [[Sophet Drahas]] (because Vanius told him directly), [[Dyrsin]] (the Cynwäll ambassador in his tower), the Chroniclers (off-stage), and now [[Duke Den Azhir]] — whose dreams from Desire have been escalating since his accession in 995.

## Hooks

> [!hook] The Daïkinee call him Wisshard
> Professor Amtyaslan at the [[Free University of Cadwallon]] has a lecture series claiming Cadwallon was built on Wisshard's tomb. He's right about the god — wrong about the name and the death. A PC who takes his course might be told a half-truth that opens a door to the rest.

> [!hook] He whispers in dreams
> Anyone who sleeps too close to a magical grove may receive an instruction from a sleeping god. Sometimes the instruction is benign ("plant this seed here"). Sometimes it's not. Cartomancers can read what's been planted.

> [!hook] The cards as wounds
> Every card collected and re-assembled in the deck closes — or *reveals* — one of Desire's 22 wounds. Vanius walked into the Labyrinth seeking him with the cards. If you reassemble them, you can find Desire. The question is what you do then.

> [!hook] The Garden's offer
> The Garden of Desire is paradise on its surface — every appetite met, every want fulfilled. The Voyagers who have walked it briefly came back hollowed. A PC offered the Garden by Desire (in dream, in grove) is being offered exactly what they want, and that's the threat.
