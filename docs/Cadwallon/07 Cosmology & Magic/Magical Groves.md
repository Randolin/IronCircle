# Magical Groves

> "Regardless of place or time, in a totally unpredictable way, an abundance of plants sprouts anywhere, on the street or in someone's home."

The most visible supernatural phenomenon in Cadwallon — and the most misunderstood. Magical groves are sudden eruptions of impossible vegetation that appear anywhere, anytime, unpredictably. Cadwës treat them as good omens of Nature. They are, in truth, **shoots from [[Desire]]'s prison** — living portals to the [[Garden of Desire]], each one a thread of a sleeping god's dream surfacing in the streets.

## What Cadwës See

To an ordinary Cadwë, a magical grove is a wonder and a blessing:

- Plants sprout in a totally unpredictable way — in a shady courtyard one day, on a rooftop the next
- They're a **good omen of Nature**, especially for the Sessairs, the **orcs**, and the **Wolfen**
- **Destroying a magical grove is a crime** in the city
- They're a source of inspiration for the [[Guild of Tailors]] and [[Guild of Dressmakers]] — the vegetal fashions of the Cadwë nobles reference them directly
- The **Day of Flowering** (12th of Privime) celebrates the first groves that appeared after the [[Dogs of War]]'s "deforestation"; the orcs observe it most carefully, with shaka ceremonies in the groves

## What They Actually Are

> [!secret] Each grove is a shoot from the prison
> A magical grove is **a shoot from [[Desire]]'s prison** — a literal extension of the [[Garden of Desire]] (the Unfinished Realm his dreams have grown inside [[The Labyrinth]]) breaking through into Cadwallon. The vegetation of the Garden has, over eras, found its way up through the maze and reached the surface.
>
> Each grove is therefore **a bond to Desire's Realm** and **a two-way portal**:
> - It carries the **influence and dreams of the god** up into the city
> - It carries the **aspirations and whims of Cadwallon's inhabitants** back down to Desire, **reinforcing his grasp on the [[Creation]]**
>
> This is the engine of the [[Cadwë Dream]]: the city dreams, Desire feeds, Desire dreams, the city blooms.

## The Danger

Groves are not safe:

- **Each grove is a portal.** Following one down (especially in the [[Garden of Desire]] proper) can lead, via the mother-roots, to a portal into [[The Labyrinth]] and Desire's prison itself
- The **elegratia** — the "flower of desire" — is an addictive narcotic that grows in the groves. Those who give in to it are drawn irresistibly underground, to fall asleep forever beneath the mother-roots or join the tribe of Desire, their memories broken. The [[Guild of Ferrymen]] secretly controls the elegratia trade.
- Anyone who falls asleep in a grove is **influenced by Desire's troubled dreams**; their behavior becomes erratic
- The [[Fayrees]] and mystics drawn to groves are not always friendly

## The Warning Sign

> [!secret] The rate is climbing
> Magical groves appear when **Desire's dreams are particularly agitated** — when the Creation is most altered by his stirring. So the *frequency* of groves is a barometer of Desire's state. And since **999** — the year the [[Coiling Emissaries]] first appeared and the [[Tractor]] was begun — **grove activity has measurably increased.** The [[Guild of Birders]]' Provosts on [[Immobilis]] have the data. The senior [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers]] who harvest the groves have noticed. **Desire is dreaming harder.** The groves are the most public sign of the least public truth in Cadwallon. *(see [[Desire]], [[Timeline]])*

## Where They Cluster

- **[[Garden of Desire]]** — the great magical jungle on the lake; the densest concentration; site of the mother-roots and a Labyrinth portal
- **[[Immobilis]] archipelago** — every island is grove-grown; the [[Guild of Birders]]' domain; the **Sea of Roots** nearby
- **[[Petropolis]]** and **[[Arcadia]]** ([[Ghieronburg]]) — magical-creature districts thick with grove activity
- **Anywhere at all** — that's the point; a grove can appear in your kitchen

## Relations

- [[Desire]] — the source; each grove is part of him
- [[Garden of Desire]] — the Realm the groves extend from
- [[The Labyrinth]] — the maze the groves' roots tunnel through
- [[Cadwë Dream]] — the feedback loop groves power
- [[Guild of Birders]] / [[Guild of Dressmakers]] / [[Guild of Tailors]] — the harvesters
- [[Guild of Ferrymen]] — secret elegratia monopoly
- [[Fayrees]] — drawn to the groves

> [!hook] The grove in the parlor
> A magical grove erupts overnight inside a noble family's upper-city parlor. By law it can't be destroyed. But this one is growing *elegratia*, and the family's youngest has already started sleeping beside it and waking confused. The family needs the grove gone — quietly, illegally — before the addiction takes hold or the law notices.
