# Cosmology

> "Before being individuals, the gods incarnate primary forces."
> — **Chronicler maxim**, recorded by Professor Amtyaslan at the [[Free University of Cadwallon]]

A working model of how the world works, why Cadwallon's gods are weird, and what's actually buried under the city.

## The Architecture

Aarklash's metaphysics is built in layers. The deeper you go, the fewer Cadwës can name what's there.

- **The Whole** — the primal source from which the first entities emerged. Beyond comprehension.
- **The Creation** — the woven structure of everything that exists. When the gods nearly destroyed it in their early wars, the Creation **fought back** and exiled them.
- **The Realms** — elemental and mystical pocket-worlds where the gods were imprisoned. **Time** appeared with the exile, the curse that keeps them confined: if they walk Aarklash openly, they suffer the punishment and are forgotten.
- **Aarklash** — the continent at the heart of the Creation, where magic thresholds (groves, vestiges, portals) link back to every Realm. Whoever controls Aarklash controls the future of the Creation. That's why everyone wants Cadwallon.

## The Generations of Gods

The Chroniclers (who archive Aarklash's history) speak of **three generations of gods** — categories that gave their names to Cadwë cult-language without anyone realizing it:

1. **Felicity** — the first, oldest powers
2. **Pain**
3. **Destiny**

The modern three Alliances ([[Ways of Light|Light]], [[Meanders of Darkness|Darkness]], [[Paths of Destiny|Destiny]]) and the [[Cadwë Dream|Cadwë]] cults of the same names are dim echoes of these. Most Cadwës worship without knowing the names beneath the names.

## Conscience and Desire — The Local Conflict

Cadwallon sits at the literal epicenter of a two-god war. *(see [[Conscience]] and [[Desire]])*

- **[[Conscience]]** is the father, the binder, the will toward [[Creation]]'s preservation. Worshipped in Cadwallon as **Felicity, Pain, or Destiny** depending on cult. His avatars are the gods of [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] (Boar), [[Akkylannie]] (Griffin), and other Light-aligned and Destiny-aligned faiths.
- **[[Desire]]** is the son, deformed and destructive at birth, slumbering since Conscience wounded him **22 times** and locked him in an abyss. The abyss is **directly beneath Cadwallon**.

> [!secret] The hidden truth of every Cadwë cult
> Only those worshipping an avatar of Conscience or Desire can call miracles in Cadwallon. Faith from anywhere else simply doesn't work here. The Cadwës have invented "Felicity, Pain, and Destiny" cults to rationalize this without realizing they're worshipping two gods only.

> [!secret] The Duke is unincarnable
> The gods have tried multiple times to take the Duke as an Incarnate. None can. **The Duke is the vessel of Cadwë spiritual energy** — a kind of accumulated will of the city that refuses possession. *(see [[Incarnates]] for the rules of incarnation elsewhere.)*

## The Six Original Dragons

When Desire walked the world, his second nature gave birth to **six titanic wyrms** — the original dragons of Aarklash:

- **Four Elemental Dragons** — air, earth, fire, water
- **[[Vortiris]]** — the Dragon of Darkness, who sided with his father Desire
- **[[Maloth]]** — the Dragon of Light, who sided with Conscience

When Vortiris led the [[Ophidians]] in their assault on Desire's prison, Maloth drove him back. Maloth then sealed the prison's threshold into **a place beyond time**, where the fury of the dragons' combat still resonates. The most sensitive Cadwës still feel it as a presence under the city without knowing its source.

> The dragons that ride out of [[Lanever]]'s Dyrsin Tower today are descendants of these wyrms. Their alliance with the [[Cynwäll]] is itself an inheritance from the era of Maloth and the Sphinx.

## The Ancient Peoples

Two vanished civilizations left their marks on Cadwallon — and one occupied this exact site:

- **The [[Utopia of the Sphinx]]** — Maloth's allies. Master technomancers who built devices animated by Light. Vanished into [[The Labyrinth]] in pursuit of the fleeing Ophidians. Their legacy includes the [[The Automaton|Automaton]] statue, the faces on [[Fort Griffin]], and countless underground vestiges. The Cynwäll inherited a sliver of their craft and call themselves the Sphinx's heirs.
- **The [[Ophidians]]** — Vortiris's serpent people. Occupied the Cadwallon site for centuries after the Sphinx fled, built a vast temple to call back their dragon father, and unknowingly siphoned mana from Desire — torturing him. **Departed en masse in 255** through a portal into the Labyrinth, following Vortiris. A handful remained as hidden sanctuaries beneath the city. Their living descendants, struck by **the Vice**, are now stirring with the Rag'narok.

## The Labyrinth and the Garden

Conscience built **[[The Labyrinth]]** — an inextricable divine maze — around Desire's prison cave. Over eras, Desire's dreams transformed the cave into the **[[Garden of Desire]]**, an Unfinished Realm of luxurious vegetation and addictive fauna. The Garden bleeds into Cadwallon through every [[Magical Groves|magical grove]], which is literally a *shoot from Desire's prison*.

This bidirectional link is the engine of much of Cadwallon's strangeness:
- The Garden's vegetation reaches up; the Cadwës' impressions and longings flow down. Desire feeds on the city's dreams.
- **Each grove is a portal.** Walking the wrong one without warding can pull a soul into the Garden.
- **Time runs strangely** in the Labyrinth. The dwarf Yelmor (Steel Hearts company, vanished 857) was found in the catacrypts in 1003 believing only days had passed.

*(see [[Magical Groves]], [[The Labyrinth]], [[Garden of Desire]])*

## Inspiration and the Stateless

A third primal being, **Inspiration**, was born from the Whole alongside the first gods. She altered the shape and spirit of everything she touched. She is now gone, but left scattered descendants — the **Stateless** — born across all peoples of Aarklash. Stateless beings instinctively mimic their surroundings, blending into any culture but never truly belonging. The most evolved among them, the **dramatis**, can become anyone — body and mind. They lose themselves a little each time.

> [!secret] Den Azhir
> Cadwallon's current Duke is a dramatis. *(see [[Duke Den Azhir]] for the full secret.)*

## Magic in Cadwallon

*(see [[Mana & Magic]] for the rules; this is the cosmological layer.)*

- **Mana** is the raw substance of the Creation. Magic shapes it.
- **Six Elements** (the four classical plus Light and Darkness) form the structured magical schools. [[Cynwäll]] and [[Akkyshan]] elves and [[Akkylannie|Akkylannian]] templars practice these.
- **Instinctive Magic** — used by orcs and shakas — draws on raw mana through totemic affinity, without parsing the elements. Effective but primitive.
- **Tarot Magic** — see [[The 22 Arcana]]. A re-enactment of the Conscience/Desire struggle, drawing on Desire's elixirs through enchanted cards.
- **Faith Miracles** — work only if the worshipped god is an avatar of Conscience or Desire (see above).
- **Spiritual Geography** — certain districts of Cadwallon are aligned to Light or Darkness based on where the Labyrinth brushes the physical city. Maloth's domains brighten the spiritual map; Vortiris's darken it. The Duke and the militia know which districts pull which way; most Cadwës just notice that some places "feel off."
