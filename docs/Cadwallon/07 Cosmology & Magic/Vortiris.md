The **Dragon of Darkness** — one of the six original wyrms born of [[Desire]]'s second nature, who sided with his father. Creator/patron of the [[Ophidians]], whom he led in the assault on Desire's prison that the [[Maloth|Dragon of Light]] drove back. Still fights [[Maloth]] within the depths of [[The Labyrinth]], shaping the city's spiritual geography.
