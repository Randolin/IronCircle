# Conscience

> "In order to protect the Creation, Conscience attempted to lock him in. Desire rebelled and both gods fought each other without mercy."

The father-god. The binding, ordering will that holds the [[Creation]] together — and the jailer of his own son, [[Desire]]. Where Desire is appetite and dream, Conscience is restraint and structure. Their war, fought before Time, is the conflict Cadwallon physically sits on top of.

## Who/What He Is

Conscience is one of the **primal forces** the Chroniclers name before the gods became "individuals." He embodies preservation, order, and the protection of the [[Creation]] against the chaos of unchecked desire. He is not worshipped under the name "Conscience" anywhere — like all the gods, he wears dozens of masks across the civilizations of [[Aarklash]].

In Cadwallon's terms, **Conscience is the god behind the cults of Felicity, Pain, and Destiny** — the three Cadwë faiths are all, unknowingly, facets of him (and of his struggle with Desire). His avatars include the gods of:

- **[[Tir-Nâ-Bor]]** (the Boar) — the dwarven defenders
- **[[Akkylannie]]** (the Griffin) — the templar empire of Light
- Other Light-aligned and Destiny-aligned faiths across the continent

## The Wounding of Desire

The founding act of the local cosmology: [[Desire]] — born deformed and destructive from the union of the first gods — caused the loss of many living beings until he found refuge in a cave. **To protect the Creation, Conscience tried to lock him in.** Desire rebelled. The two gods fought without mercy.

> Conscience **cruelly struck his son, inflicting 22 dreadful wounds.** Desire fell, and Conscience cast him into an abyss before sealing it — **sacrificing part of his own divine essence** to do so.

Those 22 wounds become the 22 [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]]. That sacrificed essence becomes the lock on the prison. The abyss is **directly beneath Cadwallon**.

## The Labyrinth and the Lock

When [[Vortiris]] and the [[Ophidians]] nearly freed Desire, Conscience responded by **building the [[Labyrinth]]** — an inextricable divine maze around the prison cave — sacrificing still more of his essence to condemn the access. This is why the Labyrinth is not merely a maze but a *divine* one: time-warped, expanding, made of the substance of a god's self-sacrifice.

## Why It Matters to Cadwallon

> [!secret] Conscience's influence is load-bearing
> Conscience's presence over Cadwallon does real work in the present day:
> - It **disrupts dark rituals** — the Akkyshan witches' working at Bloody Creek stalls because "the dark principle has problems concentrating around Cadwallon because of the influence of Conscience" *(see [[The Akkyshan Offensive]])*
> - It shapes the **spiritual geography** — districts where [[Maloth]] (Conscience's dragon) extends influence brighten toward Light
> - It is **half of why faith works strangely here** — only avatars of Conscience or Desire can call miracles in Cadwallon

> [!secret] The stake of the Rag'narok
> If **Conscience dies** in the Rag'narok elsewhere on [[Aarklash]] — or is sufficiently weakened — **the lock on Desire's prison fails.** The 22 wounds, the abyss, the Labyrinth: all of it depends on Conscience continuing to exist. Cadwallon's metaphysical neutrality is, at bottom, the world holding its breath over whether the jailer outlives the [[The Rag'narok|war]]. *(see [[The Game of the Gods]])*

## Relations
