# Garden of Desire

> *Stub — to be expanded.*

The **Unfinished Realm** at the heart of [[The Labyrinth]] — [[Desire]]'s prison cave, transformed over eras by the sleeping god's dreams into a vast, luxurious, addictive magical jungle. Every [[Magical Groves|magical grove]] in Cadwallon is a shoot from it; the **elegratia** narcotic grows here. A two-way portal between the city and the god.
