# Sophet Drahas

> "His name is whispered in fear, never at night and always in broad light. He is given many titles: King of Ashes, Ambassador of Darkness, Acheron's Scarecrow."
> — *Free leaguer instructional text*

The shadow nemesis of Cadwallon. The single most powerful and dangerous individual permanently resident in the city — and, by curse, **he cannot leave**. Every Duke since Vanius has fought him. Every Duke since Vanius has failed to remove him. He may be the closest thing the Free City has to a permanent fixture aside from the [[The Automaton|Automaton]] itself.

> [!note] Who knows what
> The **public** knows Sophet Drahas as a legendary necromancer-king, a folk-tale to frighten children, a name spoken to mean "the worst that could happen."
> The **upper city** knows he is real, dangerous, and somehow chained to Cadwallon — but believes he's still trying to take the city for Acheron.
> The **Duke** and a handful of senior insiders know the full truth below.

## Origin

Originally **a [[Acheron|baron of Acheron]]**, of the barony of **Taer Haez**. Commander of an Acheronian expeditionary army sent against Cadwallon during the founding period. Vassal of **[[Feyd Mantis]]** — the most powerful lord of the parent barony — at the time of the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]] in 832.

Lost the battle. Should have died. Did not.

## The Pact

> [!secret] What actually happened in 832
> After the battle, **Vanius secretly met with the defeated Sophet Drahas**. Vanius — proud, impetuous, and already aware that [[Feyd Mantis]] was the real Acheronian threat — revealed the powers of the [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]] to his enemy and offered him **the Emperor card** (which depicted Drahas himself) in exchange for Drahas's support against Feyd Mantis. Drahas accepted. Greed won.
>
> Feyd Mantis discovered the betrayal *before* Drahas could act on his end of the deal. As punishment for the treason and the military failure, **Feyd Mantis condemned Drahas to remain forever in Cadwallon, magically chained to a Throne of Darkness in the catacrypts.** He has never been able to leave the city since.

## What He Has Become

The curse should have destroyed him. Instead, **Drahas adapted**. Trapped in Cadwallon, he reshaped himself into a *permanent feature* of the city's hidden architecture:

- **The Court of Shadows** — his own underground "fiefdom" in the [[Court of Ashes]] beneath the [[The Automaton]] fief. A throne hall, an ossuary, a yard full of the desperate and dying who serve him in exchange for relief from their misery.
- **The Shadows clan** — agents distributed throughout the lower city's underworld, parallel to and overlapping the [[Guild of Thieves]]
- **The Devil card** and **the [[Talisman of Shadows]]** — taken from [[Aghovar Soma]] when Drahas killed him; this is the trigger of the [[Soma]] vendetta and [[Isabeau Soma]]'s hunt
- **Financial empire via the [[Guild of Usurers]]** — the Usurers' senior council has been laundering Drahas's funds and operations since at least the early 900s. He pays them well.
- **Periodic urban-renewal investments** — Drahas has secretly funded "certain renovation projects" the [[Duke Den Azhir|Duke]] could not openly support. The Duke knows. The Duke takes the money. The arrangement is poison and everyone involved knows it.

## The Status Quo

The deepest secret about Drahas is that **he stabilizes Cadwallon**. By fighting the Ways of Light *and* the city's official authorities *and* his own Acheronian masters, he has become a third axis that balances the precarious power struggle between upper and lower city. **He no longer wants to destroy Cadwallon — or hand it to Acheron — or hand it to Feyd Mantis.** He wants it for himself, weakened enough to obey his whims, but not destroyed.

He is, against his every original intention, **part of why the Free City stays free**.

## Why Den Azhir Can't Move

The Duke knows Drahas's full network. He knows the Usurers launder his money. He knows where the Court of Ashes meets and who its lieutenants are. He **cannot** move because:

1. Tackling the Usurers head-on would crash the Cadwë credit market
2. Drahas's death (if even possible) would shatter the balance between upper and lower city
3. Drahas has at least **two of Vanius's 22 arcana** — the Emperor and the Devil — and the Duke wants them back, ideally without warning Drahas first
4. The Duke needs Drahas's status-quo function during the [[The Rag'narok|Rag'narok]] — he is, perversely, a guarantee of neutrality

## Allies & Lieutenants

- **Aïnoa the Unforgiven** — senior dark lieutenant
- **Yh-Azahir the Mad** — magician of distinction in the Court
- **The Guild of Usurers** senior council — primary financiers and intelligence brokers
- The **dark paladins** guarding the gate to the Court of Ashes (a dozen at any time)
- Necromancers across the city who use the Court's resources for research
- Scattered Akkyshan and Acheronian agents who route through the Court for safe passage

## Enemies

- **[[Soma]]** — open vendetta; Drahas killed Aghovar
- **[[Isabeau Soma]]** — personal hunter; she is coming for him and the Devil and the Talisman of Shadows
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — official enemy of every Duke since the founding
- **[[Maloth]]'s former allies** — the Cynwäll do not openly engage but know exactly who he is
- **The [[Voyagers]]** — several have lost members to the Court of Ashes

> [!hook] The pact's renewal
> Sophet Drahas has held two of Vanius's cards for nearly 200 years. Recent free leaguer activity around the lost arcana has him **considering moving the cards** to a more secure location. The transit window is narrow. Three independent factions — the Duke, the Soma family, and the senior tarot-mages — would all kill to intercept that transit.

> [!hook] The escape attempt
> Drahas does *not* accept his curse. He has been studying, for two centuries, **how to break Feyd Mantis's binding**. Recent activity suggests he believes he has found a method — and that the method involves [[Desire]] himself. If he succeeds, he leaves the city. If he succeeds *partway*, he becomes something worse.

> [!hook] The free leaguer offer
> A senior figure of the Court contacts a free league directly. The offer is generous: cash, protection, exotic equipment, even safe passage out of the city. The work is a small, specific delivery to a particular Cadwë noble. Refusing is dangerous. Accepting is worse. **Reading the package** is the third option.
