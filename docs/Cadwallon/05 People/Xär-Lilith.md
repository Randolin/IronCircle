# Xär-Lilith

> "Sister, I have been in three places at once for an hour. Where were you?"
> — Reported as her opening line to a rival diplomat

Peer of [[The Automaton]] when the Automaton's face turns west toward [[Ashinân]]. Matriarch of the **Xär-Lilith family** — one of the most venerable Akkyshan elven lineages, claiming descent from Scaëlin's first courtesan. The name "Xär-Lilith" means **"Eye"** in Akkyshan; it is the title that passes to the matriarch, not the person.

## What She Looks Like

A frail-framed elderly woman, **disproportionately small for her power**. Black hair shot with silver, kept long. Eyes that never blink at the same moment. She receives audiences in dim, candle-lit chambers that smell of cobweb-silk and faint preservative spices.

## What She Can Do

- **The gift of ubiquity** — she can be in multiple places at once, fully present in each. Not illusion, not projection: genuine simultaneous presence. The mechanism is unknown even to her own family.
- **Phenomenal diplomacy** — generations of senior Akkyshan elders have called her the best living negotiator. She has not lost a major negotiation since taking the title in 977.
- **Spider-aspect magic** — like senior Akkyshans, she practices Spider-culture magic at the high levels; Lilith's blessings sit lightly on her shoulders.

## How She Rules

The Xär-Lilith family is technically a council, in practice **a court of one**. She has no recognized heirs. Her advisors rarely survive a year in her service — disgraced, exiled, killed in unclear circumstances, or simply *missing*. The court is full of intriguers angling for the line of succession that does not exist.

She has chosen this. **She has no intention of dying or naming an heir.** The family's continued power depends on the senior elders' belief that one day there will be a succession; the succession never comes.

## Her Current Project

> [!secret] The Akkyshan agent in the Glistenning
> The young Akkyshan operative recently placed in the [[Guild of Favorites]]' Glistenning is one of her plants. Officially, the operative is "an exile making her way." Actually, she is collecting upper-city intelligence on the [[Var-Nokkt]]-[[Guild of Goldsmiths|Goldsmith]] axis, the [[Drakaër]] family's leak channels, and the activity around the [[Tractor]]. Xär-Lilith does not yet act on what she learns; she stockpiles.

## Relations

- **[[Yalin Aoue]]** — co-Peer of the Automaton. Open hostility under formal courtesy. The Automaton's face decides who rules and Xär-Lilith is patient.
- **[[The Automaton]] (the construct itself)** — uncertain. The Cynwäll-set device controls when she rules. She suspects the device is *not* impartial.
- **Akkyshan embassy** — she does not formally represent it, but the embassy treats her as a senior unofficial intelligence node
- **[[Guild of Thieves]]** — operational. Akkyshan loot from the forest of Ashinân flows into Thief safehouses with her quiet approval.
- **Duke** — formally cordial. Privately she considers him a useful instrument.
