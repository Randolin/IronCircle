# Feyd Mantis

> "Drahas is the King of Ashes. I made him kneel. He has not forgotten which of us holds the chain."
> — Attributed to Feyd Mantis; written in Acheronian, found scratched into a wall of the [[Court of Ashes]] the morning after a Drahas tantrum

The **Acheronian lord** who **cursed [[Sophet Drahas]] to remain bound beneath Cadwallon forever**. Per the PHB: at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]], Drahas — then "the commander of the armies of Acheron and baron of Taer Haez" — **requested assistance from Feyd Mantis, the most powerful lord of his barony of origin**, in the assault on the [[Dogs of War]]. Mantis came. The assault failed. And Drahas, in some accounting between the two of them that has never been fully reconstructed, ended up **bound to the city he had been sent to destroy**.

Mantis is **off-stage** — he is not in Cadwallon, he has never been seen in Cadwallon, and almost no living Cadwë has met him. But his **curse holds**, three centuries running, and **breaking it is the single thing [[Sophet Drahas]] most wants in the world.**

## What the PHB Establishes

- **Lord of Acheron**, of [[Acheron|Drahas's barony of origin]] — outranks Drahas in the Acheronian hierarchy
- **Requested by Drahas as reinforcement** at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]] — Drahas's first and last admission of insufficient force
- **Most powerful lord of his barony of origin** — the PHB's phrasing implies that Taer Haez (Drahas's seat) is not Mantis's primary domain; Mantis ruled (and rules) something larger
- **The PHB stops there.** Mantis's nature, methods, what he wanted from Drahas, and why he chose binding rather than killing are *all* implied but not stated.

## What the City Whispers

- **Necromancer of necromancers.** The Drune respect him. The Akkyshan witches will not name him. The [[Guild of Usurers|Usurers']] Acheronian contacts go silent if Mantis is mentioned.
- **The Mantis name is older than Acheron's current dynasty.** The necromancer **Sinlis Mantis** invaded the Cynwäll city long before the [[Dogs of War]] arrived (per the [[Timeline]]). The Cadwë underworld believes Feyd is **Sinlis's heir, descendant, or successor-self** — possibly the same being, possibly an inherited title, possibly something stranger. **No one alive in Cadwallon can confirm the connection.**
- **He has not visited Cadwallon since 832.** Drahas's curse-binding may prevent it. Or Mantis may simply have no need.

## The Binding

> [!secret] What Feyd Mantis did to Sophet Drahas
> When Drahas's assault failed, Mantis arrived at the field — *after* the Dogs of War's victory — and held Drahas accountable. The accounting was a magical operation that **bound Drahas's lifeforce to the city of Cadwallon itself**: Drahas cannot leave, cannot die in any usual sense, and cannot fully command his own form. The [[Court of Ashes]] is his prison; the [[Court of Shadows]] is what he has built within it.
>
> Why Mantis did this rather than kill Drahas outright is **the central question of the Soma–Drahas plot thread**. The likely answer: **Mantis wanted Drahas in place**. A bound Drahas, working from beneath Cadwallon for three hundred years, has been useful to Acheron's interests — surveilling the Free City, infiltrating the [[Guild of Usurers]] networks, harvesting Cadwë souls into the Court of Shadows. Drahas thinks he runs his own shadow empire; **the Acheronian intelligence community sees him as the longest-running asset they have ever fielded.**

> [!secret] What Drahas is doing about it
> Sophet Drahas has spent the last several decades **secretly researching how to break Mantis's binding** — through the [[Garden of Desire]] (using the [[Talisman of Shadows]] he took from [[Aghovar Soma]] for this exact purpose), through the [[The 22 Arcana|Arcana]] (the Devil card was a critical step), and through agents he hopes the Court of Shadows can land inside Mantis's own retinue. **None of this has worked yet.** It is the secret motive behind nearly every Drahas operation that doesn't make obvious sense.

## What Mantis Probably Wants

- **Cadwallon's [[Desire]]**. He is a necromancer of necromancers, and there is a sleeping god under this city. Mantis is *patient*; his time horizon is centuries. He may be waiting for the Rag'narok to weaken the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] enough to reach Desire directly.
- **Drahas to stay broken**. Drahas's continued utility depends on his binding. If Drahas breaks free, the asset is lost.
- **The Talisman of Shadows recovered**. Mantis knows Drahas has it now. He has not yet acted to retrieve it. **This restraint is itself information.**

## Relations

- **[[Sophet Drahas]]** — bound subject; primary asset; *enemy who serves him*
- **[[Acheron]]** — his nation
- **Sinlis Mantis** — predecessor, descendant, or alter — the connection is unresolved
- **[[Talisman of Shadows]]** — the artifact whose location Drahas does not want him to know about
- **The [[Coiling Emissaries]]** — Mantis may know what they are. He has not communicated.

> [!hook] A letter from Acheron
> A sealed letter arrives at the [[Guild of Usurers|Usurers']] back office in the [[Bridges|Bridges]] district — Acheronian wax, no return address, addressed to **"the King of Ashes."** Drahas's couriers normally collect these without comment. **This one was intercepted** before it reached him. The contents are written in a code Drahas's people know but Cadwë cryptographers don't. If decoded, the letter would reveal more about Mantis's current Cadwallon priorities than any other living source could provide.

> [!hook] The Mantis name surfaces
> A Cadwë antiquarian researching Cynwäll-era invasions finds a **Sinlis Mantis** reference, then a **Feyd Mantis** reference in the same source — written in the same hand, dated 150 years apart. The antiquarian wants the party's help authenticating the document. **Authenticating it would establish that Feyd and Sinlis are the same being.** Mantis would prefer this not be authenticated. So would the Open Hand. So might the Duke.
