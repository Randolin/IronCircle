# Albanne Drakaër

> "My uncle has lived twice my years and learned half my lessons. The remainder is mine."
> — Albanne, after a contested council vote

Peer of [[Drakaër]]. Young, charismatic, **the public political will of the Drakaër family** — and openly the strongest noble supporter of [[Duke Den Azhir]] and Cadwallon's free status. She holds the peerage despite the existence of **her uncle [[Silnus Drakaër]]**, who outshines her in political skill and quietly works against her ducal allegiance.

## What She Looks Like

A human woman in her late twenties, **slim and tall by Drakaër standards**, with the family's characteristic light hair and grey eyes. Dresses in Drakaër military formal — even in council, when most Peers go in civilian attire. **Carries a rapier**, which is a Drakaër family signature. Speaks clearly and quickly; does not waste words on people she does not respect.

## What She Inherited

- A peerage with the **strongest formal Cynwäll alliance** of any fief — including direct access to [[Dyrsin]]'s tower and dragon-landing privileges
- A family **divided** along four cultural fault lines: Bahran-traditional, Cynwäll-allied, Light-aligned, Free-City-loyal
- The **moral inheritance from Johann of Drakaër** — the Dogs-of-War-era founder who befriended the Cynwäll and was granted the dragon-riding privilege in 843. The "Drakaër stigma" — that the family is *too Bahran* for some Cadwës — runs back to him.
- A military tradition built on **cavalry**, which is absurd in a city of suspended bridges, but which she has chosen to keep alive

## What She's Doing

- **Publicly backing the Duke** at every formal session — even when this costs her support among the older noble houses
- **Strengthening Drakaër-Cynwäll ties** — meeting Dyrsin's emissaries personally; she may be the only living Peer who has been inside Dyrsin's tower
- **Funding the [[Light Cavalry Academy of Lanever]]** in the Free District as both military reserve and symbolic project
- **Protecting the [[Guild of Scribes|Scribes' leaks]]** that compromise the guilds — at least *some* of the leaks she knows about

## Her Family Problem

> [!secret] What Silnus is doing
> Albanne's uncle **[[Silnus Drakaër]]** has been working against her ducal allegiance from inside the family. He hosts dinners she doesn't attend. He drafts policy positions she doesn't endorse. He is, slowly, building a **shadow family council** that could move against her position if a crisis arose. Albanne knows. Albanne has not yet acted because the Drakaër family unites under external threat — and acting against Silnus would *create* the very split that destroys the family.

## Her Brother

**Eric Drakaër** is Albanne's younger brother, torn between admiration of his uncle Silnus and protective devotion to his sister. He may be the swing vote in any future family council; both Albanne and Silnus are quietly cultivating him.

## Relations

- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — primary political alliance
- **[[Silnus Drakaër]]** — uncle; cold war
- **[[Dyrsin]]** — Cynwäll ambassador; personal contact
- **[[Adalban Gherion]]** — Ghieronburg military head; long-running family hatred with Silnus that Albanne has inherited
- **[[Bernadil Ghiéron]]** — Adalban's nephew; Albanne has never met him privately and probably should
- **[[Camelia Orrkrk]]** — Peer of the Rampart, sometime mediator of the Drakaër-Ghiéron feud
- **[[Argos Brisandre]]** — bard, courier of the Scriptorium leaks. Albanne has met him *once*; the meeting is not in any ducal record.

The senior Drakaër military investigation is being run by *Silnus's* people. Albanne quietly asks the party to run a parallel inquiry and report only to her.

> [!hook] Eric's position
