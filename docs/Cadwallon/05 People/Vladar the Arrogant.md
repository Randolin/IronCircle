> A weapon-bearer with attitude. The man who carries another man's sword and resents every step of it.

## Canon Facts
- Canonical **weapon-bearer** in source material.
- Voice of the free leaguer manifesto in the source's opening pages — speaks the "we have decided to reject that fatalism... we are the free leaguers" passage.
- Source explicitly identifies him as both a free-leaguer-aligned voice **and** a weapon-bearer by profession.

> [!note] Homebrew context
> Our table dropped the weapon-bearer institution as a city law (see [[Cadwallon/05 People/Homebrew|City Homebrew]]). Vladar can still exist as an NPC — possibly the *last* weapon-bearer in Cadwallon, or one of a handful of stubborn holdouts in a profession the city no longer requires. His title might now read as a relic, an embarrassment, or a private boast.

> [!hook] The contradiction
> A free leaguer (independence, swagger) who is also a hired sword-carrier (subservience, deference). What does that look like? What does he carry his master's sword *for*?
