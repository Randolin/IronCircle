# Kurn Khaurik Argam

> "I have recovered my spirits. *I am told.*"
> — Kurn Khaurik Argam, at his first public address after the regency was lifted

The **youngest Peer in Cadwallon**. Son of the assassinated **[[Urakh Khaurik Argam]]**. Heir to the warrior tradition of [[Khaurik]] himself. Survived the **soft coup** by his own goblin godfather. Is now navigating a peerage he was never adequately prepared for, while the [[Ogrokh]] succession crisis continues to ripple through the [[Guild of Blades]], the [[Guild of Blacksmiths]], and the Tir-Nâ-Bor / No-Dan-Kar power axis.

## How He Came to Rule

- **1002, early in the year:** [[Urakh Khaurik Argam]] is assassinated. The vendetta protocols of the Khaurik Argam family fail to activate — *why* is still unknown.
- **1002, mid-year:** **Dazomet** — Kurn's goblin godfather — installs himself as regent over the young Kurn, on the publicly stated grounds that "Kurn has not yet recovered his spirits." Multiple witnesses report Kurn appearing **drugged, vacant, or possibly magically compulsed**.
- **1002, late year:** The senior ogre clans of [[Ogrokh]] get loud. Dazomet backs off slightly.
- **1003, early:** **The Duke himself intervenes**, sending tutors to Kurn. Kurn "recovers his spirits."
- **1003, current:** Dazomet has "devoted himself to improving his palace." Kurn rules officially. Khark, the Mar-An-Org clan leader, handles day-to-day operations.

## What He Looks Like

A young ogre — barely adult by ogre standards — about 2.1m tall, with a developing belly and the bent, slightly-stooped gait of his kind. **Eyes that don't fully focus** sometimes, especially after long council sessions. Wears the formal Khaurik Argam ceremonial robe (red and stone-grey) over working leather. Carries his father's warhammer ceremonially; it is too heavy for him to wield well.

## What He Is

- **Genuinely well-intentioned** — wants to honor his father's memory and the ogre clans' loyalty
- **Educated, but late** — the Duke's tutors have done good work, but the gaps from his early years are real
- **Quietly traumatized** — what Dazomet did to him during the regency was *not just political*. Kurn does not fully remember some months of 1002. He does not want to.
- **Surrounded** — every ogre clan, every guild faction, every embassy has an opinion about him. He has very few people he can trust.

## Who He Trusts

- **Khark** — Mar-An-Org clan leader; handles every-day business of the fief; Kurn's most consistent advisor
- **The Duke's tutors** — still resident in the Incarnat
- His **younger sister Maya** — not yet a public figure; intelligent and watchful
- The **ogre Duke's Guard** — sworn to the line; Kurn's only true bodyguards

## Who He Doesn't

- **Dazomet** — officially "improving his palace"; actually maintaining a network of agents in Thunder Vale and the Guild of Blades
- The **goblin smiths** in Thunder Vale — favored by Dazomet's regency; Kurn would replace them but cannot move yet without provoking violence
- The **dwarven smiths** in the Welding Arc — they want Kurn to side with them openly; Kurn cannot afford to without losing the goblin clans

## Relations

- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — protector and patron. Den Azhir spent personal political capital to free Kurn from the regency.
- **Dazomet** — *cold war*
- **[[Drakaër]] family** — strong allied bond (the Drakaër-Khaurik Argam alliance is old)
- **[[Guild of Blades]]** — the long-standing landlord arrangement holds, fragile
- **[[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] embassy** — informally backing him via the dwarven smiths
- **[[No-Dan-Kar]] embassy** — informally backing Dazomet

> [!hook] What Kurn doesn't remember
> A senior ogre clan elder approaches the party. **Dazomet's regency lasted seven months. Kurn remembers two.** The elder wants the missing five reconstructed — through forensic inquiry, magical investigation, or by simply finding the agents who were in the room. Some of the agents are still alive. Some have been very quiet.

> [!hook] The warhammer
> Kurn's father's warhammer is *known* to be too heavy for him to wield. A free leaguer who tests it discovers it is also *enchanted* — and the enchantment was reset (deliberately?) shortly before Urakh's assassination. **The hammer remembers**. If Kurn could wield it, it would name his father's killer.

Cross-referencing names will reveal the network. Cross-referencing names will get the cross-referencer killed.
