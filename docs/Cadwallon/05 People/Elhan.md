# Elhan

> "Those who stay are the elves. **We are the exiled.** Let us be remembered by what we left, not what we came to."
> — Attributed to Elhan, on the founding of the Cynwäll city

The elf who **led the schism** out of [[Quithayran]] and settled what would, much later, become Cadwallon. Per the PHB: "**Elhan and his followers** exiled themselves westward to the peninsula." His followers called themselves **Cynwäll — "the exiled"** — and built the great elven city at the mouth of the **Ynkarô** and the **Leâk Shear** rivers. That city stood for centuries before falling to the necromancer **Sinlis Mantis** and being abandoned in plague. The Cadwë ruins beneath Cadwallon are his.

He is the founder-figure of the entire **[[Ways of Light]]** elven branch. **He has no surviving direct line known to Cadwë heraldry.** Whether [[Dyrsin]] is descended from him, or knew him, or remembers him, are questions Dyrsin has not been asked.

## What the PHB Establishes

- **Led the elven exile.** When the elven [[The Three Alliances]] schism happened, Elhan refused to join either the [[Daïkinee]] (Destiny) or the [[Akkyshan]] (Darkness) and led his followers **westward**, away from Quithayran, to the peninsula that is now [[Lanever]]
- **The Cynwäll name comes from him.** "**Cynwäll**" means **"exiled"** — Elhan's followers named themselves for the act of leaving
- **Settled the city.** They settled "**at the mouth of the Ynkarô and the Leâk Shear**" — the two rivers feeding the peninsula's harbor. The Cadwë call the location "where Cadwallon stands now."
- **The city's original name is forgotten.** The Cadwës call its ruins the [[Cynwäll Ruins]]; the Cynwäll themselves have never publicly used a name for the lost city
- The city was **a thriving trade hub** for centuries — "the only place foreign nations could walk Lanever's soil without provoking Cynwäll wrath." Then **Sinlis Mantis** invaded with the undead, the city fell, plague filled it, and the survivors withdrew

## What He Was

The Cynwäll do not share their dead with the Cadwës, so Elhan's biography is **what the elves are willing to volunteer** plus what archaeology has confirmed:

- **An elf of the schism generation** — the moment the three branches separated, he was the one who *chose neither side*
- **A builder rather than a warlord** — the city he founded was a trade city, with **no military pretension**, even by elven standards
- **Friendly to dragons** — the **dragon-riding tradition** the Cynwäll preserve was Elhan's, inherited from his refusal to side with either the Daïkinee or the Akkyshan but his acceptance of the *Light* affinity that became the [[Ways of Light]]
- **A name that survives among the Cynwäll as something close to "founder-saint"** — not religious in the human sense but reverenced in a way humans translate as religious

## Why He Still Matters

- **The buried Cynwäll city is his.** Every shrew expedition, every [[Circle of Explorers|Circle]] survey, every Tractor bore is touching **his** infrastructure. The Cynwäll consider it sacred. [[Dyrsin]]'s Tower watches over it for him.
- **The Cynwäll's relationship to Cadwallon is mediated through him.** The Cynwäll did not abandon the site by choice — they *retreated* after the plague. They have never legally acknowledged Cadwë sovereignty over the buried city. Elhan's claim, in some elven sense, **still holds**.
- **The Mantis line started here.** [[Sinlis Mantis|Sinlis]]'s invasion of Elhan's city was the first move of what may be a multi-generational Acheronian project. **[[Feyd Mantis]]** continues it. If Feyd and Sinlis are the same being, then Elhan's enemy is **still alive**.

## The Vanished Line

- **No Cadwë-recognized direct descendant** of Elhan is publicly named in Cadwallon. The Cynwäll do not share this information.
- **Dyrsin's lineage is private.** He may or may not be of Elhan's blood; he has not said.
- **The [[Walled Library]]** contains, per the [[Circle of Explorers|shrews]] who have reached it, **a chamber sealed since before the city's fall**. Cynwäll inscriptions on the seal name Elhan. **What is inside has never been opened.** Dyrsin has not permitted it. He has, on at least one occasion, prevented it.

## Relations

- **The [[Cynwäll]]** — his people; he created the branch
- **The [[Daïkinee]]** — his cousin branch; the people he left
- **The [[Akkyshan]]** — his opposing branch; the people he refused
- **[[Quithayran]]** — his birth-place; he left
- **[[Cynwäll Ruins]] / [[Walled Library]]** — his physical legacy
- **[[Dyrsin]]** — his (possible) heir, certainly his caretaker
- **Sinlis Mantis** (deceased? immortal?) — the necromancer who took his city
- **[[Feyd Mantis]]** — the Acheronian lord whose connection to Sinlis is unconfirmed; the Mantis line's enmity with Elhan's runs through both

> [!secret] What's in the sealed chamber
> The Walled Library's Elhan-sealed chamber contains, according to a single Daïkinee scholarly fragment, **Elhan's own writings on the schism** — his account of why he led the exile, *what he was running from*, and *what he believed would come after*. If this fragment is accurate, the chamber holds the **founder-elf's contemporary record** of the period the surviving Daïkinee and Akkyshan now contest. The Cynwäll seal it because **Elhan's account would not flatter the surviving Cynwäll-Daïkinee-Akkyshan negotiation positions**. It might also explain things the Cynwäll genuinely do not want known: *why* the schism happened, and *what* the elves were willing to do to prevent or precipitate it. **Dyrsin would prefer this chamber stay sealed during his lifetime.** If a free league opens it, the diplomatic consequences cascade through all three elven branches and possibly through the Light–Darkness–Destiny axis itself.

> [!hook] Elhan's seal
> A free league exploring the Walled Library finds a **seal bearing Elhan's name**. The seal is genuine and intact. Opening it requires a Cynwäll witness with hereditary standing. **The only such witness in Cadwallon is [[Dyrsin]].** He will not come down from the Tower. *(see [[Dyrsin]] hook on getting an audience.)*
