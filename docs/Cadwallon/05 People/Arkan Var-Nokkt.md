# Arkan Var-Nokkt

> "My brother sits on a throne of compromised stone. I would rather sit on a dwarven hearth and *know what I am sitting on*."
> — Arkan, in a letter to a Tir-Nâ-Bor cousin, intercepted by Usurer agents (year and source not recorded)

The **brother of [[Torguir IV Var-Nokkt]]**, vanished from Cadwallon some years ago and **living in hiding in the [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] mountains**. By Cadwë law his peerage rights are dormant; by Cadwë rumor he is the **one Var-Nokkt who knows what the [[Guild of Usurers]] hold over the family** and walked away rather than serve the lever. He maintains a small network of correspondents in the city — quiet, slow, careful — and is one of the very few living people who could **publicly break the Usurer–Var-Nokkt hold** if he chose to surface.

## Why He Left

Arkan grew up in the **Hildra-generation Var-Nokkt court** — the household his brother now rules through. He had access from boyhood to every chamber, every ledger, every conversation. **He figured out the Senex marriage was bought.**

Unlike [[Master Ôdril Haarken|Ôdril Haarken]], who pieced it together from outside banking traces, Arkan saw it **from inside the household** — the documents Hildra never put in any vault, the *original* dowry terms, the names of the Usurer brokers who had been at his mother's table when he was a child. He was, depending on the version of events, **somewhere between fourteen and seventeen** when he understood.

He spent the next several years deciding what to do. **He chose to leave.**

- He did not denounce the family.
- He did not approach the Duke.
- He did not tell his brother Torguir.
- He **left the city** — destination unknown publicly, in fact [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]], to dwarven cousins of his mother's line.

He has not returned.

## Where He Is Now

Arkan lives in **a small mountain settlement in the Aegis range**, several days' travel from any Tir-Nâ-Bor metropolitan center. He is hosted by a dwarven family who **share blood with the [[Senex]] line through a different branch** — a careful and politically meaningful choice. His Tir-Nâ-Bor hosts shelter him on the understanding that **if he ever surfaces with what he knows, it will reshape the Cadwë–Tir-Nâ-Bor diplomatic landscape**.

He is healthy, married (to a dwarven scholar), and the father of two **mixed-heritage children** who would, technically, have a Cadwë claim — though the Cadwë heralds have not yet ruled.

## Who Knows He's There

- **His Tir-Nâ-Bor hosts** — entirely
- **The [[Guild of Usurers]]** — yes; they have monitored his location for years and have not moved against him. **The restraint is informative.**
- **[[Torguir IV Var-Nokkt|Torguir]]** — yes, since approximately 999; the brothers have *not* communicated
- **Hildra Var-Nokkt** — almost certainly. She has not raised it.
- **[[Master Ôdril Haarken]]** — was working on a back-channel approach when he disappeared
- **[[Urakh Khaurik Argam]]** — reached for him through an unknown intermediary in the months before Urakh's assassination
- **The Duke** — informed at some point, never publicly

## What He's Doing

- **Compiling.** Slowly, over the past several years, Arkan has been writing a **dossier** — the original Senex-marriage documents, his own annotations, the Usurer brokers' names, the chain of money. **The dossier exists.** It is somewhere in the Aegis mountains, in a place only Arkan knows.
- **Waiting.** For what is not clear. Possibly for the Rag'narok to make his publication tactically possible. Possibly for the Usurers to make a move that forces his hand. Possibly forever.
- **Living quietly.** He has a life he likes. He does not want to be a hero.

## Relations

- **[[Torguir IV Var-Nokkt]]** — brother; estranged; never publicly named
- **Hildra Var-Nokkt** — sister-in-law; the woman of the marriage at the center
- **The [[Var-Nokkt]] family** — formally dormant member; informally *the one with leverage of his own*
- **[[Senex]]** — distant in-law twice over; family-by-blood and family-by-arrangement
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — monitored threat
- **His Tir-Nâ-Bor hosts** — true family, by the standard he chose
- **[[Master Ôdril Haarken]]** — would-be ally; possibly the reason for Ôdril's disappearance

> [!secret] The succession problem
> If Torguir IV dies without a clear heir surviving him, Cadwë law of peerage descent says the title would pass to **the next Var-Nokkt male of age**. The Var-Nokkt heir-of-record is **Torguir V** (Torguir IV's son). But if Torguir V dies first, or if the dowry-conditions of the Senex marriage are publicly contested, Cadwë law could be argued to **revert succession** to Torguir IV's brother — **Arkan**. The Usurers have spent considerable resources ensuring Torguir V remains healthy. They have spent even more ensuring Arkan stays in Tir-Nâ-Bor.

> [!hook] The dossier extraction
> A free league is approached — through a *very* careful chain of contacts (likely originating with [[Isabeau Soma]] or the Duke's archive) — to travel to the Aegis mountains, **find Arkan**, and bring back **a copy of the dossier**, not Arkan himself. The job is difficult, expensive, and walks the league straight through Usurer surveillance, Tir-Nâ-Bor diplomatic protocol, and Arkan's own willingness (or refusal) to release the documents.

> [!hook] The Cadwë claim
> Arkan's older child is approaching the age of majority by Cadwë reckoning. **A Cadwë claim of peerage** — pressed publicly by a Tir-Nâ-Bor royal relative — would force the city's heralds to rule on whether the child counts. The ruling itself would force Arkan's situation into the public record. The party may be hired to **promote the claim** or to **bury it**. Either job opens the same door.
