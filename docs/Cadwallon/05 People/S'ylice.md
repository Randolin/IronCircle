# S'ylice

> "You came down here looking for a monster. *I am much older than that.* I am what monsters wanted to be when they were children."
> — S'ylice, to a [[Circle of Explorers|Circle]] expedition that reached her in 998

A **living [[Ophidians|Ophidian]]** — one of the very few of her race who did *not* follow [[Vortiris]] into the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] in **255**. She has dwelt in the coiling stone-cut chambers beneath the [[Walled Library]] for **over 700 years**, alone, patient, and — by every account that has survived contact with her — entirely sane. She is **the only living source on what Vortiris is still doing inside the maze**, which makes her the most strategically valuable being beneath Cadwallon and the most dangerous person to be alone with. She is the counterpart to the undead **[[Ss'Alish]]** beneath [[Buoyancy Bay]] — *the same race, the opposite choice.* Where Ss'Alish chose unlife to wait for her god's return, S'ylice chose **simply to remain alive**, which for an Ophidian of her generation is its own act of resistance.

## What She Is

- **A living Ophidian of the pre-exodus generation.** Her people built the temple beneath the city that became Cadwallon; she remembers the temple intact. She remembers the **[[Utopia of the Sphinx|Sphinx]] war** as a thing her elders described to her, not as legend.
- **Not undead.** This is the most important clarifying fact about her. She breathes, eats (rarely, strangely), sleeps in long coils, and **ages — slowly, in Ophidian time, on a timescale Cadwës do not have referents for**. She is not immortal. She is *old in a way only her race could be.*
- **Sapient and rhetorical.** She speaks several Cadwë-era languages, the Cynwäll trade tongue, and old Ophidian. Her conversation is *unsettling* — she uses metaphors that no longer exist, references to celestial events that haven't been visible in the upper sky for centuries, and **commits the listener to their own statements with a precision that is felt rather than noticed.**
- **Not allied with Vortiris.** This is the second most important fact. She stayed *because* she did not follow him. She does not consider him her god. She considers him **a son who failed his father** ([[Desire]]) and **a brother who failed his sister-creatures** (her own kind).

## Why She Stayed

Per a Daïkinee scholarly fragment cross-referenced with the Circle of Explorers' 998 report:

When [[Vortiris]] led the Ophidians into the Labyrinth in 255 — pursuing his father [[Desire]], whose prison was at the maze's heart — **not all Ophidians followed**. A faction believed Vortiris was leading the race into a trap: a war his father did not want fought, in a maze that was specifically designed to contain *him*. This faction split. Most of the splitters were hunted down by the loyalists before the exodus; **S'ylice survived because she went underground first, into the coiling chambers below her people's library**, and **the Cynwäll** — who arrived to settle the peninsula soon after — **chose to seal her in rather than kill her**.

The seal was an **ancient compact**: the Cynwäll would not destroy her; she would not surface; and **she would remain available, in the chambers below the [[Walled Library]], to any party who could reach her with a serious question about the past.** The compact has held for seven centuries.

## What She Knows

- **What Vortiris is doing inside the Labyrinth.** Specifically, *what stage of the plan he is currently on.* She can sense it in the way the deep stone vibrates. She has been tracking it for centuries. **Vortiris is closer to freeing [[Desire]] than he has been at any point since 255.**
- **What the [[Coiling Emissaries]] are.** They are her former kin. The Day of Ashes visits since 999 are **Vortiris's loyalist faction announcing they are nearly ready to surface.**
- **What is in [[Elhan]]'s sealed chamber.** She was here when [[Elhan]] sealed it. She watched him do it. She knows what is inside. She has not said. *She has not been asked correctly.*
- **What the [[Talisman of Shadows]] actually is.** Not a Cadwë or Acheronian artifact in origin — **Ophidian**. Made by her people, taken from a temple antechamber when the [[Soma]] founder broke Drahas's snares at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]]. **Drahas now has it, and S'ylice considers this a serious problem.**

## What She Wants

- **Vortiris stopped.** Not killed — she does not believe a dragon can be killed by the means available to Cadwës — but **contained, delayed, or distracted** until the Rag'narok burns itself out. She is willing to share *targeted* intelligence with Cadwë parties who can act on it.
- **The Talisman of Shadows recovered.** From Drahas. Returned to the Walled Library, where she can re-secure it. She will pay well for this — in information, not in coin.
- **Not to be disturbed casually.** She receives at most one Cadwë visitor a year. The visitor must come with a serious question. **She turns away curiosity-seekers** by simply not speaking when they enter.

## Who Has Reached Her

- **The Order of the Chimera** — Lion-aligned scholars of the [[Walled Library]]; **regular formal contact** maintained through a single appointed envoy who serves until she dismisses them
- **The [[Circle of Explorers]]** — three formal expeditions in the last two centuries; the 998 expedition was the most recent and the report is restricted
- **[[Dyrsin]]** — **never directly.** The ancient Cynwäll compact forbids it. He has reached her through Cadwë proxies.
- **[[Yalin Aoue]]'s parents** — went looking for her in 992; **vanished**. (Whether they reached her, whether she could not save them, whether she chose not to save them, are the questions Yalin's *animae* will not answer.)
- **[[Sophet Drahas]]** — has not reached her. The Walled Library's geometry **resists Court of Shadows agents** in ways the Open Hand [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]] do not fully understand. Drahas has stopped sending people.

## Relations

- **[[Ss'Alish]]** — her opposite-choice counterpart; same race, undead path; **they have not communicated in 700 years**, though both know the other exists
- **[[Vortiris]]** — her god-of-her-people; she rejects him; he does not yet know she is still alive
- **[[Coiling Emissaries]]** — her former kin; **she is appalled by them**
- **[[Dyrsin]]** — counterpart by ancient compact; mutual respect across a relationship that cannot ever be face-to-face
- **The Order of the Chimera** — her official messengers
- **[[Yalin Aoue]]** — has not yet been told her parents went looking for S'ylice. The disclosure is coming.
- **[[Talisman of Shadows]]** — her people's artifact, her current priority

> [!secret] What Yalin's parents found
> The 992 disappearance of [[Yalin Aoue|Yalin's]] parents is connected to S'ylice but **not in the way Yalin will fear**. They reached her. They asked a serious question — about an animae they had encountered in [[The Automaton]] that responded to *Daïkinee* tonal patterns inconsistent with surface forest spirits. S'ylice answered them honestly: the animae was a **fragment of [[Desire]]** working through the Cadwallon grove-network, and the parents had been **selected** by it. The parents understood what this meant and made a choice S'ylice respected: they walked **further into the coiling chambers**, beyond S'ylice's range, **toward the Labyrinth approach**, to **draw the fragment away from their daughter**. They have not returned. They are likely not *dead* in any conventional sense. **They are somewhere in the maze, and they are still drawing it away from Yalin.**

> [!hook] The serious question
> A free league reaches the Walled Library with a question S'ylice considers serious. She answers it. **But the price of the answer is a counter-question she will hold the party to.** The counter-question is specific: *bring me the [[Talisman of Shadows]], or tell me where it is held at this hour*. The league has now contracted with the **oldest living being in Cadwallon** and may not survive the engagement, but the answer they got may be worth it.

> [!hook] The Cynwäll compact, broken
> The Cynwäll-Ophidian compact has held for 700 years. **It is about to be tested.** Either S'ylice surfaces (Vortiris's plans accelerate enough that she has to act), or the Cynwäll attempt to renegotiate the compact (Dyrsin needs more from her than the compact allows), or a Cadwë party **violates** the compact on its own initiative (kidnaps her, kills her, frees her). All three outcomes reshape the underground politics of the city. The free league that *witnesses* the renegotiation — or *causes* the rupture — becomes a permanent player in any post-compact arrangement.

> [!hook] Ss'Alish makes contact
> After seven centuries of silence, the undead Ophidian [[Ss'Alish]] sends a single message to S'ylice — through a [[Circle of Explorers|shrew]] courier hired blind and paid handsomely. **The contents of the message are not shared.** S'ylice reads it, considers, and *answers*. The reply is given to the same courier to deliver back. **Both messages pass through the party's hands.** What the messages say is at the GM's discretion; that the two Ophidians of opposite choices are *now communicating after 700 years* is itself the major event.
