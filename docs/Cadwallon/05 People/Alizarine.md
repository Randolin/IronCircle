# Alizarine

> "I do not tell the future. I tell you what the **cards** think of you. *It is rarely the same thing.*"
> — Alizarine, opening line to first-time clients at the Last Dance

The **resident tarot-reader at the Last Dance cabaret** in [[Heaven Heights]]. Cadwë by birth, **unaffiliated by declaration** — neither Open Hand nor Closed Door — and one of the very few practicing card-readers in the lower city who has **refused recruitment by both [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mage]] factions** for over a decade. Her readings are unusually accurate; her clientele is unusually broad; and the cabaret she works in is owned by **[[Isabeau Soma]]**, which means **everything Alizarine sees passes — at her discretion — to the Soma vendetta operations** that run out of the Last Dance's back rooms.

She is canonically a single line in the People MOC ("tarot reader at the Last Dance cabaret"). What follows is the framework around that line.

## What She Does

- **Tarot readings, paid in coin or favor.** Her standard rate is two ducats a reading; she has been known to take payment in stories, debts, and silence. She refuses to read for the deeply drunk.
- **Hosts a private weekly salon** in a back booth of the Last Dance — invitation only — where readings are deeper, slower, and often connected to operational questions the [[Guild of Blades]] would not otherwise put on paper. **The salon is technically a Soma asset.**
- **Maintains an unofficial register** of every public figure who has ever sat for her — a register she keeps **in her head**, not on paper. This is the asset both tarot-mage factions want her for.

## Why She Refuses the Factions

The Open Hand and Closed Door each want her — not for her card-reading skill (competent but not exceptional) but for **what she has heard from clients across twelve years of public practice in a Soma-owned cabaret in the diplomatic district**. She has read for embassy staff, guild brokers, Drakaër militiamen, Var-Nokkt couriers, and (twice, that she will admit to) **a hooded figure she suspects was a Drahas lieutenant**.

She refuses both factions on the same grounds: **the cards are not for sides**. This may be principled. It may be a position she has been *paid by Isabeau Soma* to hold, since unaligned Alizarine is more useful to the Soma operation than factional Alizarine would be. She has never confirmed or denied.

## What She Probably Knows

- **The Drakaër-lineage card's general location** — she has read for at least two Drakaër-adjacent figures whose readings turned strange in ways suggestive of card-proximity. She has not told [[Albanne Drakaër|Albanne]]. She has not been asked.
- **At least three of Drahas's lieutenants by face** — she has read for them; their readings were the strangest of her career. She remembers the faces. She will not draw them.
- **What [[Master Ôdril Haarken]] was working on** — Ôdril sat for her twice in the year before his disappearance. The second reading was extensive. **She has not shared the contents of that reading with anyone.** Isabeau Soma has not pressed.

## Relations

- **[[Isabeau Soma]]** — landlord, employer, protector. The relationship is professional and quietly affectionate. Alizarine does not know everything Isabeau is doing. Isabeau does not ask everything Alizarine has heard.
- **[[Vanho Soma]]** — has sat for her three times. Has never returned for a fourth. Will not say why.
- **The Open Hand [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]]** — soliciting her for over a decade
- **The Closed Door [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]]** — soliciting her for over a decade; more recently than the Open Hand, more aggressively
- **The Last Dance cabaret** — her workplace and effective home; she lives in a flat above

> [!hook] The reading Ôdril paid for
> A free league hired by [[Isabeau Soma]] to find [[Master Ôdril Haarken|Ôdril]] discovers — through a stage-hand at the Last Dance — that Ôdril's second reading with Alizarine **lasted nearly four hours** and included Alizarine writing things down (highly unusual for her). The notes were taken away by Ôdril. **Or were they?** Alizarine may have kept a copy. She may have given one to Isabeau. She may have done both and not yet decided which to share.

> [!hook] The faction that doesn't take "no"
> The Closed Door tarot-mages have escalated their solicitation: a senior figure makes Alizarine an offer she is going to find harder to refuse than the previous ones. She is asking a small set of trusted people — including the party, if they have read with her before — **what she should do**. Saying *no* to the Closed Door, by 1003, may not be a survivable position.
