]# Yelmor

> "I have been gone two days. Two days. Tell my captain I have not deserted."
> — Yelmor, on his first interview with [[Kal Rajghur|Constable Rajghur]]

A **[[The Dogs of War|Steel Hearts]] dwarf** — Dogs-of-War era — found wandering the catacrypts in early **1003** by a [[Militia]] patrol. He was equipped in 857-pattern Steel Hearts gear, alert, hale, and **convinced he had been underground for two days**. The city he walked out into was **146 years older than the one he left**. He is currently the most carefully-handled prisoner in the Free City: the Duke's archive holds him under medical observation, Constable [[Kal Rajghur]]'s seal on every report.

## What Happened

Per Captain Kelian Durak's report to Constable Rajghur (Secrets vol 1):

- **Year of departure:** 857 — Yelmor enlisted with the **Steel Hearts**, one of the named [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]] companies still operating as a Free-City institution at that time
- **Where he went:** the catacrypts under [[Ghieronburg]], on a contract whose details he has not been pressed for
- **What he experienced:** "a few days," in his own account — possibly less. He remembers a corridor, a turning, his unit ahead of him, and then "the path getting wider."
- **Where he came back:** wandering the catacrypts under [[Ghieronburg]] in 1003, "looking for his unit"
- **Time lost:** **146 years.**

The implication is unambiguous: Yelmor stepped briefly into the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] without realizing it, and the maze's **time-warp** did the rest.

## What He Is Like

A dwarf in his middle prime by ancestry-standards (mid-100s of his expected lifespan), with **no memory loss** of his own life before 857. He remembers his captain, his unit, his contract, his oath. He can describe Cadwallon as it was — the **smaller [[Soma]] before the plague walls**, the **pre-Tractor** lower city, the [[Trophy]] under the *Markropets* rather than the Standing-winds. His descriptions of those places match historical record so well that **the Duke's archivists are using him to correct old maps.**

He is not mad. He is **dislocated** — every social and political reference he reaches for is a century-and-a-half too old. His sense of urgency is operational: he believes his unit is still down there.

## Where He Sits Now

- **Lodged in a guarded chamber** within the Duke's archive in [[Den Azhir]]
- **Interviewed daily** by Kal Rajghur's senior staff — slowly, gently, in the dwarven custom of unhurried truth
- **Not permitted to leave** — partly for his own safety, partly because **what comes out of the Labyrinth is not always what it appears to be**
- The [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]] (both factions) have requested access. **Both have been refused.** The Duke is keeping this quiet.

## What He Wants

- **To find his unit.** Yelmor believes the Steel Hearts are still in the catacrypts, two days behind him, looking for the way back. He is patient, but he is not going to give up on them.
- **To honor his contract.** A Steel Heart who deserts is no Steel Heart. He has asked, repeatedly, to be returned to the catacrypts to find them.
- **To understand what the year is.** He is starting to.

## Relations

- **Captain Kelian Durak** — the [[Militia]] captain who found him; wrote the foundational report
- **[[Kal Rajghur]]** — the Constable who holds Yelmor's file; the only person besides the Duke with full access
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — has personally interviewed Yelmor twice; treats the case with care that surprises his own staff
- **The [[The Dogs of War|Steel Hearts]]** — his unit; possibly still in the Labyrinth, possibly long dead, possibly something else entirely now
- **The [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mages]]** — both factions want him; he is the first **physical evidence** in living memory that the corridors lead somewhere recoverable

> [!secret] The other Steel Hearts
> Yelmor is not the only one who left in 857. **There were 41 Steel Hearts on that contract.** Captain Durak's report identifies him as **"Yelmor of the Steel Hearts"** — singular, not "one of." If the maze's time-warp lets people come back at different intervals, the others may still emerge: tomorrow, in fifty years, never. Or all at once. The Duke's archive has begun quietly screening every catacrypt-found dwarf against the Steel Hearts roster.

> [!hook] The escort back
> Yelmor has petitioned the Duke for an escort back into the catacrypts to find his unit. The Duke has not refused. **A free league with deep-underground credentials and the Duke's confidence** could be that escort. The destination is the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]. The risk is that if Yelmor came out 146 years late, those who go in with him may come out somewhere else entirely.

> [!hook] The tarot-mage approach
> A Closed Door [[The Tarot-Mages|tarot-mage]] reaches out privately. The faction wants ten minutes alone with Yelmor — not to harm him, but to **read** him. The Open Hand has gotten wind of the request and is making its own counter-offer. The party can broker, sabotage, or take a side. The information at stake may be **what the corridors look like from the inside** — knowledge no living surface dweller has.

> [!hook] What he is
> Yelmor knows nothing about the [[Coiling Emissaries]], the [[Tractor]], or the *current* Steel Hearts blazon flying at the [[Great Hall]]. **He should know nothing about** the Garden of Desire either — but in his third week of interviews, asked about "the sound of running water" in the catacrypts, he describes the warm outfalls beneath the oasis (see [[The Magical Invasion]]). **He should not be able to know about those.** Either he stepped further into the Labyrinth than he remembers, or something came out with him.
