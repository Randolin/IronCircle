> The free leaguer who became Duke. Eight years in, still ruling through his friends instead of his family.

## Canon Facts
- **Duke of Cadwallon** since 995 (eight years in office at current year 1003).
- **Former free leaguer** of the [[Ruby-Heart]] before his elevation.
- **Adopted son of [[Camelia Orrkrk]]**, Peer of [[The Rampart]]. The conditions of the adoption are explicitly kept secret.
- Rules through a circle of "faithful companions" rather than blood ties:
  - **[[Kal Rajghur]]** — Constable, head of militia, former free leaguer.
  - **[[Silent Scyrnyth]]** — Steward, runs [[Den Azhir]] fief day-to-day.
- Still a bachelor; his eventual marriage will have major political consequences across embassies and houses.
- Commands the militia, can summon the free leagues, and can request the Peers' assistance.

> [!secret] The adoption
> The conditions of Den Azhir's adoption by Camelia Orrkrk remain unknown to the city. Camelia is the only living person with the full answer. The truth could destabilize his rule — or cement it permanently.

> [!hook] The bachelor question
> Every noble house and embassy wants the Duke married to their candidate. He hasn't picked one in eight years. Why?

> [!hook] The Ruby-Heart connection
> The free league he came from still exists. Old comrades have unique access to the Duke — and unique leverage.
