# Johann of Drakaër

> "I have ridden a dragon. I will not pretend it did not change me."
> — Attributed to Johann at his elevation, 843

One of [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]]'s **twenty-one lieutenants** in the [[The Dogs of War|Dogs of War]]. Originally **Johann of [[Alahan]]** — a Barhan soldier of the Light who joined the company before the founding. Befriended the [[Cynwäll]] elves who watched the city's emergence from the [[Cynwäll Ruins|ruined elven site]] and, in **843**, was granted the **honor of riding a dragon** — a privilege almost never bestowed on a human. He **changed his surname to Drakaër** in commemoration and founded the noble lineage that holds the [[Drakaër]] fief to this day.

## What the Sources Establish

- **A lieutenant of the Dogs of War.** One of the twenty-one; received one of the [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]] at the founding.
- **Originally of [[Alahan]].** A Barhan soldier — "Barhan" being Alahan's military culture-term. The Cadwë language preserves Barhan grammar partly because of the Dogs of War lieutenants who spoke it natively, Johann among them.
- **Granted the original "Pillars" fief** at the post-founding allocation — the upper-city fief built around the **feet of the Cynwäll Tower**, then called simply "the Pillars" before it took the Drakaër name
- **843 — the dragon-riding privilege.** The Cynwäll, who watched the [[Dogs of War]] from their Tower and judged the new city's worthiness, **granted Johann the right to ride a dragon** — a privilege the Cynwäll reserve almost exclusively for their own. The Timeline records the year; the source records the act; **the reason has never been published.**
- **Changed his surname to Drakaër.** "Drakaër" means **"dragon-marked"** in old Cadwë. He took it after the dragon-riding. The fief's name followed: the Pillars became *Drakaër*.
- **His Barhan moral values bred resentment.** The Cadwë sources record (without quite saying it outright) that Johann's *adherence to Barhan moral codes* — discipline, honor-duels, Light-faith — annoyed his fellow Cadwës then and **still annoys their descendants now**. The Drakaër stigma traces straight to him.

## Why the Cynwäll Chose Him

> [!secret] The dragon-rider question
> The Cynwäll do not grant dragon-riding lightly. The standard reasons (great deeds, blood relation, magical accomplishment) don't fully fit Johann. He was a brave lieutenant of a successful campaign — but so were the other twenty Dogs of War lieutenants, none of whom received dragons. The granular truth, known to [[Dyrsin]] and to almost nobody else, is that **Johann discovered something in the [[Cynwäll Ruins]] he didn't keep for himself**. Specifically: an **artifact, document, or piece of Cynwäll knowledge** that the company could have used or sold, which Johann **returned to the Cynwäll voluntarily**. The dragon was the Cynwäll thank-you. What he found has never been publicly named.

## The Founder's Legacy

The current Drakaër family inherits:

- **The dragon-riding tradition** — a Drakaër who establishes the right relationship with the [[Cynwäll]] can still petition for dragon-rider status. **None has been granted in two generations.** Albanne's interest is publicly known.
- **The Cynwäll alliance** — all Drakaër Peers honor the ancestral alliance with the Cynwäll. The Pillars were rebuilt with Cynwäll help after the [[The Great Flood|900 flood]]. [[Dyrsin]]'s Tower is in their fief.
- **The Barhan moral streak** — modern Drakaërs are still notably Barhan-disciplined; [[Albanne Drakaër|Albanne]] holds public Field-of-Honor duels in the Free District, an unusually Barhan civic gesture
- **The cavalry obsession** — a horse-soldier tradition kept up in a vertical city of suspended bridges and airships. It would be ridiculous if it weren't so consistently effective.
- **One of the 22 arcana** — Johann received one card at the founding. **It is no longer in the family.** The card was lost or sold during one of the family's lean centuries. *Which* card it was is *not* publicly known; the Drakaër archive's record was destroyed in a Crimson Dress fire two centuries ago, and **the family has been quietly looking for it ever since.**

## Why He Still Matters

- **The dragon-riding privilege is dormant, not revoked.** Albanne knows this. Silnus knows this. The Cynwäll know this. Whether to reactivate it is a current Drakaër family question.
- **The lost card haunts the family.** The Drakaër lineage card is one of the 22, and the family has never stopped trying to recover it. **[[Sophet Drahas|Drahas]] does not currently hold it** — the inventory of his collection is good enough to confirm that. But it could be anywhere on Aarklash.
- **The Drakaër stigma traces to him.** Whenever a senior Cadwë wants to insult the family, the line is "they still think they're Alahan." Johann's Barhan values are the deep reason.

## Relations

- **[[Vanius the Founder]]** — captain; the man who let him keep what the Cynwäll gave him
- **[[Khaurik]]** — fellow lieutenant; the two were close. The **Drakaër-Khaurik Argam godfather tradition** dates from Khaurik personally entrusting his eldest son to Johann's care.
- **The [[Cynwäll]]** — his patrons; the people whose dragon he rode
- **[[Dyrsin]]** — the only Cynwäll in Cadwallon today who personally knew Johann (in the Cynwäll way that elves remember founders)
- **[[Alahan]]** — his birth nation; the source of his Barhan values; not in close diplomatic contact with the family today
- **The Drakaër lineage card** — the missing piece

> [!hook] The Cynwäll testimony
> A Drakaër historian (working for Silnus) approaches the party with a Cynwäll-witnessed account of Johann's original deed — the one that won him the dragon. The account is **sealed by Cynwäll convention** and can only be unsealed in the presence of a Cynwäll witness with dragon-riding standing. The only Cynwäll in Cadwallon with that standing is [[Dyrsin]]. **Getting an audience with Dyrsin** is the actual quest.

> [!hook] The lost card
> A free league turns up a tarot card with the **Drakaër founding sigil** etched into the corner — handled, weathered, possibly traded across half of Aarklash since the family lost it. The card is one of the 22. Returning it to [[Albanne Drakaër|Albanne]] would be a service. **Keeping it** would be a power. **Selling it to the Cynwäll** might be… something else entirely.
