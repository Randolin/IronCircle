# Urakh Khaurik Argam

> "I am responsible for my clan, my fief, and my house. **I am not yet finished with any of them.**"
> — Urakh, on his coronation day, 991

Peer of [[Ogrokh]] from **991 to 1002**. Father of [[Kurn Khaurik Argam|Kurn]]. **Assassinated** in early 1002 — and the family's vendetta protocols **failed to activate**, an unprecedented breakdown that the Cadwë underworld has not yet stopped examining. His goblin-godfather **Dazomet** moved to regency over the young Kurn within weeks. The Duke himself eventually broke the regency. Urakh's grave is at the **Mar-An-Org war academy** in [[The Incarnat]], guarded around the clock.

## What He Was

- **The first Khaurik Argam Peer in two generations to be raised entirely in [[Ogrokh]]** rather than fostered abroad. His view of the ogre clans was operational, not sentimental.
- **A reformer.** Pushed back the [[Guild of Blades]]' lease terms on Thunder Vale forges (won partial concessions); modernized the Chains-and-Stone amphitheater's debate procedures (made it harder for Var-Nokkt money to swing rulings); started the still-incomplete project to **bring the Mar-An-Org war academy under direct Peer authority** rather than independent clan oversight. The reforms made him enemies in every ogre clan **and** every guild.
- **Wielded the warhammer.** The same warhammer Kurn now possesses ceremonially. Urakh wielded it in two public duels and won both. The hammer's enchantment was alive under him; it recognized the rightful Khaurik Argam.

## How He Was Killed

The known facts:

- **Date:** early 1002, exact day publicly recorded but the family contests it
- **Location:** Urakh's private chambers in [[The Incarnat]], inside the Khaurik Argam ogre clan keep
- **Method:** poison, in his evening meal, **administered by someone who had passed three layers of the keep's security**
- **The vendetta protocols failed.** Khaurik Argam vendetta protocols — the magical-and-procedural framework that automatically identifies a Peer's killer and binds the family to retaliation — **did not activate**. Why is the central mystery.

The protocols had not failed in **twelve generations**. The most plausible theories:
- The killer was someone **the protocols recognized as authorized to be there** (a household member, an oath-sworn ally)
- The protocols themselves were **disabled or suborned** before the killing, by someone with magical access to the keep
- **Dazomet** had access to both the household and the protocols. The Duke's investigation has not formally implicated him. The Duke's investigation has not stopped, either.

## What He Was Trying to Do When He Died

In the last months of his life, Urakh was negotiating **simultaneously** with three different parties on what looked like three different files. Cross-referencing his calendar against his correspondence after death revealed that **all three negotiations were about the same thing**:

- A discreet exchange with [[Master Ôdril Haarken]] of the [[Guild of Goldsmiths|Goldsmiths]]
- A back-channel to the [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] dwarven embassy contacts at [[Var-Nokkt]]
- A direct (and risky) approach to **Arkan Var-Nokkt's hidden network** (through what intermediary is not known)

What the three parties shared was **knowledge of the Usurer–Var-Nokkt leverage**. Urakh, alone among the Peers, had **figured out what the Usurers hold over the Var-Nokkts**, and was building a coalition to act on it. **The assassination almost certainly relates.** *(see [[Master Ôdril Haarken]], [[Arkan Var-Nokkt]])*

## Relations

- **[[Kurn Khaurik Argam]]** — son; was sixteen at Urakh's death; inherited the peerage; **does not remember some months of 1002**
- **[[Khaurik]]** — distant founding ancestor; Urakh was the first Peer in three generations to publicly visit his memorial unaccompanied
- **Dazomet** — goblin godfather; ascendant during the regency; **chief suspect by every metric, never formally named**
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — broke Dazomet's regency; treats the Urakh case as an open file
- **[[Master Ôdril Haarken]]** — the surviving fragment of Urakh's coalition; **knows things he has not been able to safely tell anyone**

> [!secret] The sealed contract
> A document exists in the [[Guild of Scribes|Scribes']] archive: a **sealed copy of an assassination contract from 1001**, commissioning Urakh's killing. The seal is unfamiliar to modern Cadwë heraldry — **it is an Acheronian seal, from a barony other than [[Acheron|Taer Haez]]**. (Drahas did not order this.) The implication is enormous: **someone in Acheron, not aligned with Drahas's Court of Shadows, has been operating in Cadwallon at the level of commissioning Peer assassinations.** [[Feyd Mantis]] is a possible signatory. So is one of his lieutenants. The Scribes do not know they hold this document; it is filed under a misindexed name.

> [!hook] What Kurn doesn't remember
> Dazomet's regency lasted **seven months**. Kurn remembers **two**. A senior ogre clan elder wants the missing five reconstructed — through forensic inquiry, magical investigation, or simply by finding the agents who were in the room. *(see [[Kurn Khaurik Argam]])*

> [!hook] The warhammer remembers
> A free leaguer who tests Urakh's warhammer — the one Kurn cannot yet wield — discovers the enchantment was **reset shortly before Urakh's assassination**. The hammer would have *named the killer* if active. Resetting it required access to **both the keep and the enchantment's binding ritual** — knowledge limited to the Mar-An-Org clan elders and a handful of Sihirs. The list of suspects is short. The list of survivors of that list is shorter.
