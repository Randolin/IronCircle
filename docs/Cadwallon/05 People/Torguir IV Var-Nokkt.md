# Torguir IV Var-Nokkt

> "We have run this fief like a guild for three generations. I am the fourth Torguir. I am also possibly the last."
> — Torguir IV, in a sealed letter found in the Var-Nokkt archive (1003)

Peer of [[Var-Nokkt]]. Descendant of **Duke Koliandre**. Head of the **Council of Elders** that runs the dwarven Var-Nokkt family **as if it were a guild**. The fourth Torguir to hold the peerage. Currently the most quietly-besieged Peer in the upper city — between [[Master Ôdril Haarken]]'s public challenge, his brother [[Arkan Var-Nokkt|Arkan]]'s disappearance, and the [[Guild of Usurers|Usurer]] leverage that may have caused both.

## What He Looks Like

A Tir-Nâ-Bor-descent dwarf in his early sixties — vigorous by dwarven standards, **stocky, broad-shouldered, white-bearded with iron-grey streaks**. Wears Var-Nokkt formal dress (deep blue and brass) in council; working dwarven leathers in private. **Hands callused** from forge-work he still does ceremonially. Speaks slowly, weighs every word.

## How the Family Runs

The Var-Nokkt family is **not run as a noble house**. It is run as **a chartered guild**, with Torguir as Grand Master and his closest advisors as the **Council of Elders**:

- **Torguir IV** — Peer; overall direction
- **Hildra** — Torguir's wife; supervises judiciary activities of the fief; quietly an honorary [[Guild of Dressmakers|Dressmakers']] council member
- **Arkan** — Torguir's brother; **was in charge of family treasure**; **disappeared mysteriously after a corruption scandal**
- **Torguir V** — heir; internal affairs, relations with duchy, guilds, and leagues
- **Lyfha** — daughter; relations with embassies
- **Ryankha** — daughter; **married to the first son of the [[Senex]]** (dwarf king of the Ægis); guarantees the Tir-Nâ-Bor link. Mother of **Ardhun**, the young Cadwallon-resident dwarf sent from Tir-Nâ-Bor.
- **Krondnir** — Arkan's son; leads the family troops under Torguir V's authority

## What He's Carrying

- The **Var-Nokkt-Goldsmith alliance** that has been the spine of upper-city financial power for two generations
- The Var-Nokkts' **Tir-Nâ-Bor connections** through Ryankha's marriage
- The unpaid moral debt to dwarven refugees the family has historically exploited (the Var-Nokkts famously regard Tir-Nâ-Bor refugees as "frustrated barbarians destined to live under the upper city")
- **Whatever the [[Guild of Usurers]] holds over him** — which Master Ôdril Haarken has publicly demanded to know

## What He Doesn't Talk About

> [!secret] Arkan's disappearance
> **Torguir knows where Arkan is.** Or rather: Torguir helped Arkan disappear. The "corruption scandal" was a cover. Arkan was about to expose what the Usurers hold over the family — including **specific documents naming Torguir personally** — and Torguir, his wife Hildra, and Lyfha collectively decided to send Arkan into **deep hiding in the Tir-Nâ-Bor mountains**. The **Senex III airship's unofficial drops** in the Quithayran mountains are the route. Arkan is alive. Arkan has not yet decided whether to come back.

> [!secret] What the Usurers actually hold
> The Usurers hold proof that **the Var-Nokkt-Senex marriage of Ryankha to the Senex's son was bought**, not negotiated — that the Var-Nokkts paid the Senex a massive secret bribe that, if known, would void the marriage, end the alliance, and likely cost Ryankha her position. **The Senex knows.** The Senex chose to take the bribe. Both families are complicit. Master Ôdril's public question — *what do the Usurers hold over Var-Nokkt?* — is the question that, if answered, ends two ruling families simultaneously.

## Relations

- **[[Guild of Goldsmiths]]** — primary alliance; cracking
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — leverage holder; the central deadlock of Torguir's life
- **[[Master Ôdril Haarken]]** — the man who asked the question; Torguir does not yet know whether to bribe Ôdril, kill Ôdril, or pre-empt Ôdril by going public himself
- **[[Senex]]** — Tir-Nâ-Bor dwarven king; complicit
- **Ryankha** — daughter; does not know the marriage was bought
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — formally cordial; the Duke knows too much and is waiting
