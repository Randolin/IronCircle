# Bernadil Ghiéron

> "My grandfather built this fief from cursed ground. Anyone who says we don't belong is welcome to repeat it to his face. He's still buried here."
> — Bernadil at the annual Denerac feast

Peer of [[Ghieronburg]]. Eldest grandson of **Commander Denerac Ghiéron** — the foreign general who saved Cadwallon from the **Water Elemental invasion of 956** and was ennobled in reward. Charismatic, roughly thirty, **publicly the face of a family that knows it doesn't fit in the upper city** and is determined to make that not matter.

## What He Looks Like

A human in his late twenties, tall and broad-shouldered with the Ghiéron family's characteristic dark hair and prominent jaw. **Energetic, almost performatively so.** Dresses well — perhaps slightly *too* well for someone of his actual social standing, which is part of the inferiority complex the senior families gently mock. Smiles too readily. Speaks well in public. Less guarded than a Peer should be.

## What His Family Is

The **Ghiéron family** descends from Commander Denerac. They are:

- **All human** — no inter-cultural marriages in three generations
- **Lacking culture and elegance** by upper-city standards — their attempts at grand gestures regularly come off as tacky
- **Carrying a serious inferiority complex** that masks **great ambition and a cunning mind**
- **Dynamic and demagogic** — they keep direct contact with their subjects, which the older noble houses find vulgar
- **Vulnerable to flattery** — crooked schemes have worked on them before (see [[Ghieronburg|Tamarez Canal scandal]])

## How the Family Runs

Each Ghiéron handles their own sphere with substantial independence:

- **Bernadil** — Peer, public face, central political authority
- **Gowayn** — finance (Bernadil's brother)
- **Isabess** — relations with noble houses (Gowayn's wife; the actual diplomat of the family)
- **Martolbe** — relations with guilds and leagues
- **Thismee** and **Ombeline** (cousins) — magical phenomena, Sihirs, Voyagers
- **Adalban** ("Adaldan") — military, Camp Valiant on Wyvern Road, **the family's last living direct child of Denerac**. Long-running personal hatred with [[Silnus Drakaër]].

## His Real Project

Bernadil's quiet long-term goal is to **make the Ghiéron family equal to the older houses** — not by mimicking them (which the Tamarez Canal scandal proved he cannot do) but by **leveraging Ghieronburg's actual unique asset**: the fief is genuinely magical. [[Fayrees]], Elementals, magical Familiars *live there*. **No other peer can say this.** Bernadil intends to make Ghieronburg the magical-creature diplomatic hub of the city.

This is why he has not moved against **Baron Mardi** despite 41 years of Mardi extracting unclear concessions: **Mardi's Truce works**, and Bernadil needs it to work for the long-game plan.

## What He Doesn't Know

> [!secret] The Baron Mardi exchange
> Bernadil knows Mardi takes *something* in exchange for the Truce. He believes it's prestige, or political leverage, or possibly a quiet vote at the Mardi-private rituals every decade. **The truth is that Mardi takes a percentage of Ghiéron family children's dreams** — specifically, he siphons fragments of *ambition* from young Ghiérons during sleep. This is why every Ghiéron has the same combination of inferiority complex and over-stretched ambition: Mardi has been *shaping the family bloodline* for forty-one years. The next Truce renewal will be the one Bernadil is asked to host personally.

## Relations

- **[[Silnus Drakaër]]** — uncle Adalban's personal enemy; Bernadil has inherited a manageable but real cold war with the entire Drakaër military faction
- **[[Albanne Drakaër]]** — Albanne is the head of the family that hates Bernadil's uncle. The two have never met privately. Both would consider it useful. The Drakaër-Ghiéron-Orrkrk mediation is one of the [[The Rampart|Rampart]]'s standing diplomatic projects.
- **Baron Mardi** — *the question of his reign*
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — the Duke supported Bernadil's accession; Bernadil is loyal in return
- **The Sihirs and Voyagers** managed by Thismee and Ombeline — strong, growing
