> Matriarch of House Orrkrk. Adoptive mother to the Duke. Holder of secrets.

## Canon Facts
- **Peer of [[The Rampart]]**, head of House Orrkrk.
- Adopted [[Duke Den Azhir|Den Azhir]] when he was younger. **The conditions of the adoption remain secret to this day.**
- House Orrkrk also maintains a secondary villa on the Bridges in [[Var-Nokkt]] — they refuse to leave their core fief but keep a foothold among the merchants.
