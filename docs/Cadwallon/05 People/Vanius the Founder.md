# Vanius the Founder

> "Sir, these remains please you so little that I make them my kingdom for a ducat!"
> — Vanius, throwing a battered gold coin at the feet of an Alahan emissary

The first Duke of Cadwallon. Captain of the [[Dogs of War]]. Incarnation of [[Desire]] for one day. Wielder of the original [[The 22 Arcana|22 arcana]]. The man who **walked into the [[Labyrinth]] of his own free will** to find the sleeping god — and never returned.

In Cadwë folk-tradition he is a hero, near-saint, founding father. **In actual cosmic fact he is far more complicated**: he made a deal with the city's worst enemy, he handed [[Sophet Drahas]] the Emperor card, and he is — as far as anyone knows — **still alive somewhere in the maze under the city**.

## What the Cadwës Know

- He led 200 mercenaries — the **Dogs of War**, organized into **21 free companies under 21 lieutenants** — into the cursed jungle of [[Lanever]]
- Cleared the ruins of the Cynwäll city
- Faced the Acheronian forces under [[Sophet Drahas]] at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]]
- Won — miraculously, with the appearance of 22 magical tarot cards
- Was offered titles by [[Alahan]] but rejected both kingship (would offend the Alliance) and barony (would submit to it). Chose **Duke** — an old Barhan military rank — instead.
- Carved the first fiefdoms in **835**, gave each of his surviving lieutenants a fief, established the [[Houses of Peers|original Peerage]]
- Founded the [[Free Leagues]] (833 Charter) and the **Charter of the Fiefdoms** (835)
- Disappeared after a few years of rule, leaving an appointed successor

## What the Senior Insiders Know

> [!secret] The 832 incarnation
> When [[Sophet Drahas]]'s Acheronian army nearly took the Cynwäll ruins, [[Desire]] woke briefly in his prison. **He mistook the necromancer's spells for an Ophidian intrusion**, panicked, and chose Vanius — the most charismatic mortal nearby — as his [[Incarnates|Incarnation]]. Desire spread his **22 divine elixirs** into the 22 cards of the tarot deck Vanius happened to be carrying. Each card became one of the arcana. Vanius wielded them at the Battle of the Wall of Earth.
> Desire then exhausted himself and returned to sleep.
> **Vanius retained traces of the Incarnation.** Even after the god withdrew, he remained subtly altered — more charismatic, more aware of certain currents, more compelled to *act* on the city's behalf.

> [!secret] The Pact with Drahas
> Vanius discovered after the battle that one of the cards — **the Emperor** — depicted [[Sophet Drahas]]. He kept two cards for himself and gave one to each of 20 lieutenants.
> Then, secretly, he met Drahas and offered him the Emperor in exchange for Drahas's promised support against **[[Feyd Mantis]]**. Drahas accepted. *(This is the foundational original sin of Cadwallon. The Soma vendetta, the Usurer-Drahas alliance, the entire shadow architecture of the city — all of it descends from this one pact.)*

> [!secret] The Walk into the Maze
> Vanius appointed a successor as Duke and entered the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]] to find Desire's realm — apparently believing he could complete what the brief Incarnation had begun. He had two cards with him. He has been **lost in the maze ever since**.
> Time runs strangely in the Labyrinth. To Vanius, it has possibly been weeks. To Cadwallon, it has been **over 170 years**.
> He is presumed alive. Recent fragments of evidence (handwriting on a card recovered by a free leaguer; sealed messages found in the catacrypts; the dwarf [[Yelmor]]'s confused testimony) suggest **he is still wandering, still trying to reach Desire's prison, and possibly still capable of communicating outward** through the cards.

## His Appearance

Reconstructed from the ducal archives and the Dogs of War survivors' testimony:

- Human, late 30s at the time of the founding
- Of mixed origin — Barhan father, possibly Kel-blooded mother
- "Disturbingly charismatic" even before the Incarnation
- A swordsman of distinction; not a magician by training but proved capable with the arcana
- Wore the Dogs of War colors (a dog's-head sigil in red on black) and a battered campaign cloak
- Spoke five languages including some Cynwäll and rough Daïkinee
- Famously **proud** — the trait that lost him the [[The 22 Arcana|Emperor card]] when his pride pushed him into the secret meeting with Drahas

## Why He Matters Now

- The Duke ([[Duke Den Azhir|Den Azhir]]) is the first Duke in living memory who **takes the original mission seriously**: collecting the scattered cards back into a deck, in case Vanius needs them
- [[The Tarot-Mages]] split partly over what to do if Vanius is found alive
- The card with handwriting on the back — recovered recently by a free leaguer — is being authenticated. If genuine, Vanius is *actively reaching out*
- His successor lineage (the original Duke II, Lothar, was a quiet appointee) has been broken multiple times; Den Azhir is the first Duke since Vanius who is *not* a direct line descendant of one of the original lieutenants

> [!hook] The handwriting
> A free leaguer recovered a card from the catacrypts with handwriting on the back that **matches the ducal archive samples of Vanius**. He wants six more cards delivered to specific locations. The Duke wants to know if it's real. The senior tarot-mages want to know first. Sophet Drahas wants the courier dead.

> [!hook] The cards as compass
> If Vanius wants to be found, he can be — *but only by someone holding several of his cards at once*. Reassembling the deck is the literal path back to him. This is the buried structure of any major arcana-recovery campaign: the cards are not just weapons. They are a map.

> [!hook] The walking thing
> Something has been seen in the deep catacrypts. Tall, hooded, bearing a sword of an outdated style. It has not attacked anyone. Several witnesses are dead or insane. The senior cartomancers think it might not be Vanius. **They think it might be what's left of him.**
