# People

The named figures of Cadwallon. Peers, Duke, antagonists, lieutenants, free leaguers, and the dead-but-relevant.

## The Duchy

- [[Duke Den Azhir]] — the current Duke; former [[Ruby-Heart]] free leaguer; dramatis (secret)
- [[Vanius the Founder]] — first Duke; lost in the [[The Labyrinth|Labyrinth]]; possibly still alive
- [[Khaurik]] — ogre founder of [[Houses of Peers|House Khaurik Argam]]; died 832
- [[Johann of Drakaër]] — Vanius's lieutenant, dragon-rider (843); founder of [[Drakaër]]
- [[Elhan]] — the elf who led the exile that founded the Cynwäll city beneath Cadwallon
