# Aghovar Soma

> "He was the brother who could look Drahas in the eye. We forgot that meant Drahas could look back."
> — Vanho Soma, in private to a senior magician

The **middle** of the three Soma brothers and the **previous Grand Master of the [[Guild of Thieves]]**. Killed by [[Sophet Drahas]] personally, in an act so brazen that the city's underworld is still recalibrating around the wound. **The Devil card** and the **[[Talisman of Shadows]]** were taken from his body. The Soma family vendetta — against Drahas and the [[Guild of Usurers]] — has its second and deepest origin in him.

## The Inheritance He Was

Born middle of three brothers. By Cadwë inheritance custom, that meant he was never going to be Peer of [[Soma]] — that fell to his eldest brother [[Vanho Soma|Anon]] (and then to his youngest brother Vanho, after the wounds). Aghovar's path was the **guild track**, and he excelled.

- **Joined the [[Guild of Thieves]]** as a young man — the house's traditional path for non-heir Soma sons
- **Rose to Grand Master** — by his early forties; the youngest in the guild's modern history
- **Held the [[Talisman of Shadows]]** — the Soma family's hereditary protective artifact, traditionally passed to whichever brother held the most dangerous post
- **Held the [[Devil card]]** — one of the 22 [[The 22 Arcana|arcana]], inherited from the Soma founding line. *Two cards in one hand is unusual.* That he had both was widely known in the underworld.

## How He Died

Drahas killed him **personally**, in a meeting Aghovar agreed to. The site of the killing is not public knowledge — the body was returned, the meeting was denied, and the **Talisman of Shadows + the Devil card** went with Drahas into the [[Court of Ashes]]. The Soma family's public position is that Aghovar was murdered in a robbery; the family's *private* knowledge runs all the way to Drahas's throne.

The Cadwë underworld read it as **a declaration of war**. Drahas had operated in the shadows for centuries; killing the Grand Master of the Guild of Thieves face-to-face was a step into the light, and a calculated one. Whatever Drahas wanted from Aghovar — the cards, the Talisman, *information* — he wanted enough to surface for it.

## What He Was Like

The Cadwë underworld remembers Aghovar as **the brother who could negotiate with anyone**. Where eldest brother **Anon** was a public diplomat and youngest **[[Vanho Soma|Vanho]]** is a warrior-mage, Aghovar was the family's *operator* — fluent in three languages, on first-name terms with smugglers and embassy attachés alike, comfortable in the catacrypts and at Whitehaven. **He could look Drahas in the eye.** That was, in the end, why Drahas killed him.

## Relations

- **[[Vanho Soma]]** — his younger brother; now Peer, now alone with the vendetta
- **Anon Soma** — his elder brother; assassinated by Usurer agents (the first wound); died first
- **[[Isabeau Soma]]** — niece (Anon's daughter); inherited the active hunt against Drahas
- **Sayuri Soma** — half-elf cousin; runs Cameon Mansion; was close to Aghovar
- **[[Sophet Drahas]]** — his killer
- **The [[Guild of Thieves]]** — his guild; still divided over whether his death is properly avenged

> [!secret] What Drahas took from him
> Drahas killed Aghovar **for the Talisman of Shadows**, not the Devil card. The card was a *bonus*. The Talisman is what Drahas needed: **it stabilizes the curse Feyd Mantis laid on him** at the founding, letting Drahas reach further from the [[Court of Ashes]] than he could otherwise. The Devil card is what Drahas *wanted* — one of the 22, and a specific one whose meaning resonates with the Court of Shadows. Aghovar suspected this and tried to negotiate access without surrendering either artifact. He miscalculated Drahas's patience by approximately one meeting.

> [!hook] Aghovar's vault
> A senior Soma magician has located what may be **Aghovar's personal vault** — an unmarked door in the catacrypts under [[Soma]]. It is rumored to contain his **operational notes from the months before his death**: who he met, what he learned about Drahas, what the Talisman was actually capable of. **Vanho has not yet been told the vault has been found.** The magician fears Vanho's pride will push the family into open war the moment he reads the notes. *(see [[Vanho Soma]])*

> [!hook] The man returned
> Someone matching Anon Soma's description has been seen at night in [[Heaven Heights]]. He is not a ghost. **If Anon can come back, can Aghovar?** A free league quietly hired to investigate the Anon sighting may stumble on a second figure — Aghovar, also returned, and very deliberately *not* approaching his family.
