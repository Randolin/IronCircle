# Vanho Soma

> "I bear two ghosts on my shoulders. They have not yet been avenged."
> — Said to one of his magicians, on the anniversary of Anon's death

Peer of [[Soma]]. Warrior-mage, "disturbingly handsome," pride burning under his composure. The third Soma to inherit the peerage in his lifetime — both of his elder twin brothers were murdered, and **he carries the entire Soma vendetta against the [[Guild of Usurers]] and [[Sophet Drahas]] alone**.

## The Inheritance

Vanho is the youngest of three brothers:

- **Anon Soma** — eldest. Head of the family. Killed by [[Guild of Usurers|Usurer]] assassins. The first wound.
- **Aghovar Soma** — middle. Grand Master of the [[Guild of Thieves]]. Killed by [[Sophet Drahas]] himself, who took the **Devil card** and the **[[Talisman of Shadows]]** from him. The second wound.
- **Vanho Soma** — youngest. Now Peer.

He inherited too young, too fast, and twice over. The court of Soma is heavy with absences.

## Who He Is

- **Trained warrior-mage** — both martial and esoteric tradition. The Soma family's "esoteric traditions" run multi-generationally; Vanho's magicians are careful never to display their full power.
- **Pride first** — his composure is iron, but a Soma never forgets a slight. The vendetta is not just *family duty* to him; it is *personal*.
- **Patron of the bohemian quarter** — the Soma family keeps Heaven Heights alive as the artistic district. Vanho personally funds three painters, two poets, and the Last Dance cabaret (which is technically owned by his niece [[Isabeau Soma|Isabeau]]).
- **Diplomatically careful** — knows the Soma family cannot win the vendetta in open conflict and that the Duke needs the upper city stable

## Relations

- **[[Isabeau Soma]]** (niece) — Anon's daughter. Operates as a [[Guild of Blades]] senior, hunts Drahas personally. Vanho funds her quietly.
- **[[Sayuri Soma]]** (half-elf cousin) — runs the Cameon Mansion magic school in Heaven Heights
- **[[Brehnan of Alahan]]** — Alahan ambassador at Whitehaven; long-standing diplomatic ally
- **[[Sophet Drahas]]** — primary enemy
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — open vendetta
- **[[Duke Den Azhir]]** — uneasy ally. The Duke needs Soma stability; Vanho needs the Duke's protection.
