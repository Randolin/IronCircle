# Senex

> "I was given to Cadwallon as a gift. The wrapping has not yet fallen away."
> — Senex, in private to her daughter Hildra, on the morning of Hildra's own wedding

A dwarf of the **Senex royal line of [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]]**, married into the **[[Var-Nokkt]] family** two generations ago. Mother of **Hildra Var-Nokkt** (the present judiciary; wife of [[Torguir IV Var-Nokkt]]). **The marriage that brought her to Cadwallon was secretly brokered and paid for by the [[Guild of Usurers]]** — the founding act of the Usurer–Var-Nokkt leverage that quietly holds Cadwallon's mercantile upper city in compliance.

Senex herself is **not** complicit. She is the *instrument*, not the agent. But she knows — has known for decades — and **she has not told anyone**. Including, until very recently, her own daughter.

## Who She Is

- **A dwarven noblewoman of Tir-Nâ-Bor's royal line.** Senex is a member of the dwarven royal house, sister to a senior figure in the Aegis Mountains court, second cousin to multiple Tir-Nâ-Bor diplomats currently housed at Castel Var-Nokkt. Her presence in Cadwallon is **the durable expression of the Tir-Nâ-Bor–Var-Nokkt alliance** that keeps dwarven trade flowing through the city.
- **In her late second century** by dwarven reckoning (dwarves of Tir-Nâ-Bor lineage live three centuries comfortably). To the Cadwës she looks **late middle-aged**, white-streaked, dignified. To other dwarves she is *young to middle*.
- **A scholar of dwarven jurisprudence** in her own right — the discipline she practices privately, the discipline her daughter Hildra applies publicly. Hildra learned **everything she knows about Cadwë judiciary work** from her mother's private tutoring.
- **The "Senex" of the name is the line, not the given name.** Cadwës use "Senex" as personal name because the dwarven personal name is **never given to outsiders**. Even Torguir IV, married to her daughter, calls his mother-in-law Senex.

## The Marriage

> [!secret] How the Usurers bought it
> Two generations ago — when Torguir IV's grandfather was Peer — the [[Guild of Usurers]] approached the Tir-Nâ-Bor royal council with a **dwarven-honor-coded proposition**: a marriage between a Senex daughter and a Var-Nokkt heir, with a dowry that would *publicly* honor the dwarven royal house *and* privately satisfy a Cadwë debt nobody outside the Usurer inner circle understood. The Tir-Nâ-Bor council accepted; Senex was sent; she married Torguir IV's father. **The Usurers paid the dowry on the Var-Nokkt side**, masked as a Goldsmith loan, **redeemable only by Usurer instruction.**
>
> What this means in practice: **the Usurers hold a paper that, if surfaced publicly, would prove (a) Senex's marriage was bought, (b) the dwarven royal line was misled about who paid, and (c) the Var-Nokkts have been complying with Usurer instruction for two generations to keep that paper buried.** The dowry document exists. **Senex knows it exists.** She has known since approximately the second year of her marriage, when she discovered the discrepancy between what her family was told and what was actually paid.

## What She Has Done About It

For decades: **nothing**. To expose the deal would shatter:
- The Tir-Nâ-Bor royal line's standing (they were misled and accepted a marriage they would not have accepted)
- The Var-Nokkt name (revealed as compromised assets of the Usurers for two generations)
- Her own daughter's marriage (Hildra is the product of the arrangement)
- The Cadwë–Tir-Nâ-Bor trade relationship in its entirety

Senex is **conservative by dwarven training** — she does not start avalanches. She has spent decades **building a parallel record**: the original dowry-document, the names of the Usurer brokers, her own annotated chronology of the family's compliances. The parallel record is in a **vault she controls personally** inside Castel Var-Nokkt — a vault Hildra has not yet been shown.

## Who Now Knows

- **Senex herself** — always
- **[[Arkan Var-Nokkt]]** — figured it out as a teenager in the household; left the city without confronting her
- **Hildra Var-Nokkt** — *told by Senex within the past year.* The single largest political secret currently sitting in the upper city.
- **[[Torguir IV Var-Nokkt]]** — not told, by mother-in-law's choice
- **[[Master Ôdril Haarken]]** — figured it out from the bank traces; asked the question publicly; vanished
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — yes, of course; they hold the document
- **[[Urakh Khaurik Argam]]** — figured it out before his assassination; coalition leader, deceased

## What She Wants Now

The Rag'narok is coming. The Usurer-Var-Nokkt arrangement was a peacetime tool, built for a world that is ending. **Senex is preparing the parallel record for use** — not by herself, but by **Hildra**, when the moment arrives. She believes Hildra is judiciary enough to deploy it correctly. Hildra believes the moment is **not yet**.

## Relations

- **Hildra Var-Nokkt** — daughter; political confidante since the disclosure
- **[[Torguir IV Var-Nokkt]]** — son-in-law; un-told
- **[[Arkan Var-Nokkt]]** — son-in-law-once-removed; the **other** person who knows, in exile, with **his own** parallel record. They have not communicated. **They could.**
- **[[Guild of Usurers]]** — the family who paid for her; her standing threat
- **[[Tir-Nâ-Bor]]** — her birth nation; would be devastated by public exposure
- **[[Master Ôdril Haarken]]** — never met; would have wanted to meet

> [!hook] The two records
> Senex's parallel record exists in Castel Var-Nokkt. [[Arkan Var-Nokkt|Arkan's]] parallel record exists in the Aegis mountains. **The two records have never been compared.** A free league that delivers a sealed message between Senex and Arkan — and waits for the response — performs a service that **may end the Usurer hold on Var-Nokkt in one afternoon** when the records are finally cross-referenced. The catch: every party that doesn't want this to happen is watching for exactly this contact.

> [!hook] The grandchildren
> Hildra has children. Senex's grandchildren are **half-Cadwë, quarter-Tir-Nâ-Bor royal, quarter-Var-Nokkt**. By dwarven law, they have **claims of standing** in Tir-Nâ-Bor that no full-Cadwë Var-Nokkt could press. Senex has begun teaching the eldest grandchild — quietly — what those claims are. If the political situation collapses, the grandchild may matter more than any of the adults.
