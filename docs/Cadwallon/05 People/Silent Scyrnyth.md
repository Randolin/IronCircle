> The Duke's Steward. Runs the Ducal Enclave day-to-day so [[Duke Den Azhir|Den Azhir]] can rule the city.

## Canon Facts
- **The Steward** — administers the fief of [[Den Azhir]] in the Duke's name (the Duke can't be both ruling peer and ruling Duke, in practice).
- A former free leaguer; part of Den Azhir's "faithful companions" clan.
- The epithet "Silent" is canonical — its meaning is left for our table to decide.

> [!hook] Why "Silent"?
> Vow? Injury? Reputation? Whatever it is, anyone running the ducal fief day-to-day knows enough secrets to silence a kingdom.
