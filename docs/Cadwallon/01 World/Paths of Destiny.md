# Paths of Destiny

> The ancient peoples who refuse the cosmic war as a false choice.

One of [[The Three Alliances]] — the most ancient peoples of [[Aarklash]], who reject the cosmic war as a false choice and refuse to take sides. Members include the [[Daïkinee]] elves of [[Quithayran]], the goblins of [[No-Dan-Kar]], the dwarves of [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]], the Wolfen of [[Yllia]] and [[Vile-Tis]], and the orcs of Bran-Ô-Kor and the Behemoth Mountains.
