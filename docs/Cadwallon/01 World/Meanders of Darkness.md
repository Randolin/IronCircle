# Meanders of Darkness

> Wielders of dark magic, bent on silencing the Light for good.

One of [[The Three Alliances]] — wielders of dark magic and twisted creation who aim to silence the Ways of Light forever. Members include the living-dead of [[Acheron]], the Alchemists of Dirz, the Drune, the [[Possessed]] dwarves of Mid-Nor, the [[Akkyshan]] elves, and the Empire of [[Syharhalna]]. In Cadwallon their trade runs through the [[Guild of Usurers]] and [[Guild of Thieves]].
