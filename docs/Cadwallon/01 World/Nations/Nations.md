# Nations

The foreign powers of [[Aarklash]] as they touch Cadwallon. This directory covers each nation **only to the depth the Cadwallon PHB establishes** — that is, mostly through (a) the races that live in the Free City, (b) the four official embassies, and (c) the guilds' Rag'narok alliances. Deep national lore belongs to the *Confrontation/Rag'narok* wargame line, not the Cadwallon RPG, so where the PHB goes quiet, these notes say so rather than invent.

> [!note] Canon discipline
> Everything below is sourced from the Cadwallon Player's Handbook (and cross-checked against [[The Three Alliances]] and [[Aarklash]]). Statements the PHB doesn't support are flagged as **[beyond PHB]** or omitted. We'll evaluate cross-links and Confrontation-line detail later.

## The Four Official Embassies

These nations maintain a formal embassy in Cadwallon's [[Upper City]]:

| Nation | Alliance | Culture | Embassy note |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Alahan]] | Ways of Light | Lion | First embassy established (833, "Barhan embassy"); seat at Whitehaven in [[Soma]] |
| [[Akkylannie]] | Ways of Light | Griffin | Templar empire |
| [[Syharhalna]] | Meanders of Darkness | Scorpion | Created the orcs; *(our table: New Shamir — see [[Cadwallon/01 World/Homebrew|World Homebrew]])* |
| [[No-Dan-Kar]] | Paths of Destiny | Rat | Goblins; their **klû** is the continental currency |

## The Unofficial Fifth

- **[[Lanever]]** — the elven Republic whose peninsula Cadwallon occupies. The reclusive **Cynwäll ambassador** [[Dyrsin]] lives in the tower dominating the upper city; there is no formal embassy "in the strict sense," and he is rarely seen.

## Former Embassy

- **[[Tir-Nâ-Bor]]** — the dwarven nation **once had an embassy** in Cadwallon. It no longer does, but trade relations continue and dwarven diplomats are "almost always present," housed by the [[Var-Nokkt]] family or in the best hotels.

## Other Nations of Note

These have no embassy but matter to Cadwallon's story:

- **[[Acheron]]** — Meanders of Darkness; the living-dead empire that besieged the [[Dogs of War]] at the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]]; [[Sophet Drahas]]'s origin
- **[[Quithayran]]** — the Eternal Forest; cradle of the elven race and home of the [[Daïkinee]]; not a "nation" in the political sense but the elven origin-point

## Nations by Alliance

See [[The Three Alliances]] for the full membership roster and totem-cultures. In brief:

- **Ways of Light** — [[Alahan]] (Lion), [[Akkylannie]] (Griffin), Avaggdu (Minotaur), [[Lanever]] (Dragon)
- **Paths of Destiny** — [[Quithayran|Daïkinee]] (Beetle), [[No-Dan-Kar]] (Rat), [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] (Wild Boar), Wolfen of Yllia (Wolf) & Vile-Tis (Hyena), orcs of Bran-Ô-Kor & the Behemoth Mountains (Jackal)
- **Meanders of Darkness** — [[Acheron]] (Stag), Dirz (Scorpion), Drune (Stag), Mid-Nor (Hydra), Akkyshan elves (Spider), [[Syharhalna]] (Scorpion)

> [!note] Nations the PHB barely names
> The PHB references several powers only in passing — **Avaggdu** (Sessair Kelts, Minotaur), **Dirz** (alchemists, Scorpion), **Drune** (Stag), **Mid-Nor** (Possessed dwarves, Hydra), **Bran-Ô-Kor** and the **Behemoth Mountains** (orc homelands), **Kel** (the lost human motherland), **Yllia** / **Vile-Tis** (Wolfen). These don't have their own notes yet and may not need them at table depth; develop on demand from Confrontation-line sources if a campaign reaches them.
