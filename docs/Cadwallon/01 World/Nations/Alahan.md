# Alahan

> The Lion kingdom of the Ways of Light — first of the nations to plant a flag in Cadwallon.

A human kingdom of the **Ways of Light** under the **Lion** totem, Alahan holds a distinction no other embassy power can claim: it was **the first nation to open an embassy in Cadwallon**, in 833, the year after the founding — the so-called Barhan embassy. Its thread runs back into the city's origin story. When the [[Battle of the Wall of Earth]] turned, it was the Alliance of Light's troops arriving to back the [[Dogs of War]]; and it was Alahan's emissaries whom [[Vanius the Founder|Vanius]] famously rebuffed with "my kingdom for a ducat!" — taking the Barhan military rank of **Duke** rather than kneel to Alahan as a baron or insult it as a king.

The kingdom's shining capital is **Kallienne**, a city of legend. The word **Barhan** recurs wherever Alahan touches the Free City — the Barhan embassy, Barhan carpenters, the Barhan rank Vanius adopted — a people-or-place term the Cadwë use freely without ever quite pinning down.

## In Cadwallon

- **Whitehaven** — the Alahan embassy sits in [[Heaven Heights]] in the [[Soma]] fief, in a palace given as a gift. The ambassador, **[[Brehnan of Alahan]]**, has held the post for over thirty years.
- One of the four embassy tongues whose cadence shapes the structured **upper-city dialect** of [[Daily Life|Cadwë]].
- **Guild allies** — the Lions of Alahan ride in the Rag'narok alongside the [[Guild of Architects|Architects]], [[Guild of Goldsmiths|Goldsmiths]], [[Guild of Thieves|Thieves]], and [[Guild of Cartomancers|Cartomancers]].

## Relations

- [[Akkylannie]] — the other human empire of Light; together the two anchor the Light bloc inside the city.
- [[Soma]] & [[Brehnan of Alahan]] — the embassy's host fief and its long-serving ambassador.
- [[Guild of Blades]] / [[Isabeau Soma]] — Isabeau works diplomatic security with Brehnan at Whitehaven.
- [[Vanius the Founder]] — rebuffed Alahan's emissaries at the founding, and took their Duke's title doing it.
