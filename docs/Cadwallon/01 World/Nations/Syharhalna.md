# Syharhalna

> The Scorpion empire of the Meanders of Darkness — the nation that grew the orcs in a tank.

A human empire of the **Meanders of Darkness** under the **Scorpion** totem — "the Syhars." Syharhalna is one of the four official embassy powers and the most scientifically feared of them: its alchemists and technomancers lead the continent in mutagenics, far beyond anything Cadwallon's own omnimancers have managed. Its presence in the Free City is the uneasy, necessary tolerance of a Darkness power everyone does business with and no one trusts.

Its darkest achievement is also its most consequential for Cadwallon. **The orcs of all Aarklash were created in Syharhalna's cloning tanks** — in **808**, in the capital of **Shamir**, by crossing goblin and human stock. The Khorda clones (the orcs' first name) came out cleverer and stronger than their makers intended, and in **852** they rebelled, butchered their way to freedom, and fled to Bran-Ô-Kor and the Behemoth Mountains. Orc blood still carries the stigmata of those manipulations. **Syhar** is also one of the three tongues that most shaped the Cadwë language.

> [!note] Our table: New Shamir
> This campaign reflavors Syharhalna as **New Shamir** — see [[Cadwallon/01 World/Homebrew|World Homebrew]] for the override. Everything below is the PHB baseline the reflavor sits on.

## In Cadwallon

- **Embassy** in [[The Embassies]] district of [[Den Azhir]] — one of the four official seats, and the city's standing proof that a Darkness power and a Light power can negotiate at all.
- **Guild dealings** — as a Meanders of Darkness nation, Syharhalna falls under the [[Guild of Usurers|Usurers']] umbrella alliance with all the peoples of the Darkness; Syhar alchemists also deal quietly with the [[Guild of Thieves|Thieves]], who broker black-market mutagenic supplies and host discriminated Darkness-aligned visitors.
- **The orcs** — Cadwallon's orc community, the second-largest on Aarklash, are the distant descendants of Syharhalna's creation, now a Cadwë people in their own right (Jackal culture, Paths of Destiny — they rejected their makers' alignment).

## Relations

- **The orcs** — the abandoned creation; their existence, rebellion, and spread all trace back to Shamir. See [[Cultures]] and [[Kal Rajghur]], the orc Constable.
- [[Akkylannie]] & [[Alahan]] — the Light empires Syharhalna opposes; meetable only in Cadwallon.
- [[Guild of Usurers]] — guild ally under the Darkness umbrella.
- [[Guild of Thieves]] — black-market trade in Syhar mutagens.
- **Dirz** — the *other* Scorpion-culture alchemist power of the Darkness; the PHB lists the two separately, both bioscience-obsessed, so keep them distinct at the table.
