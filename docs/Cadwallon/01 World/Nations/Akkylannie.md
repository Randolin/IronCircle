# Akkylannie

> The Griffin empire of the Ways of Light — templars, language, and a fort on the city's doorstep.

A human empire of the **Ways of Light** under the **Griffin** totem, Akkylannie is one of the four official embassy powers and arguably the most culturally embedded of them. **Cadwë grammar itself is built on Akkylannian** (one of the three tongues, with Barhan and Syhar, that most shaped the language — and, the Cadwë will tell you, the easiest to learn). The city's great civic holiday is borrowed from Akkylannie too: the **ducal jubilee** descends from the Akkylannian tradition of marking the Empire's founding every fifty years, which Cadwallon adapted into its annual celebration of the Duke's accession. The Empire was founded by **Arcavius de Sabran**.

Griffin is a broad culture — one of the Cartomancers' constellations, a human totem, and even the culture dwarves take on when raised in Akkylannie — which is fitting for a multiracial empire that absorbs other peoples into its Light.

## In Cadwallon

- **Embassy** at [[The Span]] in [[Ogrokh]] — one of the four official seats.
- **[[Fort Griffin]]** — an Akkylannian fort on a small island guarding [[Shipwreck Bay]], the eastern sea approach. The Light's navies favor those waters precisely because the fort defends them; anyone using the route needs the garrison's leave.
- **Templars** — Akkylannie is the templar power of the Light, its faithful practising the disciplined structured-school magic (see [[Mana & Magic]]).
- **Guild allies** — the Griffins of Akkylannie ride with the [[Guild of Architects|Architects]], [[Guild of Goldsmiths|Goldsmiths]], and [[Guild of Cartomancers|Cartomancers]].

## Relations

- [[Alahan]] — the other human empire of Light; together they anchor the Light bloc in the city.
- [[Syharhalna]] — the opposite pole. The PHB's favourite illustration of why Cadwallon matters: a Syhar envoy and an Akkylannian could never safely meet at home — only in the Free City.
- [[Fort Griffin]] & [[Shipwreck Bay]] — the Empire's military presence on Cadwallon's threshold.
- [[Guild of Cartomancers]] / [[Guild of Goldsmiths]] / [[Guild of Architects]] — guild allies.
