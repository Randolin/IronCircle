# No-Dan-Kar

> The goblin nation of the Paths of Destiny — the source of the coin in everyone's purse.

The goblin nation of the **Paths of Destiny** under the **Rat** totem, No-Dan-Kar is one of the four official embassy powers and, in its quiet way, the most important of them — because its currency, the **klû**, has become the standard of trade across all Aarklash. Every refugee who washes up in Cadwallon "klû-less," every merchant settling a continental account, is moving No-Dan-Kar's money. (Cadwallon's own coin is the local **ducat** — "my kingdom for a ducat" — but cross-border, the klû rules.)

The goblins are the continent's **unequalled inventors and the great promoters of the written word**, and with the dwarves of [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] they drove the technological revolution Cadwallon enjoys. Their reach is woven into the very language and calendar: goblin navigators named the four navigation constellations — Nerea, Sylhea, Elion, Olhim — long ago. Where the goblins themselves come from is genuinely uncertain; most say the swamps of No-Dan-Kar, some the Aegis Mountains, some the dawn of the world. Their clergy blame the **mutants and brats** that began appearing around 820 on the wrath of the **Rat god**, the god of the multitudes.

## In Cadwallon

- **Embassy** in [[The Embassies]] district of [[Den Azhir]] — one of the four official seats.
- **One of the oldest communities in the city** — goblin exiles settled Kraken Harbor in **255**, the earliest date on Cadwë record, centuries before the founding. Goblins shape the city's very geography, which must account for their small size and high birth rate.
- **[[Little Klûne]]** — the goblin district in [[Trophy]] (its name echoing the klû), where goblin merchants and wholesalers dominate the produce trade.
- **Guild allies** — the goblins ride with the [[Guild of Goldsmiths|Goldsmiths]], [[Guild of Thieves|Thieves]], and [[Guild of Cartomancers|Cartomancers]], fitting for a trade-and-information power. One of the four embassy tongues, too.

## Relations

- [[Tir-Nâ-Bor]] — fellow Paths of Destiny; together the goblins and dwarves are the engine of Cadwallon's economic and technological advances.
- [[Syharhalna]] — a grim historical link: goblin stock (with human) was the raw material from which the Syhars made the orcs. No-Dan-Kar bears no alignment with them.
- The **orcs** — distant kin by creation, now their own people.
- [[Guild of Goldsmiths]] / [[Guild of Cartomancers]] / [[Guild of Thieves]] — guild allies.
