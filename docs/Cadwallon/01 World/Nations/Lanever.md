# Lanever

> The young elven Republic of the Ways of Light, on whose peninsula Cadwallon stands. The Jewel of Lanever.

The elven Republic of the **Ways of Light** under the **Dragon** totem — the youngest of the Light nations and the landlord of the Free City. Cadwallon sits on Lanever's peninsula, and the city's grandest epithet, **"the Jewel of Lanever,"** names that relationship. Lanever is the political home of the **[[Cynwäll]]** elves — the Light branch of the elven race, taller and stronger than their kin, who left the cradle of [[Quithayran]] under [[Elhan]] and whose Republic now formally represents them. They are the people whose abandoned city Cadwallon is built atop.

The Dragon culture is no metaphor here: **Lanever dragons** glide across the city's sky to visit their titanic kin roosting in the [[Dyrsin]] Tower, and the Cynwäll alone hold the privilege of riding them — a privilege once extended to [[Johann of Drakaër|Johann]], granted a dragon in 843.

Lanever keeps **no embassy in the strict sense**. Instead the Cynwäll ambassador lives in the tower that dominates the upper city — a secretive figure whom very few have ever met, who never appears at any festivity unless he has something of weight to announce.

## In Cadwallon

- **[[Dyrsin]]'s Tower** — the colossal Cynwäll tower over the [[Upper City]], older than Cadwallon itself, home to the ambassador and the titanic dragons; centerpiece of [[The Pillars]] in [[Drakaër]].
- **[[The Watching Tower]]** — the Cynwäll outpost on the outskirts, guarding the road to Lanever.
- **The [[Cynwäll Ruins]]** — Lanever's elves once *were* Cadwallon. They hold the buried site theirs and cursed, watch it constantly through the shrews of the [[Circle of Explorers]], and quietly confiscate any dangerous vestige that surfaces.
- **Guild ally** — the Cynwäll ride with the [[Guild of Architects|Architects]] (an alliance forged rebuilding after the flood of 900) and the [[Guild of Cartomancers|Cartomancers]]; their language lends the upper city its most impressive turns of phrase.

## Relations

- [[Cynwäll]] & [[Dyrsin]] — the people and their ambassador; Lanever's living presence in the city.
- [[Guild of Architects]] — the deep technical alliance dating to [[The Great Flood|the flood of 900]].
- [[Quithayran]] — the elven cradle; the Cynwäll are the "exiles" (the word means *exile*) who left it.
- [[Akkylannie]] & [[Alahan]] — fellow Ways of Light, though Lanever is markedly more isolationist.
- [[Aarklash]] — "the Jewel of Lanever" frames Cadwallon's whole identity.

> [!note] Canon & scope
> Drawn from the Cadwallon PHB. The Republic's internal government, its cities, and Cynwäll society *at home* belong to the *Confrontation / Rag'narok* line and aren't table canon until we adopt them. The PHB shows Lanever almost entirely through its Cadwallon-facing side: the peninsula, the Tower, the dragons, the secretive ambassador, the buried city.
