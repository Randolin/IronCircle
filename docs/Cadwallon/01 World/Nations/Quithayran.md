# Quithayran

> The Eternal Forest — the cradle of the whole elven race, and the home of the Fayes, the spirits of nature.

Quithayran is **not a nation in the political sense** — no government, no army, no embassy — but the **origin-place of every elf** and the living heart of the **[[Daïkinee]]** (Paths of Destiny, **Beetle** totem). It is less a country than a metaphysical taproot. The elves' very existence is bound to the forest: **the closer an elf lives to its heart, the longer they live and the better their body shrugs off harm.** Far from the cradle, an elf becomes one of the *Maalivatë* — "those who have left" — their long lives (60 to 120 years) shortening with the distance. The presence of the **Fayes**, the nature-spirits dwelling in Quithayran, seems to sustain them even in exile.

The **Daïkinee** are the forest's closest people, the original stem of the elven race, still living a sylvan life in symbiosis with beast and plant. From this one cradle the elven race split into three that "are no more one people": the [[Cynwäll]] (Dragon, Light) who exiled themselves to found what became Cadwallon, the [[Akkyshan]] (Spider, Darkness), and the Daïkinee (Beetle, Destiny) who stayed. That schism left a wound: Daïkinee women suffer a curse laid by **Scaëlin, matriarch of the [[Akkyshan]]s** — they die soon after bearing the fruit of their first union. And it is the Daïkinee, keepers of the oldest true memory on Aarklash, who still tell of the ancient [[Ophidians|Serpent people]] that once held the Cadwallon site and allied with the most evil of dragons.

## In Cadwallon

The forest is distant, but its people and its spirits reach the city:

- **[[Daïkinee]] residents** — sylvan elves far from their cradle, living as Maalivatë; rare, and bound to nature. **[[Yalin Aoue]]** is the campaign's young Daïkinee, her forest-animae possibly a fragment of [[Desire]] and the [[Garden of Desire]].
- **The [[Fayrees|Fayes]]** — nature-spirits of Quithayran's kind, drawn to Cadwallon's [[Magical Groves]], which (unknown to most) are shoots of [[Desire]]'s prison-garden.
- **Daïkinee lore** is the city's best source on the [[Ophidians]] and the pre-Cynwäll past, and the longevity-by-proximity rule shapes the backstory of any long-lived elf.

## Relations

- [[Daïkinee]] — the people of the forest; the original elven stem.
- [[Cynwäll]] & [[Lanever]] — the Light branch that exiled itself from Quithayran to settle this peninsula.
- [[Akkyshan]] — the Dark branch; Scaëlin's curse on the Daïkinee binds the two peoples in tragedy.
- [[Yalin Aoue]] — the campaign's Daïkinee child, far from the cradle.
- [[Magical Groves]] & [[Desire]] — the Fayes-haunted vegetation echoes Quithayran's nature-spirits.

> [!note] Canon & scope
> Drawn from the Cadwallon PHB. Quithayran's geography, the Fayes as beings, and the deep history of the elven schism belong to the *Confrontation / Rag'narok* line and aren't table canon until we adopt them. Note the PHB frames Quithayran as a **cradle and spiritual source, not a polity** — treat it as the elven origin-myth made geographic.
