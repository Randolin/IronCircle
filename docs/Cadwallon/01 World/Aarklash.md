# Aarklash

> A war-torn continent at the heart of Creation — the board on which exiled gods move their mortal pieces.

A continent on a world torn by perpetual war. Once the gods walked here directly. They overreached, tore each other apart, almost destroyed Creation itself, and were exiled by Creation's defense — banished beyond the elemental Realms. **Time** was set as their prison: if they appear directly, they suffer punishment and oblivion.

So the gods stopped appearing directly. They have waged war for thousands of years through champions — [[Incarnates]] — and through their most faithful mortal allies. Aarklash's nations have aligned into [[The Three Alliances]] around this proxy conflict. Now the prophesied apocalyptic war, [[The Rag'narok|the Rag'narok]], looms.

> [!note] The cosmic stakes
> Aarklash sits near the heart of Creation. **Magic thresholds** can be opened on Aarklash to travel to any Realm, including the gods' prisons. The fate of Aarklash decides the fate of Creation itself. **The curse of Time is fading.** The gods are preparing to walk again.

## A History Written by Victors

The history of Aarklash was written by whoever won the last war. Glorious conquerors hide fratricide and dreadful lies; nations were built on pain, blood, and treachery; older nations fell to Time's erosion and were replaced by younger empires hungrier for power. Revenge and domination are the inheritance every sovereign carries.

## The Three Alliances

The continent's nations have aligned into three philosophical/political blocs. The split itself was a **human innovation**; the most ancient peoples refused to choose. See [[The Three Alliances]].

- **Paths of Destiny** — the ancient peoples, refusing to take sides
- **Ways of Light** — younger nations seeking an age of reason and prosperity
- **Meanders of Darkness** — wielders of dark magic, hungry for power

## [[Cadwallon Overview|Cadwallon]]

Cadwallon — the Free City — is officially neutral, sitting on a peninsula at the heart of the continent on the territory of [[Lanever]]. Built atop the ruins of an older Cynwäll trading post (Cynwäll means "exile" in the elven tongue), itself built atop something far older. Its independence is guaranteed by the [[Free Leagues]], the precarious balance of the Alliances, the city's commercial value to every faction... and by something hidden beneath the streets.

> [!secret] What Aarklash doesn't know
> Cadwallon is the keeper of a secret which could shatter the beliefs of every faction in the Rag'narok. See [[Desire]], [[The 22 Arcana]], [[Underground]].
