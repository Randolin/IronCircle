# Ways of Light

> The human-led bloc building toward an age of reason — and courting the prosperous to its cause.

One of [[The Three Alliances]] — the predominantly human bloc working to build "an age of reason and prosperity," and to seduce prosperous civilizations (especially dwarves) to their cause. Members include the Kingdom of [[Alahan]], the Empire of [[Akkylannie]], the Tribes of Avaggdu, and the elven Republic of [[Lanever]] (with the ancestral [[Cynwäll]]).
