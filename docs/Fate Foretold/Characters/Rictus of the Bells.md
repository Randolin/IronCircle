## Personality & Mannerisms

- Performs merry constantly. The performance is real — he is genuinely amused by most things — but it is also a costume worn over something that watches very carefully.
- Orbits people rather than approaching them directly. He ends up nearby without you noticing him arrive.
- The brass bells sewn into his sleeves are the tell. They jingle constantly when he works the room. When he goes still — when something is actually happening — they are completely silent. He doesn't silence them deliberately. They simply stop.
- Makes eye contact slightly too long before looking away. Just long enough that you notice, and then he's already moved on and you wonder if you imagined it.
